# `GRWTSK.AlgebraicGeometry.HaradaTwistedEulerProduct.primeFactor_eq_one_of_character_zero`

Local attribution: grwtsk contributors. MSC2020: 11M41.

For every complex s and prime p, if the actual character value chi(p)=0,
the entire Harada primeFactor is one. Substitution makes its local variable
zero, so both inverse factors are one for either residual multiplicity.
The shifted contribution is removed too; retaining it at a ramified prime
would produce the wrong Euler product.

Consumer: source-faithful treatment of zero character values, including
imprimitive characters. This is an elementary definitional consequence;
primitivity, continuation, local-system realization and positivity are
not established by this declaration.

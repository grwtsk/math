Assume every weight on S is positive. Then the product vanishes at z exactly when z belongs to S.

Proof and consumer: A zero finite product has a zero factor; a nonzero base has nonzero powers, forcing z=a for a listed location. Conversely the factor at z is zero to a positive power. The positive-weight assumption prevents artificial zero-weight support entries. Consumer: exact source support identification.

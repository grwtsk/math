For every finite index type, real family lambda and complex z, membership in the image of lambda is equivalent to existence of an index whose cast equals z. Unfold the finite image and discharge membership in the universal index set. This is the standard finite-image membership theorem, not arithmetic zero identification.

Consumer: occurrence positivity and spectralDivisor support. Repeated indices may have the same image; membership makes no multiplicity assertion. The later order theorem supplies that separate fact.

Fix a model, mode, claim and observable result. Require every witness to retain canonical validity and produce that result. The existing `ExecutionModel` costs and deductive content remain the same. Apply the inherited Dickson finiteness and characterization to this restricted witness model. Its attainable region is exactly the outcome-indexed region by rearranging witnesses and conjunctions. The proof returns to the original resource budgets without changing the units of any coordinate.

The strict-inclusion criterion has two conditions. Every incumbent Pareto minimum dominates some replacement Pareto minimum. Some replacement Pareto minimum has no incumbent Pareto minimum below it. For necessity, pick any newly attainable budget and choose a replacement minimum below it. An incumbent minimum below the replacement minimum would make the initial budget incumbent-attainable. Conversely, the replacement minimum itself witnesses the strict gap. This handles empty regions and requires complete actual coverage rather than sampled coverage. Separate-agent hostile source review confirmed the argument. Independent external review remains open.

An exact result can improve with an unchanged aggregate region. Take two results with costs 0 and 2 in every coordinate. Replacing the second cost by 1 leaves the aggregate region generated by the zero vector and strictly enlarges the second result's attainable region. The theorem provides no effectiveness claim for extracting either full frontier and no measured application cost or semantic equivalence without the implementation witness model.

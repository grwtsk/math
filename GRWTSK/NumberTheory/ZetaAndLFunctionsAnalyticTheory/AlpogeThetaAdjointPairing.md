## The positive scalar and volumes in every degree

Conversion of the remaining Petersson product to the multiplicity inner product costs exactly one factor \(N^{-1}\). Using \([K_V:J]\|f_{\rm test}\|^2=1/d_S\) gives
\[
 \langle T^*u,T^*u'\rangle_X=h_P l_e\langle u,u'\rangle_Y,\qquad
 l_e=\frac{\mathrm{mass}(V)L^S}{d^S d_S}
       \prod_\rho\frac{\dim C_\rho\,\dim L_\rho}{d_{\pi_\rho}}
                          H_{\alpha_\rho}H_{\beta_\rho}.
                                                                    \tag{4}
\]

There are two distinct normalizations used by the volume calculation. The least-norm preimage of \(u\in E_e\) under \(T\) is \((h_P l_e)^{-1}T^*u\), with norm \(\|u\|/\sqrt{h_P l_e}\), since every other preimage differs by an element of \(\ker T\) orthogonal to \(\operatorname{im}T^*\). The map on periods \(B=h_P^{-1}T^*\) satisfies \(\langle Bu,Bv\rangle=(l_e/h_P)\langle u,v\rangle\) on \(E_e\). A Hermitian Gram determinant of \(k\) vectors is multiplied by \((l_e/h_P)^k\), including dependent tuples. For a real lattice of rank \(s\) inside \(E_e\), take the Gram matrix of a real basis using \(\operatorname{Re}\langle\ ,\ \rangle\). Its intrinsic real covolume is multiplied by \((l_e/h_P)^{s/2}\).

The eigenvalues of the self-adjoint \(TT^*\) on the sum of all nonzero Hecke types are bounded below by \(h_P e^{M_0}\), where \(M_0=\mathrm{mainTerm}-8fn^2\mathrm{Lp}\). Group types with the same scalar and use orthogonality of the eigenspaces with distinct scalars. Thus \(B^*B\ge e^{M_0}/h_P\) on that sum. For any real rank-\(s\) lattice \(\Lambda\) in this sum an orthonormal basis of its real span gives a compressed real Gram matrix with every eigenvalue at least \(e^{M_0}/h_P\). Taking its determinant yields
\[
 \log\frac{\operatorname{covol}(B\Lambda)}
               {\operatorname{covol}(\Lambda)}
       \ \ge\ \frac{s}{2}\bigl(M_0-\log h_P\bigr).
\]

For the algebraic lift the map in the archive goes from the definite forms to the cuspidal realization. Suppose one has established a number-field map \(A\) on this orientation, its rational period comparison at every embedding, and an integral image of a projective source lattice inside a target lattice \(Q\) spanning the chosen rational Hecke subspace. Take the full preimage of that subspace and quotient the source lattice by its saturated kernel, obtaining \(P\) with the quotient metrics. At each infinite place the map on the orthogonal complement of the kernel has squared singular values equal to the eigenvalues of \(TT^*\) on the target subspace. The unitary comparison maps, central shifts and unit-modulus constants in \(\mathrm{ConjAt}\) do not change these values. Thus one may combine this calculation with the [projective lattice index formula](AlpogeDeterminantLattices.md) without introducing adjoints of integral maps. Precisely, for rank \(k\) and invariant target subspaces in the span just considered,
\[
 \sum_{\mathfrak p}\operatorname{length}_{\mathfrak p}(Q/AP)\log N\mathfrak p
 =\log\frac{\operatorname{covol}(P)}{\operatorname{covol}(Q)}
   +\sum_{v\mid\infty}\log\operatorname{Jac}_{\mathbb R}(A_v)
 \ \ge\ \log\frac{\operatorname{covol}(P)}{\operatorname{covol}(Q)}
       +\frac{k}{2}\sum_{v\mid\infty}[K_v:\mathbb R](M_{0,v}+\log h_{P,v}).
\]
Indeed the finite index is the ratio of the covolumes of \(AP\) and \(Q\) in their common real space. Transport of the quotient metric multiplies the covolume by the product of the real Jacobians. For a complex place the real determinant counts each complex singular value twice.

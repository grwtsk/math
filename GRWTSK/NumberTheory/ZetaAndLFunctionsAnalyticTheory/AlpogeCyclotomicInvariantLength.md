# Exact cyclotomic invariant length in the unramified column range

grwtsk contributors

For an odd prime \(\ell\), a positive integer \(n\), and an unramified finite extension \(E_w/\mathbb Q_\ell\), let \(O\) be the source coefficient DVR, \(K=\operatorname{Frac}O\), and \(\epsilon\) the \(\ell\)-adic cyclotomic character. The normalized length of the fixed subgroup of \((K/O)(\epsilon^{-n})\) under the decomposition group at \(w\) is exactly
\[
 b_\ell(n)=
 \begin{cases}
 0 & \ell-1\nmid n,\\
 1+v_\ell(n)& \ell-1\mid n.
 \end{cases}
\]
The coefficient ring may be ramified. This computes one named summand of Levent Alpöge's split Selmer upper bound at archive revision **40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005**. The source defines the action by the **negative** Tate twist and divides the ordinary \(O\)-module length by \(\operatorname{length}_O(O/\ell O)\). ## Local image at every original column place

Fix any source data \(D:\mathrm{D7}\), local index \(i\), \(\ell=D.\mathrm{nd}.l(i)\), and place \(w\mid\ell\) of the host \(E\). Suppose \(\ell\notin D.\mathrm{host}.\mathrm{small}\) and \(\ell\ne q,d\). The host conductor is \(N=Q_S qd\), with its prime divisors among the small primes and \(q,d\). The host embeds in the field generated by the \(N\)-th roots of unity. Every root of unity of order prime to \(\ell\) in a local algebraic closure belongs to a finite unramified extension. Indeed a finite residue field contains all the required roots, and the derivative of \(X^N-1\) is a unit at each root, giving distinct Hensel lifts. Thus every \(E_w\) here is unramified over \(\mathbb Q_\ell\). In the archived argument one can set \(s=0\) in the actual host image lemma, since \(v_\ell(N)=0\) and adjoining the first roots of unity adds only 1. These hypotheses hold throughout the quantified **RangeAD**; there the additional exclusion of 3 gives \(\ell\ge5\). For every \(j\ge1\), the polynomial \(\Phi_{\ell^j}(1+T)\) is monic with constant coefficient \(\ell\), and reduces modulo \(\ell\) to \(T^{(\ell-1)\ell^{j-1}}\). This follows from the geometric sum \(\sum_{a=0}^{\ell-1}(1+T)^{a\ell^{j-1}}\). It is therefore Eisenstein over every finite unramified extension of \(\mathbb Q_\ell\). Its root generates a totally ramified Galois extension of the indicated degree. The Galois action on a primitive root embeds in \((\mathbb Z/\ell^j)^\times\), whose order is exactly that degree. Inertia therefore surjects onto this unit group at every \(j\). Equivalently, the subgroups of global absolute inertia at the chosen extension of \(w\) realizing a fixed compatible sequence of unit residues form nested nonempty closed subsets of a compact group. Their intersection realizes the sequence. In the source's global decomposition-group model the rational inertia elements fix the image of \(E\), by the preceding prime-to-conductor argument; they therefore give elements of host inertia with exactly the same character. This works for each chosen \(w\) and requires no choice of a distinguished place or Frobenius sign. ## Annihilator and maximum over the original twists

Let \(I\subset O\) be the ideal of scalar differences \(\epsilon(g)^{-n}-1\) for the decomposition group. Inverting a unit multiplies its difference from 1 by a unit, up to sign. The depth of this ideal in \(\mathbb Z_\ell\) is the largest \(j\ge0\) at which every unit modulo \(\ell^j\) has \(n\)-th power 1 (depth zero is automatic). For \(j\ge1\) the group is cyclic because \(\ell\) is odd. Its exponent is \((\ell-1)\ell^{j-1}\). This proves the formula for \(b=b_\ell(n)\) above. Every difference is an \(O\)-multiple of \(\ell^b\), and a lift of a failing unit modulo \(\ell^{b+1}\) gives a difference equal to \(\ell^b\) times a \(\mathbb Z_\ell\)-unit. The image of the latter in \(O\) is still a unit. Hence \(I=\ell^b O\), including the case \(I=O\). [Odd prime-power group exponent in pinned Mathlib](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/d13f23b723b8a846827a245b89c10fc7d3f11612/Mathlib/NumberTheory/ArithmeticFunction/Carmichael.lean#L160-L172)

A fraction class \(x+O\) is fixed precisely when every difference times \(x\) belongs to \(O\), equivalently \(\ell^b x\in O\). Division by \(\ell^b\) identifies the fixed subgroup with \(O/\ell^bO\). Its ordinary length is \(be\) where \(e=v_O(\ell)>0\), so its normalized length is \(b\). This also gives the lower bound on the fixed length, which cannot be obtained by testing only one group element for annihilation. The archived proof uses the element with character \(1+\ell\) at good primes and obtains the uniform upper bound \(1+v_\ell(n)\); another archived bound allows ramified primes at the higher conductor depth \(s_0\). Write \(t=D.R.t\) and let \(C\) be any finite set of places over \(\ell\). The source's \(h_0(i,C)\) takes a maximum over \(C\times\{n:0<n<t\}\), with empty maximum zero. For \(C\ne\varnothing\), a positive layer \(j\) occurs if and only if \((\ell-1)\ell^{j-1}<t\), since the smallest eligible exponent is \((\ell-1)\ell^{j-1}\) itself. Consequently,
\[
 h_0(i,C)=
 \begin{cases}
 0 & C=\varnothing\ \text{or}\ t<\ell,\\
 1+\lfloor\log_\ell\lfloor(t-1)/(\ell-1)\rfloor\rfloor & C\ne\varnothing,\ t\ge\ell .
 \end{cases}
\]
In particular \(t=\ell\) gives exactly one for nonempty columns. The formula supplies the actual **H0CyclotomicLengthBound** at every twist with its stated restrictions, and yields zero throughout the range \(\ell>t\). As a separate uniform estimate one can use constant 1 in that clause: a surviving depth \(j\ge1\) obeys \(j\le (\ell-1)\ell^{j-1}<t\). ## Weighted return to the split bound

The contribution of these invariants to **boundSplit** is precisely \(t\,|C|\,h_0(i,C)\). Fix one index \(i(\ell)\) and column \(C_\ell\) above each prime in a finite set \(S\) of **RangeAD** primes for the same \(D\). Writing \(f=D.R.f\), the sum of local degrees bounds the column cardinality by \(2f\). For \(t\ge1\) the following bound therefore applies to the contribution with its actual multiplicity:
\[
 \sum_{\ell\in S} t\,|C_\ell|\,h_0(i(\ell),C_\ell)\log\ell
 \ \le\ 2ft\,\psi\!\left(\left\lfloor\tfrac54(t-1)\right\rfloor\right).
\]
Indeed, expand each \(h_0\) as the number of surviving layers. For \(\ell\ge5\), each such pair \((\ell,j)\) has \(\ell^j=\frac{\ell}{\ell-1}\varphi(\ell^j)\le\frac54(t-1)\). Distinct pairs give distinct prime powers, each contributing \(\Lambda(\ell^j)=\log\ell\) to the ordinary Chebyshev sum. If several local indices occur at one rational prime their multiplicities must be retained. The other terms of **boundSplit**, including the representation-dependent \(h_0^W\), \(c_B\), the actual Shafarevich lengths and the congruence ideals, have their own hypotheses. [Exact coefficient and adjacent terms](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/SharpRestriction/LevelLetterCocycles/EmbeddingTypes.lean#L293-L309) · [Column cardinality from local degrees](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/ResidueCharacters/NumSplit.lean#L85-L103)

Using the elementary Chebyshev bound \(\psi(x)\le(\log 4+4)x\) for all \(x\ge0\) gives an explicit uniform upper bound \(\tfrac52(\log 4+4)ft(t-1)\) on this weighted sum. This estimate uses no zero-free region. ## Formal scope and checks

The companion native Lean module proves seven declarations in the namespace `GRWTSK.NumberTheory.AlpogeCyclotomicInvariantLength`. `fixed_fraction_iff` proves the congruence criterion on representatives for a field with a subring and a generating scalar difference. In the application all character values are integral units, so the condition descends to fraction classes. `units_fix_iff` and `units_fix_iff_valuation` establish the exact finite layer and its tame and valuation conditions. `padic_units_fix_iff` proves the divisibility criterion for all actual units of the p-adic integers by lifting each finite unit and using the exact kernel of reduction. `layer_in_window_iff` and `layer_in_window_iff_log` optimize the original open twist window. `layer_prime_power_le` gives the prime-power inclusion in the last sum. Native elaboration, fresh kernel replay and transitive axiom audit apply to these seven declarations. The host inertia identification, normalized DVR length and weighted summation are proved in the arguments above; the local module does not import the source's D7 tower. The primary archive statements are supplied for comparison. [Companion Lean source](AlpogeCyclotomicInvariantLength.lean)

Two failures describe the hypotheses sharply. Modulo 8 all units square to one, although \(\varphi(8)=4\) does not divide 2. Over \(\mathbb Q_5(\zeta_5)\), the cyclotomic image is congruent to 1 modulo 5, so the first twist already has nonzero invariants. Neither variant lies in the good odd source range. This calculation settles the scalar cyclotomic term only. The complete arithmetic inequality, spectral correspondence, and Riemann Hypothesis remain open in this work.

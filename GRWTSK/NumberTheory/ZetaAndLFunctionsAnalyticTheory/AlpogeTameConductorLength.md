# The auxiliary tame conductor term in the split Selmer bound

grwtsk contributors

For the auxiliary places \(Q_i\) in Levent Alpöge's archive, the exact contribution to the split bound is
\[
     t\sum_{v\in Q_i}\frac1{\varphi(\ell^{a_v})},
     \qquad \ell=D.\mathrm{nd}.l(i),\qquad
     \ell^{a_v}=|\psi_{L,i}(I_v)|,\quad a_v\ge1.
\]
This includes \(\ell=2\), every local index \(i\), and the empty set \(Q_i\). Under the actual character tie, the summands also give the individual **tameVal** infima used in **partD**. There is a simultaneous constraint on how small these losses can be once the characters have been realized over a common number field. We work at revision **40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005**. ## The character on the selected inertia group

Fix \(D:\mathrm{D7}\), an index \(i\), and \(v\in Q_i\). Put \(O=D.\mathrm{nd}.O(i)\), let \(I_v\) be inertia at the source's chosen extension of \(v\), and write \(\gamma_v=D.\mathrm{nd}.\mathrm{gam0}(i,v)\). The source defines \(\zeta_v=\chi_{i,1}(\gamma_v)\) in \(O\). Its sets \(Q_i,S_i\) are disjoint, the places of both lie away from \(\ell\), and the auxiliary level places of the base twist away from \(\ell\) lie in \(S_i\). Hence the base twist is unramified at \(v\), and on all of \(I_v\) the factorization of \(\chi_{i,1}\) reduces to \(\psi_{L,i}\). The source proves this reduction separately at \(\gamma_v\). The kernel of \(\psi_{L,i}\) is open in the absolute Galois group, so its image is finite. Every element of the image has \(\ell\)-power order. Wild inertia is pro-\(p_v\), where \(p_v\ne\ell\), and its continuous image in this finite \(\ell\)-group is trivial. By the defining topological generation condition on \(\gamma_v\), the image of \(I_v\) is exactly \(\langle\zeta_v\rangle\). Indeed the preimage of this cyclic group is closed and contains both the tame generator and wild inertia. Exact ramification at \(Q_i\) means that the image is nontrivial **at each** of these places. Thus \(\zeta_v\) has exact order \(\ell^{a_v}\), with \(a_v\ge1\), and the nontriviality hypothesis on the last term of **ShaBoundTheoremB** holds for these selected data. This supplies that conjunct independently of any rank or slope condition on the automorphic representation. ## Exact normalized length

Write \(m=\ell^{a_v}\), \(h=\varphi(m)\), and \(\zeta=\zeta_v\). Every primitive \(m\)-th root \(z\) in \(O\) is a power of \(\zeta\), and conversely \(\zeta\) is a power of \(z\). The geometric sum identity gives divisibility in both directions between \(1-z\) and \(1-\zeta\); they differ by a unit. All \(h\) primitive roots lie in \(O\), since it contains \(\zeta\). Factoring \(\Phi_m\) into these linear factors, and evaluating at 1, gives the equality of ideals
\[
     (\ell)=(\Phi_m(1))=(1-\zeta)^h.
\]
Here \(\Phi_m(X)=\sum_{j=0}^{\ell-1} X^{j\ell^{a_v-1}}\), also valid for \(m=2\). Thus \(h\mid e_O\), where \(e_O=\operatorname{length}_O(O/\ell O)>0\), and
\[
   \frac{\operatorname{length}_O(O/(\zeta-1))}
        {\operatorname{length}_O(O/\ell O)}=\frac1h.
\]
For an independent check on the length step, the exact sequence for multiplication by a nonzero \(b\) gives \(\operatorname{length}(O/(ab))=\operatorname{length}(O/(a))+\operatorname{length}(O/(b))\). Applying it successively to \(1-\zeta\) gives the same formula without choosing a uniformizer. In a DVR, finiteness and positivity of the denominator follow from \(\ell\ne0\) and its membership in the maximal ideal. [Original normalized module length](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/SharpRestriction/LevelLetterCocycles/DataN.lean#L32-L39) · [Mathlib cyclotomic value at one](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/d13f23b723b8a846827a245b89c10fc7d3f11612/Mathlib/RingTheory/Polynomial/Cyclotomic/Eval.lean#L32-L47) · [Exact sequence and additivity of length](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/d13f23b723b8a846827a245b89c10fc7d3f11612/Mathlib/RingTheory/OrderOfVanishing/Basic.lean#L77-L120)

Every nonidentity inertia value \(z\) is a power of \(\zeta\), so \(\zeta-1\mid z-1\) and its normalized valuation is at least \(1/h\). Equality occurs at \(\gamma_v\). Changing to the inverse character multiplies each nonzero difference by a unit. Under **NChTie**, the local character and the finite ray class character agree after the given embeddings of values of \(\psi_0\), and the sign option can invert the whole character. These embeddings preserve the order of every value. Applying the same formula over the two coefficient DVRs gives pointwise equality with the infimum **tameVal**, strengthening the one-sided source comparison. The infimum excludes the identity and is attained. At \(\zeta=1\) the original principal quotient has infinite length in the DVR; its encoded `toNat` is zero. Exact ramification is essential for a finite-length interpretation. ## Local and simultaneous restrictions on the budget

Let \(q_v=|k(v)|\). Arithmetic Frobenius acts on tame inertia by the \(q_v\)-power map modulo wild inertia. In the finite tame extension cut out by the local character, this can be seen by sending \(\tau\in I\) to the reduction of \(\tau(\pi)/\pi\) for a uniformizer \(\pi\) upstairs. This reduction is independent of the uniformizer since inertia fixes the residue of every unit. Its kernel is the first ramification group, which is trivial in this tame extension. Conjugating \(\tau\) by arithmetic Frobenius raises the reduction to the \(q_v\)-th power. The character image is abelian, hence \(\zeta_v^{q_v-1}=1\). In particular \(\ell^{a_v}\mid q_v-1\). If \(b=v_\ell(q_v-1)\), then \(b\ge1\) and the local cost is at least \(1/\varphi(\ell^b)\), while it is at most \(1/(\ell-1)\). [Archived tame Frobenius relation](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/DiscreteGroups/GlobalIrreducibility/DzTame.lean#L130-L151)

The source's complex conjugation takes each \(Q_i\) to itself because \(\psi_{L,i}\) is anticyclotomic and ramifies exactly there. Indeed transporting inertia at \(v\) by the lifted conjugation gives inertia at \(\bar v\), and changes its character image to its inverse. The choice of valuation extending a given place has no effect since a different choice conjugates inertia and the character is abelian. Thus \(a_v=a_{\bar v}\). All these places are split in the CM extension, so each conjugation orbit in \(Q_i\) has size two. One may equivalently sum the loss with factor \(2t\) over the conjugate pairs. This uses the order of the entire inertia image and does not require the two selected tame generators to be literal inverses. The **PsiOk** hypotheses for the auxiliary choice further require residue degree one above \(\mathbb Q\) at every \(v\in Q\), and put all character values in the powers of a fixed primitive \(\ell^{k'}\)-th root. Consequently \(q_v=p_v\) is the underlying rational prime and \(a_v\le k'\). The same argument applies before tying the choices: **PsiOk** gives exact ramification, an open kernel, anticyclotomic symmetry, and support away from \(\ell\); the finite tame image is cyclic. If **partD** holds with \(\varepsilon>0\), every individual conjugate pair gives
\[
  \frac{2t}{\varepsilon}\le\varphi(\ell^{a_v})
     =\frac{\ell-1}{\ell}\ell^{a_v}
     \le \frac{\ell-1}{\ell}(p_v-1),
  \qquad \frac{t|Q|}{\varepsilon}\le\varphi(\ell^{k'}).
\]
Thus every selected rational auxiliary prime satisfies \(p_v\ge1+2\ell t/((\ell-1)\varepsilon)\), as well as \(p_v\equiv1\pmod{\ell^{a_v}}\). A split prime with a large norm but insufficient \(\ell\)-part of \(p_v-1\) cannot realize the budget. Now fix **one tied realization** with common number field \(L=D.\mathrm{what}_2.\mathrm{LL}\). For any finite collection of pairs \((i,v)\) as above, put \(M=\operatorname{lcm}_{(i,v)}\ell(i)^{a_v}\), taking \(M=1\) for the empty collection. The tie places an element of order \(\ell(i)^{a_v}\) in \(L\) itself by evaluating its \(L\)-valued \(\psi_0\) at \(\gamma_v\) and using the injective coefficient embeddings. The multiplicative subgroup generated by these finitely many roots of unity is finite cyclic, hence contains a primitive \(M\)-th root. Cyclotomic irreducibility over \(\mathbb Q\) and the tower degree formula imply
\[
                \varphi(M)\mid [L:\mathbb Q].
\]
In particular, retain one index \(i(\ell)\) with nonempty \(Q_{i(\ell)}\) for each of several distinct \(\ell\), and let \(A_\ell=\max_{v\in Q_{i(\ell)}}a_v\). The **product** \(\prod_\ell\varphi(\ell^{A_\ell})\) divides this single field degree. For example, a field carrying primitive roots of orders 9 and 5 must have degree divisible by 24, even though the lcm of their individual degrees is 12. [Common field and both embeddings in the tie](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/LocalRingCardinality/DataNChTie.lean#L17-L25) · [Degree of the cyclotomic subextension](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/d13f23b723b8a846827a245b89c10fc7d3f11612/Mathlib/NumberTheory/Cyclotomic/PrimitiveRoots.lean#L176-L196)

If the actual auxiliary costs at these indices must be at most \(\varepsilon_\ell>0\), then
\[
  \frac{t|Q_{i(\ell)}|}{\varepsilon_\ell}\le\varphi(\ell^{A_\ell}),\qquad
  \prod_\ell\frac{t|Q_{i(\ell)}|}{\varepsilon_\ell}\le [L:\mathbb Q].
\]
This necessary condition holds after fixing the tied fields and auxiliary characters. In the original **ProjectionDerivativeEstimate**, \(Q,k',\psi'\) are chosen *after* the requested error, and **NChTie** is a separate condition relating chosen data. The degree bound supplies no obstruction to enlarging the rationality field across different realizations, and supplies no existence theorem for simultaneous Selmer control or nonvanishing. The other terms in **boundSplit**, inequality (M) and the independent spectral identity remain unresolved. ## Formal scope

The companion module proves seven declarations in `GRWTSK.NumberTheory.AlpogeTameConductorLength`. `associated_cyclotomic_at_one`, `totient_mul_length` and `normalized_length` establish the principal-ideal and actual module-length identities. `generator_order` recovers the positive prime-power order from cyclicity, nontriviality and torsion. `tame_infimum` computes the real infimum of the normalized quotients on any such generated image. `order_dvd_residue_sub_one` takes the tame Frobenius relation in the character kernel and proves the residue-size divisibility. `simultaneous_order_degree` proves exact integral degree divisibility for the least common multiple of any finite family of root orders in a number field. Native elaboration, fresh kernel replay, and transitive axiom auditing apply to these declarations. The identification of the source characters, the local ramification-theoretic input, and the budget consequences above are manuscript arguments.

# The Artin fibers in the orbit prime sum

Let \(M=\mathrm{QS}\,q\,d=\mathrm{condN}\), \(U=(\mathbb Z/M)^\times\), \(A=\mathrm{Frame.A}\) written additively, \(G=\{\pm1\}^2\times A\), and \(\alpha:U\to G\) the host's actual Artin map (using the archive's additive-to-multiplicative type identification). This is the full auxiliary group \(A\), which need not be the cyclic group of the real dyadic tower. For \(p\nmid M\), the class and all the varying orbit data previously used in the prime-gain estimate are fixed on **one fiber of \(\alpha\)**. Every such fiber is a disjoint union of exactly \(\varphi(M)/(4|A|)\) ordinary reduced progressions. The possible real exceptional character either disappears on averaging each fiber, or descends to \(G\). In the descended case its sign is constant on the entire inert odd-order branch. This identifies the actual prime-count supplier above its conductor threshold, and gives a separate threshold-free bound for the extra odd-orbit gain.

## Frobenius and the grouping of the original demands

The host embeds in \(\mathbb Q(\zeta_M)\), and the source specifies the action of \(\alpha(u)\) on this embedded field by restriction of \(\zeta_M\mapsto\zeta_M^u\). For \(p\nmid M\), this automorphism with \(u=p\bmod M\) is the arithmetic Frobenius on the cyclotomic integers: reduction sends the primitive root to its \(p\)-th power, and the integer ring is generated by that root. Restriction to the host gives its arithmetic Frobenius at every prime above \(p\), independent of choice since the extension is abelian. Conversely, unramifiedness gives uniqueness, and a prime above \(p\) exists by lying over. Every automorphism of the embedded Galois field extends to the normal cyclotomic field, so \(\alpha\) is surjective. These arguments reconstruct precisely the two fields of the archive's **HostDer** used here. Thus the epsilon-selected set in the definition of \(h_P\) is the singleton \(\{\alpha(p)\}\), and \(h_P\) equals the second group component in additive coordinates. Multiplication by a preimage of \(g\) bijects \(\ker\alpha\) with \(\alpha^{-1}(g)\), giving the fiber count. The first two signs are the actions on the quadratic radicals. In particular \(c_0=(-1,-1)\) gives the inert branch, \((1,-1)\) gives signal and second sign \(+1\) gives split. For fixed \(j=i+1,\ i<m\), the demands \(w_z=(k-1)\mathrm{wPl}(D,j,z)\) are independent of \(p\); assume the source size hypothesis giving nonnegativity. Then \(g\) determines the place permutation and its specified period \(r\), including the signal step, the choice of special branch and the invariant subset for the consumer. Write \(f_g(t)\) for the associated real-variable gain and \(W_g\) for the sum of the actual demands on that subset. This use includes the exceptional \(h_P=0\), inert formula of \(\mathrm{FInert}\) with its own sum over \(\tau\). The [prime-variation estimate](AlpogeOrbitPrimeVariation.md) gives \(0\le f_g(t)\le W_g t/(t^2-1)\), \(|f'_g(t)|\le4W_g/t^2\) almost everywhere on \(t\ge3\). The three subtractions of \(\mathrm{lowCs}\) use their original coefficients. A filter on primes besides the host's class conditions must be retained separately.

## Aggregating the relative count without a totient loss

Use **PrimesRel** at modulus \(M\) and its stated constants. For \(t\ge\max(5,M^{c_2})\), each reduced class \(u\) has the source count with main term \((\operatorname{Li}(t)-\theta_u\operatorname{Li}(t^\beta))/\varphi(M)\) and absolute error bounded by \(\epsilon_M(t)\) times this main term. Here \(\epsilon_M(t)=C_A(e^{-c_3\log t/\log M}+e^{-\sqrt{c_3\log t}})\), and \(\operatorname{Li}(x)=\int_2^x dv/\log v\). With no exceptional character the second term is taken as zero. Otherwise \(\theta_u=\chi(u)\in\{\pm1\}\) for the single real exceptional character modulo \(M\); in its specified range \(t^\beta>2\). This statement is a prime-count input separate from RH. The source includes its quantitative zero-repulsion and possible exceptional-zero hypotheses in the exact formulation. [PrimesRel](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Definitions/GlobalTwisting/ZerosDisc.lean#L25-L44) · [Alpöge archive's companion proof entry point](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/GlobalInterpolation/PrimesInProgressionsRelativeErrorStatement.lean) · [Thorner and Zaman, *Refinements to the prime number theorem for arithmetic progressions*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2108.10878)

If \(\chi\) is nontrivial on \(\ker\alpha\), translation by an element of the kernel of character \(-1\) pairs opposite signs within every fiber. Put \(\eta_g=0\) in that case and in the no-exception case. Otherwise \(\chi=\bar\chi\circ\alpha\) by surjectivity, and put \(\eta_g=\bar\chi(g)\). Summing the relative bounds using positivity of each main term gives the **same relative bound** for the full fiber:
\[
 \pi_g(t)=m_g(t)+R_g(t),\qquad
 m_g(t)=\frac{\operatorname{Li}(t)-\eta_g\operatorname{Li}(t^\beta)}{|G|},
 \qquad |R_g(t)|\le\epsilon_M(t)m_g(t).
\]
The size of the kernel cancels exactly against the totient; summing errors before this normalization can introduce a spurious growing factor.

On the actual subwindow \((P,Q]\) with \(P\ge\max(5,M^{c_2})\) put \(b_g(t)=f_g(t)\log t\). For any chosen set of fibers \(S\subset G\) the weighted count has main term
\[
 \frac1{|G|}\int_P^Q\sum_{g\in S} f_g(t)(1-\eta_g t^{\beta-1})\,dt .
\]
Integration by parts shows its absolute error is bounded by the sum over \(g\in S\) of
\[
 \sum_{x=P,Q} b_g(x)\epsilon_M(x)m_g(x)
 +W_g\int_P^Q\epsilon_M(t)m_g(t)
      \left(4\log t/t^2+1/(t^2-1)\right)dt .
\]
Indeed \(m'_g(t)\log t=(1-\eta_g t^{\beta-1})/|G|\). The count up to \(P\) need not be estimated using an asymptotic below \(P\). The formula can be restricted to the permitted \(Q\le X''(n)\). Additional finite exclusions (for example the two auxiliary primes where specified) require subtracting the corresponding terms if they occur. An inclusive lower endpoint can be put back individually. If the source window lies below the threshold, this instance of PrimesRel supplies no estimate there. In particular \(X''(n)=xn\exp(x^{\epsilon/3})\) by the archive's scale definitions; a bound \(M\le b=\exp(x^\epsilon)\) alone does not place even its upper endpoint above \(M^{c_2}\). One must use the finer construction or a different prime estimate there. ## Odd-order elements and a uniform saving below the conductor threshold

Let \(A_{\rm odd}\) consist of elements of odd order. The inert branches for zero step, nonzero odd step, and even step comprise respectively \(1, |A_{\rm odd}|-1, |A|-|A_{\rm odd}|\) full fibers. These are actual residue proportions after dividing by \(4|A|\). On every odd-step fiber a descended real character has the **same** sign \(\bar\chi(c_0,0)\), since a homomorphism into \(\{\pm1\}\) kills elements of odd order. Thus the zero-order and nonzero odd-order fiber counts use the same scalar exceptional bias; any centered contrast on these fibers whose weights sum to zero pointwise has no exceptional main term. That cancellation does not justify erasing the exceptional term from an uncentered positive gain.

There is also an unconditional bound for the extra incident-load correction in the [odd-period flow formula](AlpogeOddOrbitCapacity.md), independent of a prime-count theorem. Set \(B=2^{e+2}\). The host projection \(A\to(\mathbb Z/B)^\times/\{\pm1\}\) sends every odd-order element to the identity because the latter is a 2-group. By the exact Artin projection it sends \(h_P\) to the class of \(p\). Hence every \(\mathrm{IsCs}\) prime satisfies \(p\equiv\pm1\bmod B\), and its odd period is at least three. For the correction alone, summing over the source \(\mathrm{kindBC}\) places, take \(W=(k-1)\sum_{z\in U}\mathrm{wPl}(D,j,z)\); here \(U\) consists exactly of the places with first coordinate true on this branch and is independent of \(p\). The positive surplus per place is at most its demand. Thus over **any** allowed subwindow the entire extra correction to the doubled-cover value, with log-prime weights, is between zero and
\[
 \sum_{p\text{ in window},\,\mathrm{IsCs}} W\log(p)/(p^3+1)
 \ \le\ \frac{2W}{(B-1)^3}\big(\zeta(3)\log(B+1)-\zeta'(3)\big).
\]
Indeed every candidate is \(nB\pm1,\ n\ge1\), and \(n(B-1)\le nB\pm1\le n(B+1)\). The same bound holds for the full-place correction with its own total demand. The argument preserves the ambient weights and gives a summable cubic loss without a factor for the number of classes or primes. Conversely \(p\equiv\pm1\bmod B\) need not characterize a full \(A\)-coordinate; the kernel of the projection and both first signs still matter.

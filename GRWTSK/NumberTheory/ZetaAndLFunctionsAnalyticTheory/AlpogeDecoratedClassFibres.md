## Every penultimate coupling on every framed lattice

At stage \(j+1=m\) the extra decoration of a fixed framed \(N\) is a *set* \(Y=y+D\subset E^n\). Let \(S_G=v_{Gs}\) and \(S_Q=Q_E(\mu)\) be the disjoint row-place sets. For \(P\) in their union let \(q_P=\mathrm NP\) and write \(N_P\) here for the localization inside \(V\). The larger module of eligible centers requires \(y\in q_P^{-1}N_P\) at \(S_G\) and \(y\in q_P^{-M}N_P\) at \(S_Q\). The smaller translation module \(D\) requires membership in \(N_P\) and \(q_P^M N_P\), respectively, with **no** constraints outside the union. Thus
\[
 \{Y\text{ above }N\}\ \longleftrightarrow\
 \prod_{P\in S_G}q_P^{-1}N_P/N_P\
 \times\prod_{P\in S_Q} q_P^{-M}N_P/q_P^M N_P.
\]
The map sends a center to its local cosets. Its kernel on the module of eligible centers is exactly \(D\). For surjectivity choose a rational representative \(a_i\) of each desired local coset. For each \(P_j\) pick \(k_j\) so large that \(P_j^{k_j}a_i\) lies in the translation module at \(P_j\) for every \(i\). The \(P_j^{k_j}\) are pairwise coprime. CRT supplies selectors \(e_i\) congruent to \(\delta_{ij}\) modulo \(P_j^{k_j}\). The single vector \(\sum_i e_i a_i\) has all prescribed cosets. Each target lies in its eligible center module, and adding a period preserves eligibility. The companion Lean proof works for general local period modules and arbitrary common denominator requirements on the finite list of centers. For completed local lattices the quotient modulo these open periods agrees with the localized quotient by density. ## Class maps, rational transporters and stabilizers

Use the resulting standard chart with its **full** finite adelic level \(K_\beta\). Write \(U=U(h)(F)\) for the rational unitary group, \(K_\beta^p\) for the product of the specified local away levels, and \(K_p\) for the product of hyperspecial stabilizers. Consider the \(U\)-sets
\[
 A_\beta=U(h)(\mathbb A_f^p)/K_\beta^p,\qquad
 B_p=\prod_{v\mid p}\mathrm{GL}_n(F_v)/\mathrm{GL}_n(\mathcal O_{F_v})
\]
in the specified frames. Quotienting \(A_\beta\times B_p\) by the one diagonal rational \(U\)-action gives the true adelic classes in this chart. Indeed \(K_\beta=K_\beta^p\times K_p\) under the product decomposition, since the away conditions are all imposed at individual places and the served level is hyperspecial. The map to lattices is the self-dual gluing above, together with its away decorations. At word places these are nonzero residual functionals, and at type places they are full residue frames up to the indicated level. A unitary local representative can attain any such chosen residual decoration: in the selected split branch act by integral general linear matrices, lifting invertible residue matrices, and impose the dual action in the conjugate branch. At a tuple place the framed point uses a rational \(E\)-basis matrix inducing the given localized lattice. Two such matrices differ by a rational integral basis change at that place. The source cut level reads this difference in its finite congruence quotient. Conversely an arbitrary local basis can be changed by a sufficiently deep congruence to a rational matrix using density of \(E\) entrywise in its completion. Invertibility and containment in a prescribed open matrix coset survive this change. This explains why the adelic/fibre assertion uses the standard charts' finite-depth frame levels. An arbitrary abstract subgroup of rational local basis changes need not have this property. The disjointness from the conjugate selected primes ensures that a required dual correction introduces no conflicting decoration.

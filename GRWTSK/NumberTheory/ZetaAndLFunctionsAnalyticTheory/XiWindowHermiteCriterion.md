# Hermite positivity on the actual Mathlib zeta divisor

The theorem `GRWTSK.XiWindowHermiteCriterion.weighted_windows_iff_RH` proves an equivalence with the proposition `_root_.RiemannHypothesis`. At each absolute height bound \(T\), take every nontrivial Mathlib zeta zero \(s\) with \(|\Im s|\le T\) and send it to \(z=\Im s+i(1/2-\Re s)\). The weight is the positive finite analytic order of the original zeta germ. The assertion equivalent to RH is nonnegativity of the Hermite trace form on all polynomials of degree less than the number of distinct nodes in each such window. Endpoints are included, and both signs and all horizontal coordinates are retained. [Formal statement](XiWindowHermiteCriterion.lean) · [Canonical divisor](NonrealZerosOfZetaSAndLSChiRiemannAndOtherHypotheses/CanonicalZeroDivisor.lean)

The inverse coordinate recovers \(s\). The zeta functional equation and conjugation give another actual zero \(1-\bar s\) with the same height, which becomes the conjugate node. The [finite interpolation theorem](XiContourTraceForm.lean) proves that positivity forces every node to equal its conjugate. Applying this at the height of an arbitrary nontrivial zero gives \(\Re s=1/2\) with exactly the exclusions in Mathlib. In the other direction RH puts all nodes on the real axis, making every term a nonnegative weighted square.

The formula here uses the finite actual divisor to state and verify the spectral comparison. The [operator and arithmetic tests](XiContourTraceForm.md) are defined independently by contour integrals. Residue identification, finite algebra with its canonical positive metric, closed operator assembly, verified numerical quadrature and positivity at unbounded heights are separate obligations. The analytic identification and construction are manuscript results. The universal positivity assertion remains open.

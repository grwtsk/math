# The nonsplit prime gain on the chosen cyclic host

Fix the cyclic host constructed in Alpöge's archive at revision `40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005`, with its full quadratic-prime row, zero boxes, and the stated relative prime-count input. Fix $0<\epsilon<1/52$, a sufficiently large scale $x$, and an admissible lift $j=i+1, 0\le i<m$, with its nonnegative demands $d_z=(k-1)\mathrm{wPl}(D,j,z)$. The $2n$ places are indexed by $z\in\{0,1\}\times A$, $A=\mathbb Z/n$, $n=2^e$. Write $W=\sum_z d_z$, $M=4nqd$, and $S(P,Q)$ for the sum of the archive's $\mathrm{FInert}(D,j,p)\log p$ over unramified inert-class and signal primes in $P<p\le Q$. For $5\le P<Q\le X''=xnX'$ one obtains
$$
 S(P,Q)=\frac1{4n}\sum_{u,z}\int_P^Q(d_u/t-d_z)_+\,dt+O(W\log M).                 \tag{1}
$$
The implied constant depends only on the relative prime-count constants. Under the source's full size windows let $T$ count thick places, $W_T$ be their total demand, and $W_N=W-W_T$. On the original upper cutoff the leading contribution is
$$
 S(5,X'')=\frac{2n-T}{4n}W_T\log X'
 +O\big(W\log M+W\log(xn)+W_N\log X'\big).                                      \tag{2}
$$
All place counts and weights in these formulas are for the individual lift. For $W_T>0$ the relative error in (2) is $O_\epsilon(x^{-2\epsilon/15}\log^2x)$. Equation (1) is an absolute estimate on subwindows, even when their main term is small. An inclusive lower endpoint adds its actual term.

## Conductor, real characters and the lower cutoff

The selected witness in **hostEx** uses the cyclic frame with $o=1$, $n=2^e$, $e\ge2$, and $H.QS=4n$. Its Artin group is $G=\{\pm1\}^2\times A$. Its three coordinates at $p\nmid M$ are $\chi_{-q}(p),\chi_{-d}(p)$ and the image of $p$ in $(\mathbb Z/4n)^\times/\{\pm1\}\simeq A$, with class of $5$ corresponding to $1$. Inert-class has signs $(-,-)$, signal has $(+,-)$. The branch **IsCs** requires a nonzero element of odd order in $A$, so is empty on this frame. The generic host permits other frames. [Cyclic witness and chosen host](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/GlobalInterpolation/HostExistenceOfChoices.lean#L179-L320) · [Artin fibers and restriction to the host](AlpogeArtinOddFibers.md)

For the relative count **PrimesRel** an exceptional quadratic character modulo $M$ may contribute to a single progression. If nontrivial on the Artin kernel, it cancels within every fiber by the sign-reversing translation. Otherwise it descends to $G$. Every real character of $G$ is a product of any subset of $\chi_{-q},\chi_{-d},\chi_8=(2/\cdot)$. The cyclic factor has precisely one nontrivial real character and $\chi_8(5)=-1$, $\chi_8(-1)=1$. For the six products involving $q$ or $d$, the primitive character has conductor divisible by that odd prime because its restriction to that CRT factor is the nontrivial Legendre symbol. The six **Boxq** bounds for the primitive inducing characters exclude all real zeros $\beta>1-1/400$. This can also be read directly from **BoxH.real_ne_zero** at the primitive conductor of a host character. For $\log M\ge400c_1$ this includes every possible exceptional zero. For the remaining nontrivial character $L(s,\chi_8)>0$ on real $s>0$. Its Dirichlet series converges there by bounded partial sums and grouping complete blocks gives strictly positive terms
$$
 (8a+1)^{-s}-(8a+3)^{-s}-(8a+5)^{-s}+(8a+7)^{-s}>0
$$
by strict convexity. For the principal primitive character $\zeta(s)=\eta(s)/(1-2^{1-s})<0$ on $0<s<1$ by the positive alternating-pair series. Thus all surviving fiber main terms are the same, even when the exceptional character is nontrivial on the Artin kernel. This uses the boxes on the chosen row. On $t\ge D=\max(5,M^{c_2})$ the count per fiber is $\operatorname{Li}(t)(1+O(E(t)))/(4n)$, $E(t)=e^{-c_3\log t/\log M}+e^{-\sqrt{c_3\log t}}$, by adding the progression bounds with their positive main terms. For a fixed class gain $f_g(t)$, $0\le f_g(t)\le Wt/(t^2-1)$, $|f'_g(t)|\le4W/t^2$ almost everywhere. Partial summation of $f_g(t)\log t$ against the prime count, using $\operatorname{Li}(t)\ll t/\log t$, costs per fiber
$$
 \ll \frac{W}{4n}\left(1+\int_D^\infty E(t)\frac{dt}{t}\right)\ll \frac{W\log M}{4n}.
$$
The first term of the integral is at most $\log M/c_3$, and the second is at most $2/c_3$ by $v=\sqrt{c_3\log t}$. Below $D$, the whole prime contribution is $O(W\log D)$, since $\sum_{p\le y}\log p/p\ll\log y$. Indeed $\binom{2m}{m}\le 4^m$ bounds the sum of $\log p$ on each dyadic block $(m,2m]$ by $2m\log2$. Dividing by $m$ and summing proves the claim. The continuous main term below $D$ has the same bound. These estimates apply to truncated parts of either range, and summing the large-range errors over the $2n$ nonsplit fibers costs $O(W\log M)$. The prime divisors of $M$ never enter the Artin sum. In the full **FvalP**, $d$ has its separate signed **FRamD**, the two type-$g$ primes have their own branch and are $1\bmod M$ on the chosen host. [Relative-count statement](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Definitions/GlobalTwisting/ZerosDisc.lean#L25-L44) · [Thorner and Zaman, 2021, Theorem 1.1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2108.10878v2) · [Gain variation and summation by parts](AlpogeOrbitPrimeVariation.md) · [Signed prime branches](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/LocalRingCardinality/DataForced.lean#L109-L131)

In the actual row, $\sqrt{X_1}<q\le X_1$, $\sqrt{X_2}<d\le X_2$, $\log X_1=x^{\epsilon/5}$, $\log X_2=x^{\epsilon/5}\log^2x$, and $\log M\le2x^{\epsilon/5}\log^2x$. Since $\log X'=x^{\epsilon/3}$, the paid error on the entire prefix is $o(W\log X')$. In particular the large-range supplier applies well before $X'$, even though it cannot be invoked at every prime in the original window. The lower bound on $d$ also ensures $\log M\to\infty$.

For $P\ge(X')^{1/2}$ the same argument keeps the lower limit $P$ in the discrepancy integral. Its two terms are bounded by $(\log M/c_3)e^{-c_3\log P/\log M}$ and $(2/c_3)(1+\sqrt{c_3\log P})e^{-\sqrt{c_3\log P}}$. The endpoint error is $O(WE(P))$. Including the $O(W/P)$ integrated LP approximation below, the error in (1) on these upper subwindows improves to $O_\epsilon(W\exp(-c x^{2\epsilon/15}/\log^2 x))$ for some $c>0$. Indeed $\log P\ge x^{\epsilon/3}/2$ and the prefactors can be absorbed by decreasing $c$ for sufficiently large $x$.

## The place-weight histogram

On each nontrivial inert translation the cycles have even length. A signal translation switches rows, so again every cycle has even length. For one such cycle let $U$ denote its backward shift, $w$ its nonnegative demand vector, $V=\|w\|_1$, and $y=(I+U/t)^{-1}w$. The [even-orbit optimum](AlpogeOrbitFlowClosedForm.md) gives $F(t)=V/(t^2-1)+(1-1/t)\sum(-y)_+$. The equation gives $y-(w-Uw/t)=U^2y/t^2$ and $\|y\|_1\le tV/(t-1)$. Hence $A_0=\sum(Uw/t-w)_+\le V/t$ and the 1-Lipschitz bound for the negative part yields
$$
 |F(t)-A_0|\le V/(t^2-1)+(1-1/t)V/[t(t-1)]+V/t^2
 \le 25V/(8t^2)\qquad(t\ge3).
$$
On the zero inert translation the archive instead prescribes $W/(t^2-1)$, and its proxy is zero. Both totals $W$ agree since **tauPl** bijects places with the source's embeddings. Summing cycles uses each place once. For each destination $(b,a)$, ranging over all shifts runs through all incoming sites of its own row on inert-class fibers and of the other row on signal fibers. Each ordered pair occurs once. Division by the full group size $4n$ gives the integrand in (1), with absolute error at most $25W/(16t^2)$. Integrating contributes $O(W)$. For fixed $a=d_u, b=d_z>0$, set $v=\min(Q,\max(P,a/b))$. The pair integral is $a\log(v/P)-b(v-P)$. At $b=0$ it is $a\log(Q/P)$. ## Full-window size comparison

For the full **ParameterChoice'** bounds, put $b=\exp(x^\epsilon)$, $b'=xb/X'$, $w''=b/(nX')$. Thick weights before the factor $k-1$ lie between $b$ and $b+\eta+1$. Thin weights are at most $w''+\eta$. The selected top weights are at most $5b'/4+b'/x+\eta$. The source gives $t_{\rm rank}\eta\le w''/x$ with $t_{\rm rank}\ge1$. Thus for sufficiently large $x$ all nonthick weights are at most $2b'$, and all thick weights are in $[b,2b]$. There are $1\le T\le n$ thick places. They occupy the second row. The positive second-set cardinal lower bound implies its image is nonempty, since $n_0,L_w>0$ on the chosen frame. [Lift windows and scales](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/LocalRingCardinality/DataParams₂.lean#L149-L157) · [Bound for top weights](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/LocalRingCardinality/ColumnWeightSpreadOffTop.lean#L85-L102) · [Size error](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/LocalRingCardinality/DataParams.lean#L242-L250) · [Thick set and cardinal lower bound](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/LocalRingCardinality/DataHost.lean#L129-L166)

For a thick source $u$ and nonthick destination $z$, the integrand is $d_u/t-d_z$ up to $Q_0=X'/(4x)$, because $Q_0d_z\le d_u/2$. Its integral from $5$ to $Q_0$ is at least $d_u(\log(Q_0/5)-1/2)$, and its whole-window integral is at most $d_u\log(X''/5)$. Each differs from $d_u\log X'$ by $O(d_u\log(xn))$ as a one-sided bound. Two thick places give zero for $t\ge5$. For nonthick sources, summing the elementary upper bound $d_u/t$ over all destinations and dividing by $4n$ costs at most $W_N\log(X''/5)/2$. This proves (2). The rank choice $n\le2x^{2+8\epsilon}$ gives $W_N/W_T\le 4nx/X'$ and $\log(xn)=O_\epsilon(\log x)$. The main coefficient lies between $1/4$ and $1/2$. Constant demands furnish a hostile check on applying (2) outside these size hypotheses. The histogram vanishes identically for $t\ge1$, while the true gain retains its summable baseline. [Rank bounds](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Definitions/GlobalTwisting/PairRowIffQdRows.lean#L28-L42)

The actual host also supplies a counterexample to a uniform $O(W)$ error in (1) on arbitrary subwindows. **Rowq** forces $\chi_{-d}(p)=1$ for every prime $p\le Y=x^{\epsilon/5}$. Thus $S(5,Y)=0$. For large $x$, $5<Y<Q_0$ and every thick-to-nonthick pair contributes at least $(d_u/2)\log(Y/5)$ to its integral. The density main term is therefore at least $(2n-T)W_T\log(Y/5)/(8n)\ge W_T\log(Y/5)/8$, and $W_T/W\to1$. Any refinement at this precision must account for the initial forced splitting.

## The full local valuation on rational primes

Let $S_{\rm val}(P,Q)=\sum_{P<p\le Q}\mathrm{FvalP}(L,j,p)\log p$ over rational primes. For the selected host with the full size bounds and sufficiently large $x$, one has exactly, for $5\le P<Q\le X''$,
$$
 S_{\rm val}(P,Q)= S(P,Q)
 +\mathbf 1_{\{P<d\le Q\}}\,\mathrm{FRamD}(D,j)\log d .
$$
Indeed the first branch is supported on the two distinct primes $g_0,g_1$. Each satisfies $g_i\ge(kb)^8>X''$ eventually, since $\log X''=x^{\epsilon/3}+\log(xn)$ and $\log b=x^\epsilon$. The next branch applies exactly at $d$. Since $H.QS=4n$ is a power of two, the $\mathrm{ram2}$ prime is $2$. The $\mathrm{ramQ}$ prime is $q$. Both give zero, as does every remaining split prime. The other two classes are precisely those in $S(P,Q)$, and their cutoff includes equality at $X''$. Here the distinct $g_i$ are $1\bmod M$ and hence never equal $d$ or $q$. This uses the ordering of the cases in **FvalP**, including the priority of the two type primes over their usual splitting class. [Prime classification](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/LocalRingCardinality/HostCls.lean#L29-L79) · [Actual type-prime set](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/LocalRingCardinality/DataParams₂.lean#L197-L200) · [Type-prime size and congruence](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/LocalRingCardinality/DataParams.lean#L266-L273) · [Local signed valuations](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/LocalRingCardinality/DataForced.lean#L112-L131)

At $d$, the exact additional term is $-\frac{k-1}{2}\sum_{t\in A}\min(w_{0,t},w_{1,t})\log d$, where $w_{b,t}=\mathrm{wPl}(D,j,(b,t))$. Every site in row $0$ is nonthick, hence $0\le w_{0,t}\le 2b'$. Consequently its absolute value is at most $(k-1)n b'\log d\le W_T(nx/X')\log d$. This applies with zero correction if $d$ is outside the particular window. On the full window, $5<d<X'$ eventually by the two row bounds for $d$. The relative change to (2) is $O((nx/X')\log d/\log X')$, so the same leading coefficient and error hold for $S_{\rm val}(5,X'')$.

Summing **FvalP** over all rational primes changes the answer further. On the chosen large row the possible inert or signal primes below and including $5$ are absent by forced splitting. Above $X''$ the nonsplit branch vanishes. The all-prime sum is therefore exactly
$$
 S(5,X'')+\mathrm{FRamD}(D,j)\log d
 -\tfrac12\,\mathrm{fockDeg}(D,j)\,(\log g_0+\log g_1).
$$
The last term is an additional signed obligation for any all-prime budget. Summing valuations over places with local degrees or lattice indices also requires the corresponding place-to-prime conversion.

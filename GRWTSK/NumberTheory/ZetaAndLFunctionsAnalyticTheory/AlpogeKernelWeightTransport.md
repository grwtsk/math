## The unipotent volume with its actual index orientation

At a row place \(P\) write \(q=\mathrm N P\), a rational prime for these split places. Let \(C=\mathrm{Cbe}(J_{i+1,P},h_{i+1})\), \(U=C\cap N_1\), \(K=N_1\cap\operatorname{Stab}(\lambda_{Y,i+1,P})\) and \(H=U\cap K\). Since \(\operatorname{relIndex}(H_1,H_2)=[H_2:H_1\cap H_2]\), the factor multiplying \(\mathrm{E1at}\) in the source is
\[
 \mathrm{volN}^{-1}=[K:H]/[U:H].
\]
The numerator is an integer. For finite \([U:H]\) we prove that it is a power of \(q\). Under the source's conventions an infinite index has natural cardinal zero and inversion of zero is zero, so the integrality assertion also covers a vanishing totalized factor. Positivity in a genuine Haar-volume interpretation separately requires finite indices. [Definitions of the volume and finite sum](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/LocalToGlobal/Columns/FiniteSumNonzeroLargeDepth.lean#L542-L580) · [Reciprocal volume in the actual local weight](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/LocalToGlobal/Columns/FiniteSumNonzeroLargeDepth.lean#L936-L949) · [Split row-place norms](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/GlobalInterpolation/BadPrimeNormPrimeNeLevelPrime.lean#L52-L66)

For \(g\in U\), write \(g=1+V\) in the local matrix algebra. The group \(N_1\) fixes the initial block and acts identically on the succeeding one-dimensional graded pieces of its filtration of length \(c+1\). Thus \(V^{c+1}=0\). For \(1\le k\le c\), \(n\mid c!\binom nk\), since \(k\binom nk=n\binom{n-1}{k-1}\) and \(k\mid c!\) for \(n>0\). Taking \(n=q^a\), the valuation of each coefficient of \(V^k\) in \(g^{q^a}-1\) grows without bound. This holds also for every conjugate \(z^{-1}gz\) with \(z\in U\). The lattice stabilized by \(K\) is diagonal in the given basis with local summands \(q^{e_j}\mathcal O_{E,P}\). Its stabilizer contains an open neighborhood of the identity: conjugate the integral congruence subgroup by the diagonal basis change and intersect with the rational matrices. Consequently \(g^{q^a}\) fixes each prescribed coset \(zH\) for large \(a\). For a finite coset set take the maximum over all its points. The resulting permutation has \(q\)-power order. The effective permutation group is a finite \(q\)-group by Cauchy's theorem, so its transitive orbit has \(q\)-power size. Normality of \(H\) was unnecessary. Alpöge's comparison proves the same index property by prime-to-\(q\) root extraction. ## The periods and the normalized projection

Put \(c_A=(\prod_Pq_P)^A,\ c_B=T(u)(\prod_Pq_P)^B,\ c=c_Ac_B\), and \(SS=v_T(u)\cup\mathrm{rowSG}\). For the selected catalogue construction \(m\le M\le A,B\). To see the early support and period directly, at a type place \(\omega\Phi\) vanishes outside the Hom of the weighted lattices and is invariant under \(q\) times that Hom: the original test is determined by reductions on the weighted lattices, and both frame maps preserve them. At \(i+2\le m\), all weights lie between 0 and \(m-1\). This gives ordinary matrix support in \(q^{-m}\mathrm{Mat}(\mathcal O_{E,P})\) and period \(q^{m}\mathrm{Mat}(\mathcal O_{E,P})\). At a tuple place support lies in \(q^{-(i+1)}\mathrm{Mat}(\mathcal O_{E,P})\) by the previous calculation. A difference \(q^{i+1}Z\) with \(Z\) integral becomes divisible by \(q\) after multiplication on the left by \(h_t^{-1}\) and on the right by \(h_s\), because \(q^ih_s\) is integral. This preserves the cutoff and all residue tests, even at a point off support. In \(\mathrm{xx1}(x,t)\) the original block is precisely \(x\) and changing it leaves the auxiliary columns in \(\mathrm{chArg}\) unchanged. The local sum inherits support with denominator \(q^A\) and period \(q^B\). For \(\operatorname{joinCol}\), variation in \(x\) varies just this block, so the same reasoning applies. For an abstract catalogue with smaller \(A,B\), use its stated \(\mathrm{xiSupp},\mathrm{xiInv},\mathrm{psiSuppX},\mathrm{psiInvX}\) fields instead. ## Which served series can be chosen

Fix a served place \(w\), a split local datum \(KD\) with its genuine class maps, a kernel-entry datum \(E\), and \(KD.\mathrm{lam}=E.\mathrm{lam}(w)\). A valid local lattice tuple has a column basis \(g_{j,L,v}\) at each place, with nonzero determinant in its local field. Choose such bases simultaneously, the identity on invalid tuples. Write \(A_{k,\tau}=\mathrm{rhoMat}(KD,k,\tau,g_{k,L_k})\). At stage \(j\) define \(D_{k,\tau}=A_{k,\tau}\) at the sites flagged by \(\mathrm{IsTypeV}\) **at \(j\)**, and \(D_{k,\tau}=A_{k,\tau}^{-\mathsf t}\) at the other sites, for both \(k=j,j+1\). The forward substitution, including the source-to-target transpose, is \(Y_\tau=D_{j+1,\tau}X_s^\mathsf tD_{j,\tau}^{-1}\) for \(\tau=\mathrm{tauPl}(s)\). Set the local series on rank-compatible pairs equal to
\[
 \Theta_{\rm served}(Y)
     = E.\mathrm{Th}\!\left(\left(D_{j+1,\tau}^{-1}Y_\tau D_{j,\tau}\right)^\mathsf t\right),
\]
with \(u,\mu(w),w,j\) and local class maps fixed. Use zero off the required rank equalities. The two substitutions compose to the identity by site bijectivity and matrix inversion. A homogeneous linear change of finitely many variables acts coefficientwise on formal series, so equality holds in all degrees. This gives the existential served-place comparison with all site scalars \(1\). The source constructs a split datum with a free series field and then replaces precisely that field. For an externally fixed local series the same argument gives a test for equality, not a proof of its value. The split levels and class maps, the arithmetic meaning of the coefficient and orbit embeddings, and identification with an independently defined automorphic theta lift require separate arguments. Two ingredients of the split-datum construction admit sharper statements. First, suppose the supplied split places correspond to distinct places \(v\) of \(F=E^{+}\) and their local fields and integral rings are identified with \(F_v,\mathcal O_{F_v}\), compatibly with restriction of the given \(E\)-embeddings. For a valid tuple \(L_v=g_v\mathcal O_{F_v}^n\) choose **one** \(B\in\mathrm{Mat}_n(F)\) lying in \(g_v\mathrm{GL}_n(\mathcal O_{F_v})\) simultaneously at all \(v\). These are open neighborhoods of the \(g_v\) in their matrix spaces. Entrywise weak approximation follows by clearing a common integer denominator and applying the Chinese remainder theorem to powers of the finitely many distinct prime ideals. For a nonempty tuple the local determinants imply \(\det B\ne0\); for an empty tuple choose the identity. Then the finitely generated free \(\mathcal O_E\)-lattice \(B\mathcal O_E^n\) has local span exactly \(L_v\) at all these places. Its basis supplies one inclusion, and the integral rings contain the images of \(\mathcal O_E\), giving the other. This produces the source's \(\mathrm{glob\_loc}\) field without requiring separate global generators at each coordinate. Prescribing a preassigned global decorated class, simultaneous self-duality at other places, or its automorphic equivalence relation imposes additional conditions. [Split completions, injectivity and the exact requested local spans](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/IwasawaDecomposition/ExistsGlobalModuleLocalisingToLattices.lean#L86-L128)

Second, suppose the local coefficient field \(K_f\) admits some ring embedding \(i_0:K_f\to\mathbb C_p\), as in the source reconstruction datum. To match the **prescribed** embedding of the countable algebraic coefficient field \(LL\), identify the two copies of \(LL\) by their field isomorphism inside \(\mathbb C_p\). The two transcendence bases of \(\mathbb C_p\) over these copies have cardinality \(\lvert\mathbb C_p\rvert\), since the base is countable and the ambient algebraically closed field is uncountable. A bijection of bases extends the field isomorphism to the rational function fields, then to their algebraic closures, giving a field automorphism \(\sigma\) of \(\mathbb C_p\). Now \(i_K=\sigma i_0\) satisfies the required comparison on \(LL\). The Frobenius-position formula in the source compares two elements of \(K_f\) **before** applying this map. It follows from the underlying split comparison's \(\mathrm{pos\_emb}\) on the specified orbit position, the identity of the underlying Hermitian records, and CM conjugation on \(E\) for odd position, identity for even position. Applying \(i_K\) preserves that equality. This reduction leaves construction of the original split orbit comparison and an initial \(i_0\) as premises. The embedding extension alone gives no valuation comparison on arbitrary transcendental matrix entries. Indeed choose \(t\in\mathbb Z_p^\times\) transcendental over the algebraic closure of \(\mathbb Q\) inside \(\mathbb C_p\), which is possible by countability. Complete \(t\) to a transcendence basis over that algebraic closure. The substitution \(t\mapsto pt\) fixing the other basis elements is an automorphism of the rational function field; it extends to an automorphism of \(\mathbb C_p\) fixing all algebraic coefficients and changing the valuation of \(t\). Thus a claim about the norm of a specified transcendental local entry requires an extra argument. The original served identity uses only matrix inversion, equality of coefficients and the unit scalar \(1\).

## Integrating one boost

For one opposite-sign pair let \(x_c,y_c\) be its two rows of variables, \(1\le c\le n\). On polynomials put
\[
 E=\sum_c x_cy_c,\qquad F=\sum_c\partial_{x_c}\partial_{y_c},\qquad
 H=[F,E]=n+N_x+N_y .
\]
Leibniz gives the last identity column by column and \([H,E]=2E\). Factorial Gram weights give \(E^*=F\). The compact subgroup \(\operatorname{diag}(e^{i\theta},e^{-i\theta})\) has infinitesimal weight \(H\): its action adds one on each creator in these two rows, and their commutator fixes the vacuum shift \(n\). More explicitly, \(E-F\) sends an \(x\)-creator by commutator to \(-\partial_y\) and sends \(-\partial_y\) to \(x\). With the phase/sign convention for identifying real displacements one obtains the boost or its inverse by changing the sign of \(t\). Both have the same compression below. Complex root phases of modulus one also cancel. These operators realize the trace-zero rank-one Lie algebra, fixing the additive scalar in \(H\). Generators for disjoint row pairs commute.

Suppose \(Fv=0\) and \(Hv=h v\). Induction gives \(H E^jv=(h+2j)E^jv\) and \(F E^{j+1}v=(j+1)(h+j)E^jv\). Thus \(\|E^jv\|^2=j!(h)_j\|v\|^2\) with rising factorial. For \(h=0\) the generated unitary module is trivial. Otherwise unitarity gives \(h>0\), and
\[
 u(t)=(\cosh t)^{-h}\sum_{j\ge0}\frac{(\tanh t)^j}{j!}E^jv
\]
converges in norm for every real \(t\), also after termwise application of \(E,F\) or real differentiation on compact \(t\)-intervals. The norm estimate follows directly from the factorial identity and \(|\tanh t|<1\). The ladder gives \(u'=(E-F)u\), \(u(0)=v\). Closedness of the infinitesimal boost generator and differentiation of its inverse unitary group applied to \(u(t)\) identify this with the actual group action. Against \(Q\in\ker F\) every term of positive \(j\) vanishes. Decompose the right harmonic polynomial into \(H\)-weights. For several disjoint pairs repeat the expansion, using commuting ladders and joint weight components. This proves (1) including \(p q=0\), when there are no boosts.

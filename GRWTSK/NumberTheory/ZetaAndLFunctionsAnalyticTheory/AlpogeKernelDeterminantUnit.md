## Principalization and the two signed characters

\[
 z=a/\bar a,\qquad z\bar z=1,\qquad J^{2n}=(z).
\]
The injective norm-one ideal parametrization, and its principal and product laws, give \(d^{2n}=e(z)b\) with \(b\in U_O\), the image of integral finite ideles. For every \(\tau\), extension of the determinant ideal to \(R\) gives
\[

Indeed the preceding comparison identifies \(JR\) with \((\det B_\tau)R\), and formation of fractional ideal products commutes with extension. The argument applies to any ideal class order, including \(n\) divisible by \(p\).

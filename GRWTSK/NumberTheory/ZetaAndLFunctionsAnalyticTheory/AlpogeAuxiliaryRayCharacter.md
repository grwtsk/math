# Auxiliary ray characters of prescribed order for the split Selmer estimate

grwtsk contributors

Fix the original CM host and twisting data, an index \(i\), and \(N=\ell^M\), where \(\ell=X.l(i)\) and \(M\ge1\). We construct the archive's **K1** admissible family, including full decomposition-group triviality above \(\ell\), and exhibit its two families of test places with the Frobenius coordinates in **Steps**. In particular, for every error \(\varepsilon>0\) and finite forbidden set \(A\), the choices in **PsiOk** and **partD** alone can be made with one split conjugate pair and nontrivial inertia of arbitrarily large order. The joint Selmer and theta estimates for the same choices remain open here. The original revision is **40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005**. ## The splitting field and its inertia

Write \(E=D.\mathrm{host}.E\), \(c\) for CM conjugation, \(T\) for the finite places over \(\ell\), and \(R=\mathcal O_{E,T}\). The source's designated places \(V=X.pD(i)\) lie away from \(T\): the underlying rational prime is the first of two distinct primes unless it equals \(\ell\), in which case it is the second. Choose fractional ideals \(B_j\) of \(R\) giving independent invariant-factor generators of its finite ideal class group, of respective orders \(d_j\). We may take them relatively prime to all places in \(V\), by weak approximation applied to their valuations there. Choose \(\alpha_j\in E^\times\) with \(B_j^{d_j}=\alpha_jR\). The \(\alpha_j\) are units at all places in \(V\). Choose a finite generating set \(u_a\) for \(R^\times\), including generators of the torsion. Its finite generation follows already from Dirichlet's unit theorem and the valuation map into \(\mathbb Z^T\) with kernel \(\mathcal O_E^\times\). The class group is a quotient of the ordinary finite ideal class group. [Source choice of level prime](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/SharpRestriction/LevelLetterCocycles/DataN.lean#L309-L319) · [Distinct designated primes](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/LocalRingCardinality/DataHost.lean#L76-L84)

Let \(L/E\) be the splitting field of \(X^{2N}-1\), all \(X^N-u_a\) and all \(X^N-\alpha_j\). At each \(v\in V\), the prime-to-residue-characteristic cyclotomic extension suffices. When \(N\) is odd, \(\mu_{2N}=\langle-1,\mu_N\rangle\), including at residue characteristic two. Each other polynomial has unit constant term and exponent \(N\) invertible at \(v\). Over the maximal unramified local extension its reduction splits with simple roots and Hensel's lemma lifts every root. Thus \(L/E\) is unramified at **every** \(v\in V\). Put \(H=G_L\subset G_E\), open and normal. It contains the inertia at every prolongation of these places. Let \(S\) be a finite conjugation-stable set containing \(T,V,X.S0(i)\) and the supports of the \(B_j\) and \(\alpha_j\). For any \(P\notin S\) with \(\mathrm{Dec}_P\subseteq H\), \(P\) splits completely in \(L/E\). Hence \(N\mid |k(P)^\times|\) and every \(u_a,\alpha_j\) reduces to an \(N\)-th power at \(P\). The field \(E/\mathbb Q\) may be non-Galois.

## A primitive character at one place

Take any such \(P\) with \(cP\ne P\). Choose a surjection \(\lambda:k(P)^\times\to C_N=\mathbb Z/N\mathbb Z\), written multiplicatively. On the group of principal fractional \(R\)-ideals prime to \(P\), send \(xR\) to \(\lambda(\bar x)\), where \(x\) is a unit in \(E_P\). The ambiguity in the generator is a \(T\)-unit whose residue is an \(N\)-th power. The same observation gives value 1 on \(\alpha_jR\). Every fractional \(R\)-ideal prime to \(P\) has the form \(xR\prod B_j^{m_j}\) with \(v_P(x)=0\). Send this expression to \(\lambda(\bar x)\). In two expressions for the same ideal each exponent difference is divisible by \(d_j\), independently for every \(j\). The corresponding quotient of principal generators is consequently a product of powers of the \(\alpha_j\) and a \(T\)-unit. All these elements have value 1. This gives a homomorphism with values in the **same** \(C_N\), even if \(\ell\mid d_j\). Simply extending to the divisible circle group would lose control over that exponent.

Pull this ideal character back to ordinary ideals prime to \(P\) by localization. It kills every principal ideal with generator congruent to 1 at \(P\), so factors through the ray class group of modulus \(P\). The CM field has no real places. By finite global Artin reciprocity and existence of the ray class field it defines a continuous Galois character \(\theta_P:G_E\to C_N\). Its inertia away from \(P\) is trivial. At \(P\) the local unit character on the residue group is \(\lambda\) or its inverse according to reciprocity convention: the product formula on principal ideles gives this by lifting any nonzero residue to an element of \(E\) of valuation zero. Its inertia image is therefore all of \(C_N\). At each place of \(T\), localization also kills the prime ideal itself. The unramified Frobenius thus has value 1, giving triviality on the **whole decomposition group**. [Milne, *Class Field Theory*, v4.03, Ch. V §§1, 3, 5](https://www.jmilne.org/math/CourseNotes/CFT.pdf)

Set \(d_P(g)=\theta_P(g)/\theta_P(cgc^{-1})\), using the source's lift of complex conjugation to the algebraic closure. The chosen complex embedding identifies its square with the identity and its restriction to \(E\) with CM conjugation. Thus \(d_P(cgc^{-1})=d_P(g)^{-1}\). On inertia at \(P\) the second factor is unramified, so the full order \(N\) survives. On inertia at \(cP\) the second factor supplies all of \(C_N\). The character is unramified elsewhere and trivial on decomposition over \(T=cT\). Different prolongations conjugate inertia and decomposition inside \(G_E\), without affecting an abelian character. This includes \(\ell=2\) without taking a square root or dividing by two. The archive's split-place witness is equivalent to \(cP\ne P\): an integral element in \(P\setminus cP\) gives the witness, and the two ideals are maximal. Choosing these \(d_P\) for *every* allowed \(P\) and the trivial character for the other places gives **AdmFam**, hence **K1** for every twist index \(i\) and every \(M\ge1\). ## Two families of Frobenius test places

Fix any open normal \(J\subset G_E\), any \(z\) in inertia at a designated place \(v\in V\), a finite forbidden set \(A\), and an integer \(r\ge0\). Write \(F/E\) for the finite Galois extension fixed by \(J\cap H\). Chebotarev over \(E\) supplies \(r\) distinct places \(P_b\) unramified in \(F\) whose Frobenius belongs to the class of \(z|_F\). At each choice exclude all forbidden places and their conjugates, all previously chosen rational primes, all primes ramified in \(E/\mathbb Q\), and \(S\). We may require absolute residue degree one: the contribution of degree at least two to the Dirichlet density is zero, since the sum of their inverse norms converges. These degree-one places have completion \(\mathbb Q_p\), so cannot be stabilized by \(c\): the induced continuous automorphism on \(\mathbb Q_p\) would be identity on \(E\). Since \(z\in H\), they split in \(L\); in particular their norm is \(1\bmod 2N\). Every choice of arithmetic Frobenius at the source prolongation is conjugate to \(z\bmod J\). [Sutherland, *Global class field theory, the Chebotarev density theorem*, lecture 28](https://math.mit.edu/classes/18.785/2021fa/LectureNotes28.pdf)

Let \(B/E\) be cut out by the tuple of left characters \((d_{P_b})_b\). At \(P_b\), the image of inertia in the tuple is precisely the \(b\)-th coordinate subgroup, since the conjugate pairs are disjoint. Those subgroups generate \(C_N^r\), proving surjectivity of the tuple. Moreover \(B\cap F=E\): \(F\) is unramified at every \(P_b\), so the quotient corresponding to \(B\cap F\) kills each of the generating inertia subgroups. Consequently the joint Galois group over \(E\) is the direct product. Applying Chebotarev to the element \((e_a,1)\in C_N^r\times\mathrm{Gal}(F/E)\) gives distinct right places \(P'_a\), with the same degree, congruence and avoidance conditions, splitting completely in \(F\). Their Frobenius coordinates under the left characters are \(d_{P_b}(\mathrm{Frob}_{P'_a})=\delta_{ab}\pmod N\), independently of prolongation. The right characters also exist by **AdmFam**. Together these places satisfy **SlopeTestRemainingChoices** and **Steps** for \(J,z\). This realizes the identity matrix required in **Steps** without assuming disjointness of auxiliary fields in advance. The construction gives no prime-size bound uniform in the auxiliary inputs.

## Return to the tame term and formal scope

For \(\varepsilon>0\) choose \(M\ge1\) with \(2t/\varphi(\ell^M)\le\varepsilon\). A single place \(P\) of absolute residue degree one splitting completely in \(L\), outside the forbidden sets, suffices here. Identify \(C_N\) with the subgroup generated by the specified primitive root \(X.zk(i,M)\). The universal property **art_univ** applied to the finite, anticyclotomic image gives \(\psi':X.\mathrm{RCG}(i,Q)\to(X.\mathrm{Ok}(i,M))^\times\), \(Q=\{P,cP\}\), inducing exactly \(d_P\). Thus all the requirements of **PsiOk** hold. Both inertia images have order \(N\); the normalized infimum at each is \(1/\varphi(N)\), attaining the desired **partD** bound before any rationality-field tie. One can force triviality on any extra finite set of decomposition groups for this single-pair result by enlarging \(T\) conjugation-stably before forming its unit group. That enlargement need not preserve the assertion that \(L\) is unramified at \(V\); the proof of **K1** above uses \(T\) equal to the places over \(\ell\). For a finite check take \(E=\mathbb Q(i)\) and \(T\) the place over 2. At \(p=257\), choose \(i\mapsto16\) at \(P\). Both unit generators \(16\) and \(17\) have \(32\)-nd power 1 modulo \(257\), so \(x\mapsto x^{32}\) annihilates their residues and has image of order eight. The conjugate ray character on a principal ideal generated by \(a+bi\), prime to \(257\), is the \(32\)-nd power of \((a+16b)/(a-16b)\) modulo \(257\), up to global inversion. Either local residue can vary independently by the Chinese remainder theorem. This gives inertia orders \(8,8\) and tame cost \(t/2\) for the coefficient prime \(2\).

The archive proves **exists_admissible_family** assuming **RayCharacterArtin**. It enlarges the allowed support until every ideal has a generator away from that support, then takes a Kummer group for units and antisymmetrizes the resulting ray characters. Here the localization is fixed at the coefficient prime, and the finite list of principal invariant-factor relations replaces that enlargement. Both arguments use class field theory and the same antisymmetrization. The archive's **psiOk_partD** deduces a bound from an auxiliary family, exponents and a specific character factorization through **delU**. Here **K1**, the two prime lists and the isolated tame estimate follow from ray class theory and Chebotarev. Agreement with the independently specified coordinates **IsCoord** for a supplied **delU**, the Selmer determinant **OrdHyp**, the theta evaluation **partC**, and the common rationality-field tie require further work. In particular generating the test Frobenius matrix determines no coefficient of that Selmer determinant. For \(T\)-unit residues alone a class-relation obstruction can already occur: the nontrivial character on \(2\mathbb Z/4\mathbb Z\) does not extend to a character of \(\mathbb Z/4\mathbb Z\) valued in \(C_2\). Killing the principal class relations in the splitting field addresses precisely this finite-exponent obstruction.

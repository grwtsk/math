# Conditioning of the retained divisor-sum samples

Local source attribution: grwtsk contributors. The integer-recurrence proof in Joseph M. Shunia's Lemma 3.4 uses the inverse of a rational moment matrix at distinct nodes $x_i=1/q_i$. The $q_i$ are full prime powers in the retained block. The local argument works for arbitrary distinct positive integers $q_i\le B$ and positive weights with reciprocals $p_i\le B$. [Lean source](ReciprocalVandermondeBound.lean) · [Shunia, equation (3.9)](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.34242v1#S3.SS3)

For $a<b\le B$, $|(1/a-1/b)^{-1}|=ab/(b-a)\le B^2$. Multiply an interpolation polynomial by the linear factor $(X-x_j)/(x_i-x_j)$. The sum of absolute coefficients increases by at most $(1+|x_j|)/|x_i-x_j|\le2B^2$. The same argument bounds every truncated sum because multiplication by $X$ shifts coefficients by one. A column of the inverse Vandermonde matrix is the coefficient vector of a fundamental Lagrange polynomial, a product of $\ell-1$ such factors. Its absolute column sum is at most $M=(2B^2)^{\ell-1}$. The identity follows by evaluating each column at every original node. Distinctness is essential. At a repeated node the two evaluation rows coincide.

With nodes as rows in the Vandermonde matrix $V$, the retained matrix is $V^{\mathsf T} D V$ for $D_{ii}=p_i^{-1}x_i^{J+1}$. Each row sum of $V^{-1}$ is at most $\ell M$, each row sum of its transpose at most $M$, and $D^{-1}_{ii}=p_i q_i^{J+1}\le B^{J+2}$. Matrix multiplication and the triangle inequality therefore give $\ell M^2 B^{J+2}$ as the uniform inverse row bound used by the recovery theorem. This proof keeps the factor $B^J$ in a single diagonal matrix, which permits its cancellation against the sample error. A finite check independently inverted 972 rational moment matrices by Gauss–Jordan elimination, covering every set of one to four distinct nodes drawn from $1/2,\ldots,1/9$, two weight assignments and three exponents. Every inverse row satisfied the stated bound. A repeated-node example was singular.

The final four-module fresh-kernel replay passed on the pinned root environment, covering the reciprocal-node inverse estimate, the rational recurrence, existence of a successful order and executable full recovery. It checked 94 safe declarations and 21,898 transitive constants. One compiler-generated recursive execution helper was excluded as a seed.

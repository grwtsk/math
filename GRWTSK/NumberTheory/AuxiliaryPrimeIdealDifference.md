# Extracting the rational auxiliary prime

Local source attribution: grwtsk contributors. The mathematical source is OpenAI's *Deterministic Polynomial Factorization over Prime Fields*, October 4, 2026, Proposition `analytic:auxiliary`, at revision `3344da59991ed94f9ba711217b29c1d760badfa6`. The proof concerns the field extension $K=\mathbf Q(\mu_{12q})\subset M=K(p^{1/q})$ with $q$ prime in the paper's large-characteristic branch. [Exact analytic argument, blob `21081c288d68760167d2d94a34247ba61240dddf`](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/3344da59991ed94f9ba711217b29c1d760badfa6/preprints/Deterministic-Polynomial-Factorization-over-Prime-Fields-October-4-2026/build/sections/10-analytic.tex)

For an integer scale $x\ge2$, the local sums use pairs consisting of an actual nonzero prime ideal $P$ of the ring of integers of the field and a power $j\ge1$ with $x<(\mathrm NP)^j<2x$. The weight is $\log(\mathrm NP)\,\varphi((\mathrm NP)^j/x)$. Compact support of $\varphi$ in $(1,2)$ is what makes this finite sum correspond to the paper's smoothed sum. The proof builds a finset using Mathlib's bounded-norm finiteness; each exponent in the window satisfies $j<(\mathrm NP)^j<2x$. The theorem `mem_window` checks that the finite enumeration omits no such pair. The source chooses $x=T B^{20000000}$ and can increase the absolute scale constant $T$ to an integer. [Local definitions and proof](AuxiliaryPrimeIdealDifference.lean)

The ordinary terms on the $K$ side have $j=1$, prime norm $\ell$, and $\ell\notin\{2,3,p,q\}$. Thus $\ell$ is the underlying degree-one rational prime. The nuisance mass is the sum over **every** other term. It includes higher prime powers and prime ideals of larger residue degree. The local cyclotomic premise supplies $\ell\equiv1\pmod {12q}$ on ordinary terms. If $p^{(\ell-1)/q}=1\pmod\ell$, the local splitting premise gives at least $q$ distinct prime ideals $Q$ of $M$ in the window with $Q\cap \mathcal O_K=P$ and $\mathrm NQ=\ell$. These are the first-power contributions forced by complete splitting in the paper.

Every such $Q$ has the same weight as $P$. A prime ideal of $M$ contracts to a unique ideal of $K$, so different ordinary $P$'s use disjoint sets of $Q$'s. If all ordinary primes fail the auxiliary test, summing those fibers gives $q$ times the entire ordinary mass of $K$ at most the entire mass of $M$. Nonnegativity of $\varphi$ justifies the final inequality. Consequently
$$
 \Psi_K(x)-\Psi_M(x)/q
 \;\le\; \sum_{\text{nuisance terms of }K}\log(\mathrm NP)\,\varphi((\mathrm NP)^j/x).
$$
A strict excess over this bound produces an actual rational prime $x<\ell<2x$ satisfying every condition of `AuxiliaryPrime p q l`. A non-strict comparison cannot force existence: the identically zero test function makes both sides vanish at any scale. The result yields the requisite numerical cap $2x$ for the [certified finite search](PrimeFieldAuxiliarySearch.md).

For distinct primes $p,\ell$ with $p$ odd, suppose $\ell\equiv1\bmod24$ and $p^{(\ell-1)/2}\ne1$ in $\mathbf F_\ell$. Euler's criterion implies that $p$ is a nonsquare modulo $\ell$. Since $\ell\equiv1\bmod4$, the reciprocity sign is positive and $\ell$ is a nonsquare modulo $p$. Hence $z=\ell\bmod p$ is a nonzero representative below $p$ with Legendre symbol $-1$. If the supplier gives $\ell\le E$ then $z\le E$ by the elementary remainder bound. The condition $\ell\equiv1\bmod4$ matters: $3$ is a nonsquare modulo $7$, but $7$ is a square modulo $3$.

For the complementary primes, exhaustive search through $p$ costs polynomially in $B$ under that branch condition. This yields a conditional uniform search route; the number-field and analytic suppliers and fixed bit-step program remain unqualified.

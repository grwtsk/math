## A. The exact source and the previous local quartic

F(s)=(s-1)zeta(s) is entire with F(1)=1 by analytic continuation.
xi(s)=Gamma(1+s/2) pi^(-s/2) F(s). The factor is holomorphic and nonzero
on Re(s)>-2, covering every domain in this contribution. With J(s)=1-conj(s),
xi(J(s))=conj(xi(s)). This transports zero sets with multiplicity. It does
not transport numerical F-moduli without the normalizer's cost.

The inherited finite stack E_(N,m) retains modes k=1,...,N-1, the N^(1-s)
completion, the half-endpoint term, and all m Bernoulli corrections:
 E=(s-1)sum_(k<N)k^(-s)+N^(-s){N+(s-1)/2
   +(s-1)sum_(j=1..m)B_(2j)/(2j)! (s)_(2j-1)N^(1-2j)}.
The exact source remainder on Re(s)>1-2m is
 F-E=-(s-1)(s)_(2m)/(2m)! integral_N^infty Btilde_(2m)(x)x^(-s-2m)dx.
The periodic Bernoulli norm divided by (2m)! is less than 4/6^(2m).

At c, the complete stack has Taylor coefficients a_k in outward complex
rational intervals, through degree d=24 on an independently bounded outer
disk of radius R=1/2. For r<R,
 tail_d(r)=epsilon_EM+M_E(r/R)^(d+1)/(1-r/R).
Let alpha_0, alpha_1, alpha_2 be rational midpoints, and eta_k bound their
complex modulus errors using the sum of real/imaginary half widths. Define

Then |F(c+z)-p(z)|<=E_r for every |z|<=r. This is a whole-disk allowance,
not an estimate only at the selected points. It retains coefficient,
finite-Taylor, analytic-Taylor and Euler--Maclaurin errors separately.

Consequently every zero in a tested rectangle of L1 radius r is in

## B. Discriminant-controlled conversion of a quartic candidate to disks

Let p(z)=Az^2+Bz+C, A!=0. Write Delta=B^2-4AC, let r_+,r_- be its two
roots, and assume
                         |Delta| > 4 |A| E.                 (ZI1)
Set eps=E/|A| and d=|r_+-r_-|=sqrt(|Delta|)/|A|. Thus eps<d^2/4.
If |p(z)|<=E, then |z-r_+||z-r_-|<=eps. Let t be the nearer distance.
First t^2<=eps, so t<d/2. The other distance is at least d-t; hence
 t(d-t)<=eps.
On [0,d/2], t(d-t) increases. Solving its quadratic inequality proves

 t <= (d-sqrt(d^2-4eps))/2
    = 2eps/(d+sqrt(d^2-4eps))
    <= 2E/sqrt(|Delta|).                                  (ZI2)

The exact Cassini sublevel set therefore lies in two disjoint disks with
the first radius in ZI2. This changes a quartic membership restriction into
explicit root neighborhoods WITHOUT changing F or projecting roots.

The rational implementation takes a lower enclosure Dlo<=|Delta| and an
upper enclosure Aup>=|A|, requires Dlo>4 Aup E, and uses 2E/sqrt(Dlo) with
an outward denominator bound. Proposed complex roots of p are rational
midpoints only. A separate polynomial Rouche test proves a root within
eta=2^-72 of EACH proposed center. These eta-disks are checked disjoint;
since p has degree two, they identify both polynomial roots completely.
Adding eta and rounding the necessary radius UP gives the stored disks.
The enlarged stored disks need not inherit the exact Cassini disjointness;
the code explicitly checks their disjointness and does not sum overlapping
target counts. Each target count has its own independent domain check.

For a proposed offset z0 and radius h, the separate target test is

 |p'(z0)| h > |p(z0)| + |A| h^2 + E.                      (ZI3)

Provided the ENTIRE closed disk is inside |z|<=r, Rouche compares F(c+z)
with p'(z0)(z-z0), giving exactly one simple zero in the disk and no zero
on its boundary. A positive ZI3 without the containment check cannot be
used: the error E was only proved on its declared domain.

The distant second root of a local quadratic is often outside that domain.
An exact lower bound on |z0| exceeding r+h proves its stored disk disjoint
from the parent. That branch is excluded from the local candidate set; it
is NOT a second zeta zero. The tests include a quadratic with two polynomial
roots but only one valid target disk.

At |Delta|<=4|A|E this splitting rule declines to decide. Neither a root
collision nor off-line zeros follow from that failure. At Delta=0 the
square-root conditioning loss is real, and a cluster certificate or a finer
approximation is necessary. Likewise, A=0 takes an unresolved linear-
degeneracy exit instead of dividing by zero.

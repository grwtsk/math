This package translates the three-channel waveform/screw/quartic architecture into
versioned mathematical objects. Its new exact core is the primitive additive-to-
multiplicative transfer for coprime positive triples a+b=c. At finite cutoff X,
the ordinary and primitive Tornheim sums are related by gcd scaling and Möbius
inversion with every floor retained. The finite primitive projection has trace
sum_{c=2}^X phi(c). Complete three-channel energies retain all three mixed terms.
The full additive lattice has Z^2 semidirect C2 symmetry; D_infinity is only the
fixed-total difference fiber.

The finite exact producer uses only integers and fractions. Candidate Lean modules
are inert .lean.txt evidence and have not been compiled. The K-theory report does
not claim a Fredholm module or arithmetic index pairing. The safe-domain
holomorphic estimate is for a Mordell-Tornheim source in absolute convergence,
not for zeta in the critical strip.

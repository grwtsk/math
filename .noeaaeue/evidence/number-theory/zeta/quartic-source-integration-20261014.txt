1. Source-to-moment transport (written analysis)

Let f and P be holomorphic on a neighborhood of the closed disk D(c,h), h>0.
Assume, on the ENTIRE boundary,

 |f-P| <= epsilon < mu <= |P|, epsilon>=0.

Rouche gives equal finite interior zero counts N, with analytic multiplicity.
For normalized powers u=(z-c)/h define S_k by summing u(rho)^k over those
interior zeros. Only interior polynomial roots are used. Then

 S_0(f)=S_0(P)=N,
 |S_k(f)-S_k(P)| <= k*epsilon/(mu-epsilon), k>=1.           (1)

Proof. On a collar of the contour the power-series branch
L=log(f/P)=log(1+(f-P)/P) is single-valued. It need not extend through interior
zeros. Its boundary modulus is at most -log(1-epsilon/mu), which is at most
epsilon/(mu-epsilon). The difference of the residue formulas is
(2*pi*i)^(-1) integral u^k dL. Integration by parts on the CLOSED contour
gives -k/(2*pi*i) integral_(|u|=1) u^(k-1)L du and proves (1). No numerical
value or bound for f'/f is used. The single-valued branch accounts for winding.
For an analytic weight psi the same calculation gives

 |sum_f psi(rho)-sum_P psi(rho)|
       <= h*sup_boundary|psi'|*epsilon/(mu-epsilon).        (2)

Suppose the actual normalized source divisor is conjugation-stable. Its
moments are real. Put H_f=(S_(i+j)(f))_(0<=i,j<N), and use the REAL parts
of P's interior moments for K_P. Equation (1) gives entry error at most
(i+j)*theta, theta=epsilon/(mu-epsilon), so

 ||H_f-K_P||_(2->2) <= (3/2)*N*(N-1)*theta.                (3)

Indeed the largest absolute row sum is bounded by
N*(N-1)+N*(N-1)/2. For N=4 the allowance is 18*theta.
A paid lower bound K_P >= (18*theta+delta)I implies H_f>=delta I.
Reality is supplied by the actual divisor symmetry, not by rounding imaginary
parts to zero. These continuum implications are written, not Lean-checked.

2. General even-quartic coefficient consumer (written algebra)

For P(X)=X^4+pX^2+r with p,r real, let G=p^2-4r and let H(p,r) be the
explicit Newton-sum Hermite matrix in the archived QuarticHermite candidate.
Then

 H(p,r) >= 0  iff  p<=0, r>=0, G>=0;
 H(p,r) >  0  iff  p< 0, r> 0, G> 0.                      (4)

Permuting indices to (0,2,1,3) gives parity blocks
 [[4,-2p],[-2p,2p^2-4r]],
 [[-2p,2p^2-4r],[2p^2-4r,-2p^3+6pr]],
with determinants 4G and 4rG. Necessity follows from principal minors;
if G=0, r=p^2/4 already. Strict positivity forces all strict signs.
For sufficiency at p<0, write q(u)=u^T H u. Direct expansion gives

 (-2p)q(u) = -8p(u0-p*u2/2)^2 -2pG*u2^2
             +[-2p*u1+(2p^2-4r)*u3]^2 +4rG*u3^2.         (5)

All terms have nonnegative coefficients under (4), and -2p>0. With strict
signs, equality forces all coordinates to vanish. At p=0 the conditions
force r=0 and q(u)=4u0^2. No division by zero is made.
For a GENERAL divisor r>=0 is necessary: p=-1,r=-1 has G=5>0 but odd
minor 4rG=-20. For an orbit r=(a^2+b^2)^2 it is automatic.
The test suite reads the hash-bound literal arithmetic body of `def value`
from the candidate patch and verifies (5) against it. This limited arithmetic
reader is not a Lean parser, elaborator, or proof-term extractor.

3. Actual finite producer and its unchanged scope

The source is the entire regularization F(s)=(s-1)zeta(s), F(1)=1 by analytic
continuation. The four existing Python modules supplying its compensated
Euler--Maclaurin jets, quadratic errors, root localization and exact intervals
are unchanged and hash-pinned by the replay command.
Use centers c=1/2+i*b with b in
 113/8, 21, 25, 487/16, 527/16, 601/16,
radius h=1/16, and spectral coordinate w=-i(s-1/2)/40.
For the complete quadratic p(z)=Az^2+Bz+C, the producer independently proves

 mu=|B|_lower*h-|C|_upper-|A|_upper*h^2 > epsilon.

Comparison with Bz gives one interior polynomial root, then Rouche gives one
interior source root. Its polynomial reference is enclosed separately at
radius eta=2^-72. A second quadratic root outside the parent domain is not
included. The complete k-th spectral-moment allowance is

 k*((b+h)/40)^(k-1)*(h*epsilon/(mu-epsilon)+eta)/40.

Pair two positive disks with their negative-height conjugates. The exact
nonvanishing completion factor xi(s)=Gamma(1+s/2)*pi^(-s/2)*F(s) transports
zero multiplicities and symmetry. It does NOT give F a same-modulus reflection
law. The four-zero divisor has m0=4, odd moments zero, and real even moments.
Newton identities yield p=-m2/2 and r=m2^2/8-m4/4. The consumer widens p,r
outward to 64-bit dyadic boxes and tests the WHOLE boxes using
 p.hi<0, r.lo>0, lower(p^2-4r)>0.
All three saved examples pass. They concern the same twelve zeros already
localized through height 40, not a new region or independent analytic replay
of the full atlas. Source disks and moments are recomputed, not accepted from
self-declared status flags or from a table of zero ordinates.

A counted divisor containing FOUR DISTINCT zeros is NOT the orbit polynomial
constructed from one hypothetical zero and four symmetry slots. The latter
has G=-16a^2b^2 and repeated slots on the axis. Our selected divisor has G>0.
The source identification of these two different polynomials is never asserted.
Likewise sum q(root)^2 is not replaced by sum |q(root)|^2.

 python3 -B scripts/replay_quartic_source.py --output-dir /tmp/quartic-source-run

The raw audit contains #print axioms requests. Counting those requests is NOT

1. Exact source, without discarding prime powers
------------------------------------------------
Retain the original completed xi and the repository discrepancy
 Delta(x)=psi(x)-x+1, psi(x)=sum_(p^j<=x)log p, x>=1.
Write theta(x)=sum_(p<=x)log p, and let
 P2(x)=theta(sqrt(x)), P3(x)=sum_(j>=3)theta(x^(1/j)), P(x)=P2(x)+P3(x).
Each sum is finite at fixed x. Prime bases are prime and exponents are exact;
for example p^6 occurs in j=6, not also as a prime cube. Then psi=theta+P.
All initial values theta(1),P2(1),P3(1) are zero.

For any of these functions F define
 m_k(F)=integral_0^infinity F(exp t)e^(-3t/2)L_k(2t)dt,
 m_k^X(F)=the same integral over [0,log X], X>=1.
Every fixed-k integral used below converges absolutely by elementary bounds.
The original u_k=m_k(Delta), D_N=sum_(k<N)|u_k|^2 are unchanged.

Define the compensated PRIMARY-PRIME discrepancy and exact remainder
 H(x)=theta(x)-x+sqrt(x),
 R(x)=P(x)-sqrt(x)+1=[theta(sqrt(x))-sqrt(x)]+P3(x)+1.
Then H(1)=R(1)=0 and

 Delta=H+R, u_k=v_k+r_k, v_k=m_k(H), r_k=m_k(R).            (1.1)

Here r is a new proper-power remainder, NOT the gamma jet, the fractional
mesh error, or the earlier centered Mobius pickup residual. The original
source coefficients are not redefined. Both (1.1) and its finite-X version
retain their mixed energy 2 Re sum v_k conjugate(r_k).

2. Elementary bounds before importing PNT
------------------------------------------
For all y>=1, theta(y)<=3y. Indeed primes m<p<=2m divide binom(2m,m), so
 theta(2^j)-theta(2^(j-1))<=2^j log 2.
Sum these inequalities and choose 2^(J-1)<y<=2^J. Then
 theta(y)<4y log 2<3y. Values y<2 are immediate. The rational comparisons
2/3<log 2<3/4 follow from the atanh series and its positive geometric tail.

For the square input f2(t)=e^(-t/2)P2(e^t), 0<=f2<=3. The companion bounded-
input Laguerre theorem gives, for every N and every cutoff X,

 sum_(k<N)|m_k(P2)|^2<=36N, sum_(k<N)|m_k^X(P2)|^2<=36N.   (2.1)

For j>=3 put f_j(t)=e^(-t/2)theta(e^(t/j)). It is zero for t<j log 2.
The same Chebyshev bound gives
 ||f_j||_2^2 <=9*2^(-(j-2))/(1-2/j)<=27*2^(-(j-2)).
Minkowski and the convergent geometric series prove
 ||sum_(j>=3)f_j||_2 <=3sqrt(3)(sqrt(2)+1),
 ||sum_(j>=3)f_j||_2^2 <162.
The nonnegative partial sums agree pointwise eventually with e^(-t/2)P3(e^t);
Minkowski/Fatou identify their L2 limit with that function. Bessel gives

 sum_(k>=0)|m_k(P3)|^2 <81.                                (2.2)

The same bound holds for all finite-X projected prefixes. Thus even without
a PNT import the proper-power vector costs at most 6sqrt(N)+9 in l2, and
its energy is at most 225N for N>=1. These are actual-source bounds, not
random-label assumptions or a consequence of a generic Gram sign.

3. An explicit square-error estimate from a published PNT bound
----------------------------------------------------------------
The external input is Johnston--Yang, arXiv:2204.01980v2, Corollary 1.2 and
the first row of Table 1 (printed pages 2 and 3): for every y>=2,

 |theta(y)-y|/y <=9.40 (log y)^1.515 exp(-0.8274 sqrt(log y)). (3.1)

The 9.40 is A+0.01, with A=9.39. Theorem 1.1's 9.39 belongs to PSI and is
not silently assigned to theta. Both pages and the table were visually
inspected. This is an established unconditional estimate, but its proof,
upstream finite-height computations, and formalization were not replayed.

Let b(t)=e^(-t/2)[theta(e^(t/2))-e^(t/2)]. For all t>=0, |b(t)|<=2 by
0<=theta(y)<=3y. Put u=t/2. On u>=256, (3.1) implies
 |b(2u)|<=10 u^2 exp(-(4/5)sqrt(u)).
The powers and constants have all been weakened in the upper-bound direction.
Consequently, writing a=8/5,

 ||b||_2^2 <=2048+400 integral_16^infinity z^9 e^(-az) dz
 =2048+400 exp(-128/5) sum_(j=0)^9 (9!/j!)16^j/a^(10-j)
 <2250.                                                    (3.2)

The last strict inequality is exact rational: bound exp(-128/5) above by
the reciprocal of sum_(j=0)^128 (128/5)^j/j!. The resulting rational upper
bound is approximately 2244.430281847384; decimals are not the acceptance test.
There is no large finite prime enumeration in (3.2).

4. Uniform H2 reconstruction, with the critical carrier retained
-----------------------------------------------------------------
The normalized input for R is b+sum_(j>=3)f_j+e^(-t/2).
Bessel gives squared coefficient norms below 1125, 81, and 1/2 respectively.
Their triangle bound is smaller than 34+9+1=44 in norm. Thus

 sum_(k>=0)|r_k|^2 <1936<2000.                              (4.1)

We use the round upper bound 2000 as the contract. It is independent of N
and X; truncation of the three input functions preserves the same bound.
The repeated number 1936 here has no asserted relation to any other manuscript
constant. Equation (4.1) defines an actual H2 function Rcal(w)=sum r_k w^k
on the disk, in the convention ||Rcal||_H2^2=sum|r_k|^2.

The critical carrier is q(x)=sqrt(x)-1. Its coefficients are EXACTLY

 kappa_k=(-1)^k-(2/3)(-1/3)^k,
 kappa(w)=1/(1+w)-2/(3+w)=(1-w)/[(1+w)(3+w)],
 sum_(k<N)kappa_k^2=N-3/2+2*3^(-N)-(1/2)*9^(-N)=:K_N.      (4.2)

The Laplace integral of L_k(2t) at rate lambda>0 equals
(lambda-2)^k/lambda^(k+1); expanding the finite polynomial proves this.
The carrier is NOT square summable. Its disk pole is at w=-1, corresponding
to the boundary s=1/2, not a nontrivial zeta zero or a Hilbert-space harmonic
mass. No Hodge or Tao time is identified with this bookkeeping coordinate.

The full proper-power coefficient sequence equals kappa+r. In particular,
for W_N=sum_(k<N)|m_k(P)|^2,

 |sqrt(W_N)-sqrt(K_N)|<sqrt(2000),
 |W_N-N| <=2sqrt(2000N)+2002,
 W_N/N ->1.                                                (4.3)

This is an unconditional all-depth asymptotic for the proper-power COMPONENT,
not for the full discrepancy energy D_N. The genuine cancellation of its
critical carrier against the primary-prime contribution must remain.

Let V_N=sum_(k<N)|v_k|^2 for H in Section 1. From (1.1) and (4.1),

 |sqrt(D_N)-sqrt(V_N)|<sqrt(2000),
 D_N<=2V_N+4000, V_N<=2D_N+4000.                            (4.4)

Therefore subexponential (or polynomial) upper growth of one is equivalent to
that of the other. An additive norm error does NOT give a bounded difference
of energies: the full cross term remains. This construction does not prove
such growth for either sequence. The repository's scalar/logarithmic-
derivative return consumes that missing bound and retains the original xi.

For clarity, let a(w)=xi'(s(w))/xi(s(w)), s(w)=(3+w)/(2(1-w)). The preceding
repository identity is a=r_gamma-(3+w)U_Delta/2. Thus this update gives
 a=r_gamma-(3+w)V/2+E, E=-(3+w)Rcal/2, ||E||_H2^2<8000.
It holds first as an identity of germs. The complete error E is holomorphic on
the disk. It cannot cancel any nonremovable pole of V inside that disk.
Neither V nor U_Delta is asserted holomorphic there before the growth premise.
The error's Toeplitz/Hankel finite-section norms are at most sqrt(8000N), by
the full Frobenius sum and coefficient multiplicities at most N. This is a
polynomial reconstruction price, not an estimate for the retained prime matrix.

5. Uniform-in-depth approximation of the remainder itself
-----------------------------------------------------------
Define r_k^X=m_k^X(R). For R0 an integer >=1 take X=2^(3R0^2), so
T=log X>=2R0^2 by log2>2/3. The PNT square tail from (3.2) satisfies
 integral_T^infinity |b(t)|^2dt
 <=400 exp(-8R0/5) sum_(j=0)^9(9!/j!)R0^j/(8/5)^(10-j).
For R0=1 the relevant u>=1 suffices for the same majorant.
Also 0<=e^(-t/2)P3(e^t)<=(9/2)t exp(-t/6), since there are at most
 t/log2 exponents and each j>=3 contributes at most 3exp(-t/6).
Its squared tail at T0=2R0^2 is bounded by
 (81/4)exp(-T0/3)(3T0^2+18T0+54).
The remaining constant has squared tail exp(-T0). Bessel and the complete
three-term triangle inequality give

 sum_(k>=0)|r_k-r_k^X|^2 <= (3/2)[the three displayed tails]. (5.1)

The calculator replaces each exp(-z) by a rational upper bound: the minimum
of 2^(-floor(z)) and the reciprocal positive Taylor partial sum through 128.
The first alternative guarantees exponential decay for increasing schedule
index; a fixed Taylor degree alone would not give that rate. This yields
 error squared <10^-6 at X=2^3072, and <10^-25 at X=2^12288,
SIMULTANEOUSLY for every coefficient depth. Neither cutoff is enumerated.
This schedule approximates only R, not the unresolved compensated primes H.

-------------------------------------------
The remaining arithmetic target is log(1+V_N)=o(N) for the ACTUAL H=theta-x+sqrt(x).
The existing all-depth diagonal, mesh and prime-label tail controls are preserved.
The present contribution removes proper powers at a bounded coefficient-norm

counting function or a counterexample to RH. It refutes a general inference
from a fixed subunit power remainder or PNT-type decay to the retained bound.

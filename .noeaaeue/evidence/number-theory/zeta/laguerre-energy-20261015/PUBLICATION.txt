Laguerre energy package: repository publication
==============================================

The pending source, runner, tests, derivation, inputs.json, source-ledger.json
and requirements-tests.txt are installed byte for byte. They were freshly
replayed in both Python modes before publication. The 147 tests per mode and
128-moment calculations at 768/896 working bits passed; the four producer
outputs match the original prepared outputs. This is not a new all-depth bound.

The other seventeen original package payloads, including the historical
README.txt, results.json, package manifest, failure logs, receipt controls and
original compressed replay, are retained exactly inside the multipart archive
at ../laguerre-centered-atoms-20261015/. Their original paths are archive member
names rather than missing files. That directory's publication-plan.json maps
all 24 original payloads, and archive-index.json gives hashes and assembly order.
Fresh logs are separate publication-run/ members of the same archive. Historical
no-publication claims and timestamps are retained as historical statements.

Run from the repository root with requirements-tests.txt installed:

 python3 -B scripts/verify_laguerre_discrepancy_energy.py --mode both

No archive extraction is needed. Mathematical scope, publication preservation,
new centered-atom results and whole-repository/native verification limits are
recorded in ../laguerre-centered-atoms-20261015/README.txt and results.json.
No existing repository file or source PDF was changed by this publication.

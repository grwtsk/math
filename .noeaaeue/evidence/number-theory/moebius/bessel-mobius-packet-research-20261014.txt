## 1. Freeze the actual target

For n>=2 let lambda_n=n(n+7/6), nu_n=n+7/12, and let B_n(y) be the
complete Bessel reference in the preceding derivation: it includes the
parity (-1)^n, normalization (7/6)_n/n!, frequency nu_n, geometric
prefactor, and argument 2 nu_n arcsin(sqrt(y)). The actual coefficient is

 t_n = (2 lambda_n)^(-1) integral_(n^-4)^(1/n)
          y^(7/6)(1-y) g'(y) B_n'(y) dy,
 g(y)=y^(-5/12) V(y^(-1/2)),
 V(x)=2 sum_(k<=floor(x)) mu(k)(k^(-1/2)-x^(-1/2)).       (CP1)

The prime-product completion and both original endpoint sums are not
modified. The existing comparison states

 ||d-t||_(ell2(4n+7/3), n>=2) <= D
 D=sqrt(4725)+sqrt(420000)+sqrt(10500)<821.                (CP2)

Only the difference is bounded. No supplier of
 sum_(n>=2)(4n+7/3)t_n^2 < infinity
is supplied here. Research priorities are joint source estimation and
source-aware finite evaluation, not another unpriced change of basis.

## 2. Recognize each coefficient as one finite arithmetic functional

For a single source atom, in the y coordinate,

 g_k(y)=2[k^(-1/2)y^(-5/12)-y^(-1/6)] 1_(0<y<=k^-2).

The bracket is zero at y=k^-2. Thus g_k is continuous at its arrival
boundary and locally absolutely continuous away from zero. Its derivative
has no delta term and almost everywhere equals

 g_k'(y)=[-(5/6)k^(-1/2)y^(-17/12)+(1/3)y^(-7/6)]
          1_(0<y<k^-2).

Put a_n=n^-4 and b_(n,k)=min(1/n,1/k^2). Define K_(n,k)=0 when
b_(n,k)<=a_n; otherwise define

 K_(n,k)=(2 lambda_n)^(-1) integral_(a_n)^(b_(n,k))
       (1-y)[1/3-(5/(6sqrt(k)))y^(-1/4)] B_n'(y) dy.      (CP3)

Every endpoint is rational. The Bessel kernel and square roots are actual
real functions; rational endpoints do not make the integral rational.
Only finitely many source atoms are active on [a_n,1/n]. Finite linearity
therefore gives the exact identity

                  t_n=sum_(k=1..n^2) mu(k) K_(n,k).     (CP4)

No improper exchange, zeta continuation, or target energy assumption is
needed. At k=n^2 the interval degenerates, so K_(n,k)=0; its coefficient
is still in the closed inventory. At k<=sqrt(n), b_(n,k)=1/n: the entire
carried source before sqrt(n) survives. Deleting those atoms is not a
localization of the same coefficient. The code records both support cases.

## 3. Classical cubic identity with a strict support gate

Let * denote finite divisor convolution on positive integers, 1 the
constant-one arithmetic function, and epsilon its convolution identity.
For an integer U>=1 define a(k)=mu(k) when k<=U and a(k)=0 otherwise;
put b=mu-a. Since mu*1=epsilon, expanding (mu-b)^3 in
the convolution ring proves

 mu = 3a - 3(a*a*1) + (a*a*a*1*1) + (b*b*b*1*1).         (CP5)

This is the standard order-three Mobius identity, not a new cancellation
theorem. Each nonzero term of the last convolution requires three factors
at least U+1, so it vanishes coefficientwise at k<(U+1)^3.
For any K<(U+1)^3 and every 1<=k<=K,

 mu(k)=3a(k)-3(a*a*1)(k)+(a*a*a*1*1)(k).                 (CP6)

All tuples are ordered; neutral multiplier repetitions are retained.
For a supported only at one, the second branch is identically one and the
third branch is the divisor-count function, not identically one.
The gate is strict: U=1,K=8 gives reconstructed coefficient 1 at eight,
where actual mu(8)=0. A zero response at a touching horizon is not a
substitute for this coefficientwise gate.

Huxley--Watt (2018), equation (1.6), gives the general order version and
records its classical antecedents. Their coefficientwise discussion after
Theorem 1 explains why arbitrary finite test weights are then allowed.
Our proof uses only finite convolution; it does not assume multiplicativity
of K_(n,k) or import a Mangoldt Type-I theorem for the Mobius function.

## 4. One frozen input prefix for a complete degree shell

Let D0=2^j, j>=1, and D0<=n<2D0. Put

 K=(2D0-1)^2,             U=floor(K^(1/3)).               (CP7)

These are exact integer operations: U^3<=K<(U+1)^3. The code computes
the cube root by integer inequalities. It queries actual mu only at
1,...,U. No mu(k) above U is needed to compile the entire source prefix
through K. This is an arithmetic input-range statement, NOT a speed claim.
In particular U<=4^(1/3)D0^(2/3); the square-root version would use an
input prefix of order D0 on this degree shell.

Extend K_(n,k) by zero for k>n^2. Define, using this common U,

 T_(1,n)=sum_(u<=U) mu(u) K_(n,u),
 T_(2,n)=sum_(u,v<=U; uvr<=K) mu(u)mu(v) K_(n,uvr),
 T_(3,n)=sum_(u,v,w<=U; uvwrs<=K)
                         mu(u)mu(v)mu(w) K_(n,uvwrs).   (CP8)

Here r,s range over positive integers, including repeated factors.
Equations CP4 and CP6 give

                    t_n=3T_(1,n)-3T_(2,n)+T_(3,n).      (CP9)

All occurrences at the same product use the SAME exact K_(n,k), including
its support, normalization, and cutoff. Aggregate before estimating. The
cubic identity does not itself justify separate estimates for its branches.

## 5. Complete mixed energy, not three positive branch budgets

For this degree shell the exact energy is

 E_j=sum_(n=D0..2D0-1) (4n+7/3) *
       [9T1^2+9T2^2+T3^2-18T1*T2+6T1*T3-6T2*T3].       (CP10)

It equals sum (4n+7/3)t_n^2. All three mixed terms retain both orders.
Dropping them fails already at (T1,T2,T3)=(1,2,3): the complete square
is zero, whereas the three diagonal terms sum to 54.
A positive Gram representation proves E_j>=0, not a small upper bound.
The normalized coherence of only three aggregate columns is at most three
by the trace bound, but their diagonal capacity remains uncontrolled.
Consequently importing a coherence slogan without its capacity theorem
would prove no improvement.

The exact smallest remaining condition in this organization is

                         sum_(j>=1) E_j < infinity.    (CP11)

A stronger sufficient arithmetic statement is
 E_j<=C/(j+1)^(1+eta), every j>=1, C>=0, eta>0.          (CP12)

It gives sum E_j<=C(1+1/eta), and through CP2 and the existing source-exact
moment criterion,
 H_(1/3)(T)<=Q_1+(sqrt(C(1+1/eta))+D)^2
          <1+(sqrt(C(1+1/eta))+821)^2, every real T>=0.   (CP13)

The constants C and eta have NO supplied arithmetic witnesses. A uniform
bound per shell alone, without summability over shells, would not suffice.
No P04, individual-branch bound, or uniform operator bound is imposed as
an extra necessary condition. All-positive-damping closure remains a
further family of arithmetic estimates, not a consequence of a single line.

## 6. The Bessel argument is not uniformly oscillatory

For n>=4 the physical subinterval [3nu_n,n^2] is nonempty and lies inside
[sqrt(n),n^2]. Put z=2nu_n arcsin(1/x). Since
 arcsin(u)=integral_0^u (1-v^2)^(-1/2)dv
          <=u/sqrt(1-u^2), 0<=u<1,
we obtain on this subinterval

 z^2<=4nu_n^2/(x^2-1)<=4nu_n^2/(9nu_n^2-1)<=1/2.       (CP14)

The normalized Poisson integral for F_(1/6) is a cosine average against a
probability measure whose second moment is 3/7. The elementary bound
cos(zv)>=1-z^2v^2/2 yields

               F_(1/6)(z)>=1-3z^2/14>=25/28.           (CP15)

This is NOT a sign theorem for B_n', K_(n,k), or t_n. It does show that a
large-argument oscillatory approximation cannot be assigned to the entire
integration interval. Any proposed oscillatory proof must retain the
small-argument portion and its interaction with the rest. No new phase
approximation is inserted into CP3.

1. Fixed source and exact cancellation
-------------------------------------
For f in C_c^infinity(R), supp f subset [-A,A], use
 hat f(z)=integral f(u) exp(izu)du,
 C_f(x)=integral f(u+x) conjugate(f(u))du,
 h(x)=Re C_f(x), N=||f||_2^2,
 w(t)=Re digamma(1/4+it/2)-log pi.
The literal Weil explicit formula [S22, (5.15)] on f*tilde(f) is

 Q_W(f)=Arch(f)+Pole(f)-Prime(f),
 Arch=(1/(2pi)) integral_R w(t)|hat f(t)|^2 dt,
 Pole=2 Re(hat f(i/2) conjugate(hat f(-i/2))),
 Prime=2 sum_(n<=exp(2A)) Lambda(n)n^(-1/2)h(log n).             (1)

The pole product, rather than two independent squared moduli, is essential.
The source sum includes all prime powers and both signs of every correlation.
Define the actual closed-floor discrepancy

 Delta(y)=sum_(n<=y) Lambda(n)-y+1, Delta(1)=0.

For T=2A, h(T)=0 and h is C^1 (indeed smooth here). Direct Fubini gives
 Pole=4 integral_0^T cosh(x/2)h(x)dx.
Stieltjes integration, d psi(exp x)=exp(x)dx+d Delta(exp x), gives

 Q_W(f)=Arch(f)+B(f)+D(f),                                    (2)
 B(f)=2 integral_0^T exp(-x/2)h(x)dx,
 D(f)=2 integral_0^T exp(-x/2)Delta(exp x)[h'(x)-h(x)/2]dx
     =2 integral_0^T exp(x/2)h(x)dx-Prime(f).                  (3)

To verify the signs, set k(x)=exp(-x/2)h(x). Then
 integral k dDelta=[k Delta]_0^T-integral Delta k'=-integral Delta k'.
The lower endpoint is zero because Delta(1)=0; the upper because h(T)=0.
The pole term minus 2 integral exp(x/2)h is 2 integral exp(-x/2)h.
A partial interval with nonzero h at its endpoint must retain its boundary term.
These operations are on compact intervals with finitely many prime jumps; no
infinite Euler-product limit or unjustified rearrangement occurs.

B is genuinely nonnegative:
 B(f)=(1/(2pi)) integral_R |hat f(t)|^2/(t^2+1/4)dt >=0.        (4)
This uses the Fourier transform of exp(-|x|/2); it does not make D positive.
The identity is valid for complex f after taking the real autocorrelation.
The same Delta appears in the concurrent Laguerre work, but the test weight
and readout in (3) are new and must remain explicit.

The crude derivative estimate is
 |D(f)| <= J_A [2||f'||_2||f||_2+||f||_2^2],
 J_A=integral_0^(2A) exp(-x/2)|Delta(exp x)|dx.                 (5)
It follows from |h|<=N and |h'|<=||f'||_2||f||_2. This is an H^1 estimate,
not an L2 operator bound with a source-independent derivative ratio. High
modulation preserves N and can make ||f'|| arbitrarily large.

2. A rejected next supplier: the discrepancy alone has exponential negative size
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
One tempting strengthening would discard Arch except for w(t)>=-11/2 and try
to prove a subexponential L2 lower bound for D alone. This route is FALSE even
on smooth pole-null tests for the ACTUAL prime discrepancy.

Let kappa_D,0(A) be max(0, sup -D(f)/||f||^2), over nonzero smooth f supported
in [-A,A] with hat f(+-i/2)=0. Then, using the ordinary prime number theorem,

 lim_(A->infinity) log(1+kappa_D,0(A))/A = 1.                 (6)

Proof of the upper bound. By |h|<=N,
 |D(f)| <= [4(exp A-1)+P_A]N,
 P_A=2 sum_(n<=exp(2A)) Lambda(n)/sqrt n <=8A exp A.
This gives upper rate <=1, with an immaterial fixed factor at small A.

Proof of the lower bound. First fix A and a nonnegative smooth compact envelope
g, supported in [-A,A]. For f_tau(u)=exp(i tau u)g(u),
 h_tau(x)=C_g(x)cos(tau x), with C_g>=0.
The finite phase vector (exp(i tau log n)) over the prime-power inventory has
an unbounded sequence tau_j returning to the identity. For completeness, take
integer powers in a finite torus. Compactness gives a convergent subsequence
with index gaps increasing to infinity. Quotients of consecutive subsequence
points tend to the identity, with tau_j those positive gaps. No independence
of log primes or quantitative Diophantine constant is needed.
The continuous part of (3) tends to zero by the Riemann--Lebesgue lemma, while
all these finitely many cosine factors tend to one. Hence

 D(f_tau_j)/||g||^2 -> -2 sum Lambda(n)n^(-1/2)C_g(log n)/||g||^2. (7)

To impose EXACT pole zeros, instead set
 F_tau=(-D_u^2+1/4)(exp(i tau u)g(u)).
Integration by parts gives hat F_tau(+-i/2)=0, and
 F_tau/tau^2=exp(i tau u)[g-2ig'/tau+(g/4-g'')/tau^2].
This differs from f_tau in L2 by at most
 2||g'||/tau+||g/4-g''||/tau^2 ->0.
At fixed A the bound preceding (7) makes D a bounded quadratic form on L2,
so its normalized values have the same limit (7). All support and pole
constraints therefore survive the passage to the supremum.

For all A>=3 choose a fixed nonnegative smooth probability eta supported in
[-1/2,1/2], and let g_A=1_[-A+1,A-1]*eta. This is smooth, 0<=g_A<=1,
supported in [-A+1/2,A-1/2], and equals one on [-A+3/2,A-3/2]. Consequently
 ||g_A||^2<=2A,
 C_g_A(x)>=1 for 2A-5<=x<=2A-4.
With X=exp(2A-5),Y=exp(2A-4), the sum in (7), after division by N,
is at least
 (psi(Y)-psi(X))/(A sqrt Y).
The prime number theorem gives psi(Y)-psi(X)~(1-exp(-1))Y, so this is
>=c exp(A)/A for some fixed c>0 and all sufficiently large A. No explicit
numerical onset is claimed. The lower rate is >=1, proving (6).

This is NOT a negative-growth theorem for Q_W. Arch was deliberately removed
in the false route; it grows with the high modulation used in (7). The theorem
shows exactly why this channel cannot be replaced by the constant w(0) when
seeking a sharp all-test growth bound. Positive B is bounded by 4N and does
not repair that omission. The full arithmetic and archimedean interaction,
or a genuinely restricted family with its own estimates, remains necessary.

3. Actual compact test functions and a complete finite evaluator
---------------------------------------------------------------
Choose A=1/2,1,2 and define
 g_A(u)=(1-u^2/A^2)^4 for |u|<=A, zero otherwise,
 f_A=(-D^2+1/4)g_A.
These are literal compact C^1, H^2 tests, not smooth Gaussian pair isolators.
g and its first three derivatives vanish at +-A; f and f' vanish there.
Integration by parts proves the exact pole values zero. No approximate pole
value is rounded to zero by the program.

The explicit formula extends to these H^1 tests by smooth compact approximation.
For example approximate g in H^3 on a slightly larger fixed interval and
apply -D^2+1/4. Then f converges in H^1 and keeps exact pole annihilation.
The archimedean multiplier has O(log(2+|t|)) growth, so its quadratic form is
continuous for this H^1 convergence. Finite translations and pole evaluations
are continuous on fixed compact support. The resulting exact formula depends
only on f and h and is (1). This is a written extension, not a native theorem.
No source effect is inferred for every other function on the same support.

For 0<=x<=2A,
 h_A(x)=integral_-A^(A-x) f_A(u+x)f_A(u)du
is an explicitly computed rational polynomial of degree 17. Its norm N and
N1=||f_A'||^2 are exact rational integrals. In particular h(0)=N,h'(0)=0,
h(2A)=0 are algebraically checked. The polynomial is used ONLY in this interval;
its extension by zero and by evenness supplies the rest of the correlation.

Each finite prime-power contribution is evaluated with the exact polynomial
and enclosed log(n), log(p), sqrt(n). An independent factorization verifies
every integer label through the whole physical cutoff. Prime powers 4,8,9,
16,... are not replaced by primes alone. An ambiguous cutoff comparison fails
rather than silently excluding a possible term.

The actual discrepancy integral is evaluated in TWO ways:
 (a) the continuous-plus integral minus the full prime sum in (3);
 (b) integration on each closed-floor cell [log n,log(n+1)], where
     Delta(exp x)=psi(n)+1-exp x, with the last partial cell retained.
The latter keeps every nonprime cell. It uses exact antiderivatives of
exp(-x/2)(h'-h/2) and exp(x/2)(h'-h/2). Output intervals must overlap.
This is an implementation control; the universal identity rests on section 1.

4. Archimedean tail and rigorous error accounting
------------------------------------------------
Put w0=-EulerGamma-pi/2-3log(2)-log(pi). The digamma series gives

 Arch(f)=w0 N+2 sum_(j>=0) [N/lambda_j-
                         integral_0^(2A) exp(-lambda_j*x)h(x)dx],
 lambda_j=2j+1/2.                                             (8)

Each bracket is nonnegative, since h(x)<=N. Using
 N-h(x)=||f-T_x f||^2/2 <= x^2 N1/2,
the complete omitted tail for j>=J is between zero and

 2N1[lambda_J^(-3)+(1/4)lambda_J^(-2)].                        (9)

Indeed integral_0^infinity exp(-lambda*x)x^2/2 dx=lambda^(-3),
and sum_(j>=J)lambda_j^(-3) <=lambda_J^(-3)+integral_J^infinity
(2x+1/2)^(-3)dx. The code uses J=512 for acceptance and also retains the
nonzero upper error, rather than labeling the truncated series exact.

Polynomial-exponential antiderivatives keep both endpoints. Interval arithmetic
and logs/exp reuse the bound repository evaluator reflected_zeta_cover.py.
A real-log extension, Machin arctangent brackets and exact integer square-root
brackets are implemented explicitly. No floating-point special-function value
enters the certificate. EulerGamma is enclosed at n=64 by
 H_n-log n-1/(2n)+1/(12n^2)-1/(120n^4)
 <EulerGamma< H_n-log n-1/(2n)+1/(12n^2),
using the signed remainder in DLMF 5.11(ii). This nonzero analytic error remains.

All three complete Q_W(f_A) intervals are positive. The A=1/2 discrepancy
D(f_A) is strictly negative, while its archimedean term is positive. At A=1
and A=2, D is positive and Arch is negative. The whole expression is positive
in all three instances. This directly tests the need to retain both channels.
Finite positivity of these three tests proves neither a uniform eigenvalue
bound nor positivity on the entire infinite-dimensional test space.

5. Remaining frontier and formal status
---------------------------------------
The pending compact-power argument gives, in writing, a sharp fixed-pair
obstruction: an off-line displacement d forces the negative part of the FULL
pole-null Weil form to have lower exponential rate at least 2d. A cofinal
upper bound of rate alpha would give d<=alpha/2; rate zero would close RH.
The finite computations here provide no such alpha.

The exact source regrouping (2) is available to that route, but replacing Arch
by a constant and asking D alone for rate zero is now refuted by (6). The next
supplier must estimate their JOINT quadratic, or a stated restricted detector
family with all normalization and interpolation costs. Merely centering psi,
taking a positive kernel, or verifying three tests does not discharge it.
P04 remains an alternative, not a mandatory premise.

No new active Lean files are installed. The previous ten proof candidates and
unexecuted audit requests are retained byte-for-byte. Native elaboration,
owned/transitive axiom auditing, and full-repository structural checks were
not available in the partial source snapshot. All continuum statements in
this record remain written proofs, distinguished from executed finite tests.

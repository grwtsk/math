# Source map for the ten-paper heat and boundary continuation

All ten PDFs are by R.A. Jacob Martone. The corpus has 180 pages. Each original PDF is preserved; this package records its exact hash rather than redistributing it. The supplied manuscripts provide the mathematical basis. Their reported companion-program and external-library results are not newly replayed merely by reading the PDF.

The two versions of the sofic paper remain separate records. The updated version supplies the main heat and reflection framework; the original gives the fuller fixed-mesh boundary construction.

Locations: Theorems 2.3, 3.1; Proposition 3.4; Theorem 4.2; Sections 5-7.

**Source-derived result.** Actual quotient holomorphy, rather than an assumed uniform operator norm, is enough via the source-specific strip return. Heat and metric errors must have subexponential total cost.

**Use here.** Keep as the matrix-route alternative; the new pickup reduction asks only subpower growth of the retained means in the arithmetic cutoff.

**Not supplied or not replayed.** No all-depth growth bound or conditioning improvement is supplied by the paper. Forty Schur margins and the 128-dimensional bound are paper-reported, not replayed here.

## The actual dependency chain

The actual centered-pickup transform is built from the signed source in Grover-Hodge Section 2 and the already published logarithmic-cell decomposition. Its new whole-strip error estimate is independent of a positive approximation to the arithmetic screw kernel.

The finite cyclic covariance, Hodge block, and parity formulas are exact new adapters between supplied interfaces. Their source comparison is the separately stated weighted integral error delta_n. A proof that delta_n tends to zero, not finite-model positivity alone, would trigger the supplied uniqueness-set Schur return.

The score, tensor, oscillator, and geometric papers offer alternative certified suppliers. None is inserted as an unstated proof of that source comparison. The full two-sided nonvanishing and all-height mean-growth assertions remain open.

## Source-text checks

PDF formulas were extracted without OCR. Selected pages were rendered for mathematical signs and layout. In particular, PDF page 12 of the updated sofic paper uses sqrt(2) h+a in its finite model; the plain-text extraction drops the radical. The model is read as printed, without modifying the source.

Square-variation coherence package

  sum_n (M_n (n^-1/2-(n+1)^-1/2))^2 < 14/125.

Consequences: the negative part of complete endpoint coherence is uniformly
bounded, every dyadic negative-sector mass is below 49/625, and the positive
and signed dyadic block growth criteria are equivalent up to that fixed
constant.  The horizon diagonal completion improves from O(log^2 N) to the
explicit bound (14/125) log(N+1).

# Documentation instructions

- Use the official MSC2020 heading projection for mathematical subject folders in `mathematics/`, its verification subject subtrees, and `taxonomy/subjects/`. Administrative/reference material belongs under the existing support areas.
- Bind current document navigation to `taxonomy/msc2020/documentation-layout-v1.json`. Use readable mathematical document names and numbered source editions/occurrences where distinct records share a name. Keep cryptographic hashes in source/declaration identities and provenance metadata rather than active document filenames.
- Resolve live source and document links through the current layout aliases. Keep immutable revision URLs, actual declaration namespaces, literal source snapshots, source commit/blob pins, hypotheses, unresolved premises and historical verification scopes unchanged.
- Authored subject-concept UUIDs identify authored records; folder placement does not equate them with official MSC concepts or establish semantic review or proof acceptance.
- Source publication and mathematical acceptance are separate. Do not turn document movement or successful navigation checks into compilation, kernel replay or axiom-audit evidence.
- Keep generators, mutable processing state, execution attempts and receipts in `grwtsk/noeaaeue`; maintain only the mathematical documents and static navigation/specification data here.

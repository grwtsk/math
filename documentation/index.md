## Historical inventories

The [retained page inventory](mathematics/_support/reference/library-pages.md), [structured inventory](mathematics/_support/reference/library-summary.json), and [count report](mathematics/_support/reference/documentation-counts.json) preserve earlier population measurements and their identities. Their page counts, namespace names, dependency pins, and evidence labels are historical observations, not a live inventory after relocation and deletion.

The [previous documentation overview](https://github.com/grwtsk/math/blob/99e3b1da781bfe0fa338b4852f27bd5d1478e283/documentation/index.md) retains the former count tables and source-command census. Current dependency configuration is recorded in [`lean-toolchain`](../lean-toolchain), [`lakefile.toml`](../lakefile.toml), and [`lake-manifest.json`](../lake-manifest.json).

[Legacy artifact registry](mathematics/_support/reference/legacy-artifacts.md) records historical artifact identities and unresolved readback boundaries. Inventory counts and navigation checks do not establish proof acceptance.

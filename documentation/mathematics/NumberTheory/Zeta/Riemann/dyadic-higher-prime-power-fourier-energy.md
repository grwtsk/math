# Complete higher-prime-power Fourier energy is controlled

The previous module controlled each fixed higher-prime-power Fourier correction. The frequency denominator upgrades this to the full Fourier channel.

For k nonzero,

|h_X(k)|
 <= 2 M_X / |k|,

where M_X is the weighted non-prime von Mangoldt mass in the octave.

Therefore

|h_X(k)|^2
 <= 4 M_X^2 / k^2.

The p=2 series over integer frequencies is summable, so

sum_k |h_X(k)|^2
 <= C_freq M_X^2

for one universal finite frequency constant C_freq.

Pinned Chebyshev already gives

M_X = O(X^(-1/2)).

Hence the entire higher-prime-power Fourier energy is O(X^(-1)).

This is stronger than merely controlling each fixed mode. It means the complete higher-prime-power channel fits inside the target RH-G01 energy scale unconditionally.

The unresolved coordinate-energy theorem can therefore be reduced to the prime-only weighted DFT channel together with the corresponding prime-only endpoint discrepancies. Higher prime powers belong in the explicit residual ledger, not in the hard arithmetic core.

# Finite residue assembly and a necessary packing-decay condition

Two consequences sharpen the remaining route.

For any continuous complex multiplier F and finite absolute-height cutoff T, define

Z_T(F) = sum over rho in heightHead(T) of -m(rho) F(rho),

where the head contains every actual nontrivial zeta zero of absolute height at most T and m(rho) is its analytic multiplicity. Every summand is already proved to be the actual weighted logarithmic-residue coefficient of -zeta'/zeta. The pole term is exactly F(1).

No equality to a prime sum and no T-to-infinity convergence are asserted.

Separately, suppose there exists a comparison

V_n^2 = lambda_n H(Re(s)-1/2,Im(s)) + R_n,

with lambda_n -> L>0 and R_n->0. Structural positivity of V_n^2 first forces H>=0. The exact quartic sign theorem then forces Re(s)=1/2 and H=0. The comparison identity therefore reduces to V_n^2=R_n, so necessarily V_n^2->0.

Hence convergence of the concrete canonical prime-power packing volumes to zero is an independent necessary arithmetic theorem for this route. If that decay fails, the proposed comparison cannot exist for any zero.

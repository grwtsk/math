## Literal response space

Fix the frozen physical horizon T and one exact retained finite core interval I.
For each core n define the real response vector

  R_n(u) = a_j(n) K_N(n,u) 1_(0<=u<=T) 1_(n in I).

Take the real Hilbert space L2([0,T]) (equivalently the subspace of L2(R)
supported on [0,T]).

The pickup Gram is, by its source definition,

  G_j(m,n)=integral_0^T K_N(m,u)K_N(n,u) du.

Therefore finite scalar multiplication gives the exact identity

  <R_m,R_n>
   = a_j(m)a_j(n) 1_(m in I)1_(n in I) G_j(m,n).

No positivity theorem is used beyond the ordinary Hilbert inner-product
identity. The arithmetic signs remain outside the response Gram.

## What scalar Gowers uniformity would need to control

The map from the scalar rough Mobius coefficient to the Hilbert source is

  a_j(n) -> a_j(n) K_N(n,·).

A naive pointwise multiplier estimate uses

  ||K_N(n,·)||_2

or its selected-divisor absolute envelope. Applying such a bound independently
at the four corners discards the q,s,q',s' cancellation and returns the
previous growing divisor factor. It gives no new exponent.

A useful multiplier theorem must instead control the Hilbert-valued box
observation as a WHOLE, for example by an inequality schematically of the form

  |Box(a_j K_N)| <= ResponseCost_j * UniformityNorm(a_j)^4

with log ResponseCost_j=o(log N), while preserving the exact source interval
and gcd collision inventory.

No such inequality is proved here.

## First multiplier obstruction

For arbitrary scalar f and arbitrary Hilbert vectors V_n, scalar U2/Gowers
smallness of f alone cannot control the box of f(n)V_n: choosing V_n to encode
the sign/phase of f can destroy scalar cancellation. Hence a response-specific
regularity theorem is mathematically necessary.

The pickup response is highly structured rather than arbitrary: K_N(n,u) is
causal, piecewise generated by selected divisors, and has controlled variation
in n/u. The next bounded test is therefore to use finite Abel summation in the
core variable INSIDE the Hilbert inner products and price the total variation
of the complete signed response, not the absolute divisor expansion.

If that variation is only subpower, quantitative Mobius U2 decay may survive.
If the variation reproduces the previous A_j envelope, the multiplier route
does not improve the exponent.

# Direct compact-cover descent of joint polar continuity

Status: SOURCE_DRAFT / NOT_RUN / NOT_KERNEL_AUDITED.

Pinned parent PR #1002 head `8c5c80a6142641106f4561fac6abee438faf53c5`.

The inherited attempt to transfer quotient-map status from a compact square to the full real-angle product projection is stronger than needed for the immediate consumer and has an unverified Mathlib factorization API. This child instead proves a candidate direct continuity criterion: the target joint polar map is continuous if its pullback along the compact quotient cover is continuous. The exact compact-cover source square is recorded. Fundamental-domain coverage and target Hausdorffness remain visible prerequisites, and ancestor proof scripts remain unelaborated. No unconditional joint continuity, cone quotient descent, disk homeomorphism, CW or RH claim.

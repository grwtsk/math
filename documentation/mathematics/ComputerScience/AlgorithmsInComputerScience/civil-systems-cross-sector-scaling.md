# Cross-sector scaling of civil-service accountability and professional capacity

**Attribution:** grwtsk contributors. **Mathematical status:** source proof candidate, `NOT_RUN` for Lean compilation, fresh kernel replay and transitive axiom audit. Not a legal opinion, medical conclusion, statistical validation, or deployed intervention.

## Problem and preserved source

The existing `GRWTSK.CivilSystems.Accumulation.Trajectory` is a discrete burden balance with separately defined direct work, feedback rework and actual repair:

\[
 B_{t+1}+R_t=B_t+D_t+F_t.
\]

The additional mathematical obstruction in multi-sector operations is **noncomparability**: counting ten case actions in a housing office is not the same as ten minutes of clinical administrative diversion, and a source from one reporting period cannot establish another period's observations. The generalization must preserve sector and unit identity as well as excluded observations.

The new source `GRWTSK/ComputerScience/AlgorithmsInComputerScience/CivilSystemsCrossSectorScaling.lean` introduces nine named reference sectors, three physical work units and four work channels, but its theorems are grounded in the exact original accumulation representation rather than a newly assumed dynamics.

## Theorems and proof routes

- `WorkEvent.partition`: for **each** source event, selected-sector admitted units plus excluded residual equal its original quantity. It is a direct case split on scope and source qualification.
- `admitted_residual_partition`: induction on the event list. All observations retain their quantity, even when excluded, duplicated, sourced outside scope or denominated in another unit. This is not an attestation of upstream source completeness.
- `WorkEvent.other_sector_is_residual`: a nonmatching sector contributes zero admitted units. No pooling without a separately justified map and a common metric definition.
- `unreviewed_feedback_not_feedback` and `unreviewed_feedback_not_counted`: feedback is *unclassified* until independent causal review, not inferred from recurrence.
- `SectorTrajectory.bounded` and `SectorTrajectory.unbounded`: exact transport to published `Accumulation.Trajectory` and its already-stated boundedness/unboundedness hypotheses; no result about actual service quality follows.
- `reduced_diversion_protects_professional_time`: reuses the existing fixed-budget arithmetic; direct service time, safety and care quality are separate outcomes.
- `permission_cannot_become_duty_gap`, `unknown_evidence_cannot_become_duty_gap`, `mismatched_sector_cannot_become_duty_gap`, `potentialDutyGap_sound`: the Boolean review screen is a conjunction of externally reviewed scope facts and a contradiction under a **duty**. It is not a breach judgment.

### Negative controls

One synthetic housing feedback record with twelve minutes and unreviewed causation remains unclassified, contributes zero to a healthcare total, and contributes twelve to the matching housing/unit total. An unverified legal source, unmatched actor or unknown finding does not justify a legal flag. A consistently busy but adequately repaired service need not accumulate burden; the earlier `steadyWork` example is a counterexample to any claim that repeated work alone produces an avalanche.

## Legal reference inventory and its limitations

Noe's source-profile register proposes candidates for healthcare, postsecondary education, housing, Medicaid hearings, employment, court communication, public licensing, telecommunications relay service and public administration. Some common rules may apply to multiple sectors, but each application requires independent checks of jurisdiction, current and historic effective versions, institutional coverage, triggering facts, exceptions, remedies and qualified professional authority. The reference register is **nonexhaustive**; the model cannot claim that all laws governing a complaint have been exercised.

Never convert HIPAA permission into a care-coordination duty. Do not generalize postsecondary academic regulations to every school or telecommunications rules to water and electricity. A screening flag is not a finding of discrimination, fraud, conspiracy, coercion or criminal liability.

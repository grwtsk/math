The study of the ordinary least-squares coefficient estimator and its statistical properties in real linear models with a fixed full-column-rank design.

**Scope.** Fix integers n >= p >= 1, a deterministic real n-by-p matrix X of rank p, and Y = X beta + epsilon. For the moment calculations below require E(epsilon) = 0 and Cov(epsilon) = sigma^2 I_n with finite sigma^2 > 0. These covariance assumptions do not imply independent or Gaussian errors. Rank-deficient designs, weighted or regularized estimators and random-design claims require separate scopes. Estimating residual variance additionally requires n > p.

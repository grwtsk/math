## The complete finite-regulator Hilbert and its operator

For nonzero finite regulator L and coupling beta>0, let H_L be the
actual real gauge-invariant subspace of L2(slice SU2 links, Haar), as
constructed in WilsonSliceGaugeHilbert. This is not a finite residue
span and not an assumed vector space. Let T_L(beta) be the actual
compact symmetric strictly-positive gauge-invariant Wilson slice
transfer operator; let lambda0>0 be its largest eigenvalue.

Its full spectral Hilbert-sum coordinates are
    U : H_L ≃ (⊕_{lambda} eigenspace(T_L,lambda))_{ell2}
with multiplicities retained. The normalized transfer-logarithm
hamiltonian (regulated) is

    H_(L,beta,a) = - (1/a) log(T_L(beta)/lambda0),

with exact operator domain
    D(H) = {x in H_L : (lambda↦
      log(lambda0/lambda)/a * U(x)(lambda)) belongs to ell2}.

The operator is not bounded on all of H_L; its domain is part of its
type. The source's DiagonalHilbertDomain.adjoint_graph_iff and the
WilsonSliceSpectralApproximation consumer prove, at their stated
source-candidate scope, that D(H) is dense, the graph is closed,
and the maximal graph is self-adjoint. The existing strict positivity
ensures all nontrivial spectral eigenvalues have positive lambda, so
the logarithmic weights are nonnegative for a>0. Thus

    <x,Hx> >= 0    (x in D(H)).                       (1)

## New source-specific vacuum witness (not a formal wrapper)

The actual principal simplicity source constructs a unit positive
eigenvector Omega with
    ||Omega||=1,
    T Omega=lambda0 Omega,
and every other lambda0 eigenvector is a scalar multiple of Omega.

The new module selects this specific source-proved witness, and
identifies it with the Hilbert-sum vector having EXACTLY one nonzero
component, at lambda0. This requires the source-proved
coordinates_inverse_single identity, not a guessed projective map.
By DiagonalHilbertDomain.single_mem, the corresponding vector is in
the genuine maximal domain D(H). On that one coordinate,

    log(lambda0/lambda0)/a = log(1)/a = 0.

The exact diagonal-operator-single identity then yields

    Omega in D(H),   H Omega = 0.                    (2)

No zero-energy vector was stipulated into the Hamiltonian. It is
derived from the actual transfer eigenvalue and all Hilbert
multiplicities are retained.

For every x in D(H) with U(x)(lambda0)=0, existing exact
finite-regulator estimates give

    <x,Hx> >= gamma_(L,beta,a) ||x||^2,              (3)

where
    gamma_(L,beta,a)
      = -log(1-exp(-24 beta L^3))/a > 0.

Principal simplicity identifies the lambda0 eigenspace with the
span of Omega; the coordinate-zero condition therefore removes the
entire principal vacuum sector, not one arbitrary coordinate or a
finite set of residue classes. For L>=8 the Hilbert contains
infinitely many gauge-invariant modes, by
WilsonSliceTracePowersInfiniteRank. The estimate concerns all those
spectral modes as long as x lies in the maximal domain, not just
finite matrix approximations.

Equation (3) supplies positive separation for each fixed regulator
beta,a,L; it is NOT regulator uniform. The coefficient degenerates
as L grows at fixed beta and a. In particular no conclusion at
fixed physical nonzero continuum mass follows.

## Why this is not the full reconstructed physical Yang--Mills Hamiltonian

The regulator Hilbert H_L is an entire invariant spatial-slice
L2 space, and H is the normalized logarithm of the actual source
transfer. It is still not an OS-completed physical Hilbert derived
from the full reflection quotient, not a separately proved
electric-Laplacian-plus-magnetic Kogut--Susskind Hamiltonian, and not
a continuum Yang--Mills quantum field theory satisfying all axioms.

The real reflection-positive route presently includes:

    full 4D finite Wilson action and Haar density

The missing full physical return requires more than finite spectral
positivity: prove translation preserves the ENTIRE physical quotient,
is bounded and contractive in the reflection norm on a dense
domain, extends to a Hilbert completion, respects the vacuum, and
is linked by an exact unitary/intertwining or source equality to

OS identification. Further, continuum reconstruction requires
regulator-uniform bounds, physical units and a vacuum-compatible
limit. The source checks must not treat a conditional comparison
to another operator as an identification.

## Independent hostile checks and noncircularity

1. A finite residue Gram cannot be the full H_L for L>=8:
   the existing infinite-dimensionality theorem builds
   infinitely many independent actual Wilson trace powers.
   Every finite residue span is finite-dimensional and cannot
   equal the whole Hilbert. Positive definiteness on a finite
   Gram would still leave unmeasured excitation directions.
2. The normalized logarithm's domain is not all of H_L:
   weighted spectral coordinates must remain square summable.
   Sending an arbitrary Haar-centered observable to H without
   domain evidence is invalid.
3. Principal simplicity does not identify Haar-mean-zero with
   Omega-orthogonality. The true vacuum is beta-dependent and
   strictly positive, not generally the constant one function.
4. At beta=0 the transfer operator may collapse to rank one,
   so neither strict-positive spectrum nor the beta>0
   logarithmic-gap argument may simply be extended to beta=0.
5. The positive regulator coefficient gamma(L,beta,a) is
   not uniform in L. Restricting to a subsequence with a
   favorable finite gap is not a full continuum argument.
6. An isometric intertwiner on only a proper invariant
   subspace cannot transfer the entire target spectral gap:
   a complementary zero-energy sector might be omitted.
   The older GapTransport and proper-isometric-lift
   obstructions apply with their preserved hypotheses.

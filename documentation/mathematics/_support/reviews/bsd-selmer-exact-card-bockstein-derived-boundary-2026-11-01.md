## I. Exact prime Selmer arithmetic without suppressing Sha

Let E/Q be a nonsingular elliptic curve, p an actual prime, A=E(Q), and r=rank_Z(A). The REAL elliptic Kummer map is
  delta: A/pA -> Sel_p(E/Q)
with injection and exact cokernel Sha(E/Q)[p]. The true exact sequence

is supplied as an independently established arithmetic theorem (e.g. Silverman *Arithmetic of Elliptic Curves*, chapter X, or the genuine Tau Ceti formalization). The finite-level Sha[p] is finite; no finiteness of full Sha is assumed. The arithmetic group A is finitely generated by the classical Mordell--Weil theorem, so all the displayed groups at a fixed p are finite.

By the first isomorphism theorem and the fact that fibers of a surjective group homomorphism are cosets of its kernel, for ANY exact sequence of finite additive groups
  D -(injective)-> S -(surjective)-> H
with image D = ker(S->H), one has |S|=|D|*|H|. The new Lean candidate applies the **pinned Mathlib** AddSubgroup.card_mul_index, quotientKerEquivOfSurjective and Nat.card_congr rather than an artificial cardinal certificate. It takes injectivity, exactness and surjectivity as EXACT theorem inputs, intended to be discharged by the real elliptic Kummer maps, not by BSD rank equality.

This includes all finite torsion and Sha classes. For p prime, these are F_p vector spaces, giving

## II. A rigorous higher-derived Selmer-complex algebraic mechanism

Let R be an actual commutative ring and C0,C1,C2 actual R-modules, with degree-zero differential d0=(d00:C0->C1,d01:C1->C2), first deformation d1=(d10,d11), and second deformation d2=(d20,d21). Assume only the actual three coefficient identities from
    d(t)^2=0 mod t^3:
    d01*d00=0,
    d01*d10+d11*d00=0,
    d01*d20+d11*d10+d21*d00=0.
This is a concrete algebraic candidate for the first two Bockstein stages of a genuine deformed Selmer complex (Nekovar's Selmer complexes and Sano's derived Bockstein regulators). The conditions must be proved for the TRUE Galois-cohomological source; they are not invented automorphic Euler factors or a self-adjoint operator.

For closed x0 (d01 x0=0), define the first obstruction
    beta1([x0]) = [d11 x0] in H2(C0)=C2/im(d01).
The anticommutation identity proves beta1([x0+d00 a])=beta1([x0]), and the first obstruction vanishes if and only if an actual x1 exists with d01 x1+d11 x0=0. This is the exact first lifting problem, NOT a conjectural analytic derivative.

If first lift x1 exists, define the raw second residual
    o2(x0,x1)=d11 x1+d21 x0 in C2,
with a second lift x2 precisely when this residual is a d01-boundary. A change of degree-zero representative
    x0->x0+d00 a, x1->x1+d10 a
changes o2 by -d01(d20 a), so its class mod im(d01) is gauge-invariant.

BUT different valid first lifts x1 and y1 of the SAME x0 differ by a d01-closed 1-cochain z=y1-x1. Their second residuals differ by d11 z, which need NOT be a d01-boundary. Declaring this difference zero would be a **false higher-derived theorem**. The exact choice-independent second obstruction therefore lies in
    C2 / (im(d01) + d11(ker d01)).
The source candidate proves the congruence of the two second residuals only in that larger quotient. This retains precisely the first Bockstein image ambiguity. It is the correct finite cochain-level boundary for deriving successive obstruction filtrations, not a proof of a constructed elliptic Selmer-complex filtration or a complex central zero multiplicity.

An actual higher derived regulator additionally needs duality and a pairing on the Selmer cohomology, proof of perfectness/nondegeneracy, and determinant-line bookkeeping. None follows merely from these lift equations. The source's first and second Bocksteins are generic module algebra; an independent provider must construct them from actual elliptic p-adic Galois representations and the chosen Iwasawa augmentation.

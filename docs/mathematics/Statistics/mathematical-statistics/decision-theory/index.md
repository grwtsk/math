The mathematical study of statistical procedures as rules choosing actions from data, evaluated using a specified model and consequences represented by a loss function.

**Scope.** Estimation and testing can be formulated as decision problems, but this does not identify their canonical subjects or force one inferential philosophy. Expected loss, minimax criteria, Bayes risk and admissibility require their own definitions and assumptions. A decision-theoretic optimum is not ethical approval, repository permission or authority to execute an action.

The mathematical study of statistical models and procedures, including their assumptions, identifiability, uncertainty, error and performance under specified data-generating conditions.

**Scope.** Finite-sample and asymptotic theories, parametric and nonparametric models, and different inferential frameworks belong here. This is not a synonym for all applied statistical practice or for every use of probability. A proof about a procedure is conditional on its stated model; it does not verify that an observed population satisfies that model.

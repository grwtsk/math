The study of data-dependent rules for distinguishing specified statistical hypotheses while controlling or evaluating their probabilities of erroneous decisions.

**Scope.** Null and alternative hypotheses, sampling assumptions, rejection rules and error criteria must be explicit. Failure to reject a null is not a proof of that null. A p-value is not the posterior probability that a hypothesis is true, and statistical significance is not effect magnitude. Multiple testing, sequential use and selection after observing data require their own calibration.

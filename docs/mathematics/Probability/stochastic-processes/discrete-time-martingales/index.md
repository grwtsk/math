The study of adapted integrable real processes M_n on a filtered probability space satisfying E[M_(n+1) | F_n]=M_n almost surely.

**Scope.** The filtration is part of the model, and integrability is required at every time. Constant expectations alone do not imply the conditional identity. A martingale need not be Markov or have independent increments. Stopping at an unbounded random time or passing expectations through a limit requires additional conditions; neither optional-stopping equality nor L1 convergence follows merely from the label.

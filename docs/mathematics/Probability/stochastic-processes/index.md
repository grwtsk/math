The study of families of random variables indexed by a specified set, including their joint distributions, dependence structure and, when meaningful, sample-path properties.

**Scope.** The index set can represent discrete time, continuous time or spatial inputs; it is not automatically a physical clock. State spaces, measurable structures and filtrations must be specified where used. Finite-dimensional distributions and properties of a chosen path version are different data. A family of one-dimensional marginals alone does not determine the joint process.

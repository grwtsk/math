## Mathematical source-comparison failure boundaries

- A generic model's original cross plus defect equality does not prove the defect is small. In particular an empty physical-bit readout has observed cross 0 but complete source defect.
- The global source flip \(\mu\mapsto-\mu\) leaves every quadratic pair product unchanged; exact Gram matching alone cannot recover source signs.
- The signed original rough-gap pair mask, fixed compensated core mask and gcd-reduced FAR selector differ; no pairwise identification is asserted from common notation.
- The complex L-family signed Hermitian product uses conjugation. A general family inverse coefficient can exceed modulus 1; classical real Möbius root cost does not automatically apply.
- The exact finite complex Gram and Euler identification have authored Lean proofs in the concurrent sources, but the current Mathlib compilation and transitive axiom checks still have explicit NOT_RUN status.
- The portfolio includes the original elliptic/Putnam/physical/phase readout residuals. Taking a minimum does not turn an unproved source upper into a proved all-rate bound.

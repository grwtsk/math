## 2. General symmetric-degree energy lemma

For any symmetric relation `edge` on the finite endpoint inventory, define

\[
X_{\mathrm{edge}}(T)=
\sum_{m,n\in S_T}1_{\mathrm{edge}(m,n)}
\mu(m)\mu(n)K_T(m,n).
\]

Put

\[
d_m=\mu(m)^2K_T(m,m)\ge0.
\]

The already proved literal Gram--Young inequality is

\[
2\,|\mu(m)\mu(n)K_T(m,n)|\le d_m+d_n.
\]

If every vertex has at most `D` neighbours, summing this inequality over the
ordered relation and using symmetry gives

\[
\boxed{|X_{\mathrm{edge}}(T)|\le D\sum_{m\in S_T}d_m
      =D\,\operatorname{pickupDiagonal}(T).}
\]

The existing diagonal estimate then gives

\[
\boxed{|X_{\mathrm{edge}}(T)|\le2D T^2.}
\]

This is the abstract finite graph form of the Putnam counting mechanism. It
uses triangle inequality only after the complete signed packet has been
formed.

## 3. Multiplicative-ratio graph

For a finite set `Q` of positive integers, relate `m` and `n` when

\[
m\ne n,
\qquad
n=qm\ \text{or}\ m=qn
\]

for some `q in Q`.

For each fixed `m` and each positive `q`, there is at most one outgoing
neighbour `q*m` and at most one incoming neighbour `m/q`. Thus

\[
\deg_Q(m)\le2|Q|.
\]

The general degree lemma yields the unconditional source estimate

\[
\boxed{|X_Q^{\times}(T)|
       \le2|Q|\,\operatorname{pickupDiagonal}(T)
       \le4|Q|T^2.}
\]

This includes every selected prime-delay pair, every selected composite-ratio
pair, both ordered orientations, and all actual Möbius zeros.

\[

\[

\[

## 5. Disjoint additive and multiplicative sectors

To avoid counting a pair twice, retain the existing additive packet

\[

\]

\[
m\ne n,
\qquad |m-n|\notin Q,
\qquad n=qm\ \text{or}\ m=qn
\quad(q\in Q).
\]

Deleting additive edges cannot increase the ratio-graph degree, so the same
bound holds:

\[
\boxed{
|X_{\mathrm{ratio}\setminus\mathrm{gap},Q_{\varepsilon,T}}(T)|
\le \frac{32}{\varepsilon^2}e^{\varepsilon T}.

\]

Define the direct remaining packet by

\[
X_{\mathrm{core},Q}(T)=
\sum_{\substack{m,n\in S_T,\ m\ne n\\
 |m-n|\notin Q\\
 \nexists q\in Q:\ n=qm\text{ or }m=qn}}
\mu(m)\mu(n)K_T(m,n).
\]

Finite case splitting gives the exact source partition

\[
\boxed{

 +X_{\mathrm{ratio}\setminus\mathrm{gap},Q}(T)

\[
X_{\mathrm{long},Q}(T)

The old long-gap packet has therefore lost every selected small integral-ratio

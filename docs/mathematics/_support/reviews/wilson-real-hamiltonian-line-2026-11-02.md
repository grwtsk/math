# The real line that constructs the Hamiltonian coordinate \(q\)

Local mathematical attribution: grwtsk contributors.

## Result

The relevant line is not an affine line of integer primes. It is the affine
line of self-adjoint Hamiltonians over the real numbers

\[
  \mathcal H(q)=H+qI,\qquad q\in\mathbb R,
\]

on the unchanged maximal logarithmic domain of the regulated Wilson
Hamiltonian. The previously published prime-window Hamiltonians are the
special values

\[
  q_r=\operatorname{pntWindow}(p_r-1,1)=\log p_r.
\]

Thus the arithmetic source marks an injective transcendental subset of a
universal real operator line.

## Exact form

For every domain vector \(x\),

\[
  Q_q(x)=\langle x,\mathcal H(q)x\rangle
        =Q_0(x)+q\|x\|^2.
\]

If \(q\ge0\), source nonnegativity gives

\[
  q\|x\|^2\le Q_q(x).
\]

The actual normalized vacuum \(\Omega\) satisfies \(H\Omega=0\) and
\(\|\Omega\|=1\), hence

\[
  \mathcal H(q)\Omega=q\Omega,\qquad Q_q(\Omega)=q.
\]

Therefore the line constructs and reads out \(q\) exactly as its vacuum energy.

## The real affine state line

For domain vectors \(x,y\), restrict the form to

\[
  \ell_{x,y}(t)=x+t y,\qquad t\in\mathbb R.
\]

The restriction is the exact quadratic polynomial

\[
\begin{aligned}
 Q_q(x+t y)
 &= (1-t)Q_q(x)+(t^2-t)Q_q(y)+tQ_q(x+y)\\
 &=Q_q(x)+2tB_q(x,y)+t^2Q_q(y),
\end{aligned}
\]

where

\[
 B_q(x,y)=\frac{Q_q(x+y)-Q_q(x)-Q_q(y)}2.
\]

The line values at \(t=\pm1\) reconstruct both the direction energy and the
polar pairing:

\[
 Q_q(y)=\frac{Q_q(x+y)+Q_q(x-y)-2Q_q(x)}2,
\]

\[
 B_q(x,y)=\frac{Q_q(x+y)-Q_q(x-y)}4.
\]

This is the precise sense in which one real line constructs the quadratic
Hamiltonian data.

## Real evolution line

The scalar character generated by \(q\) is

\[
  c_q(t)=e^{-tq}.
\]

It satisfies \(c_q(s+t)=c_q(s)c_q(t)\), is positive, and for every \(t\ne0\),

\[
  q=-\frac{\log c_q(t)}{t}.
\]

The existing graded Wilson evolution uses exactly this character:

\[
  \operatorname{stepWeight}(r,t)=e^{-tq_r}.
\]

At one unit of time,

\[
  e^{-q_r}=e^{-\log p_r}=\frac1{p_r}.
\]

Thus the rational decay \(1/p_r\) and the transcendental Hamiltonian generator
\(\log p_r\) are the same real one-parameter datum viewed through exponential
and logarithm.

## Projective line between residue vacua

For two orthogonal graded vacua \(\Omega_r,\Omega_s\), the normalized state

\[
  \frac{\Omega_r+t\Omega_s}{\sqrt{1+t^2}}
\]

has Rayleigh energy

\[
  R_{r,s}(t)=\frac{q_r+t^2q_s}{1+t^2}.
\]

If \(q_r<q_s\), then \(t\ge0\) sweeps the interval
\([q_r,q_s)\). For \(q_r<q<q_s\),

\[
  t=\sqrt{\frac{q-q_r}{q_s-q}}
\]

constructs a normalized state with Rayleigh value \(q\). This is an analytic
consequence of the published graded form decomposition; it is not included as
a separate Lean theorem in the current source candidate.

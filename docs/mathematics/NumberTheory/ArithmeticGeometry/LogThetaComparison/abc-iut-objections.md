# Pilot comparisons and source correspondence obligations

The formal `PilotDiagram` has separate abstract theta and q lines, indexed concrete theta components, a concrete q line, two arithmetic-degree lines, and every directed map on the two proposed comparison routes. Its route defect is the quotient of their positive scales. If the left route scale is `j²` times the right route scale for natural `j > 1`, the actual linear equivalences differ.

This is a conditional statement about an explicit scalar-linear model. `IUTSourceCorrespondence` requires a separately located interpretation for each object, arrow and normalization. ## Objection and response propositions

Scholze and Stix's [report](https://www.math.uni-bonn.de/people/scholze/WhyABCisStillaConjecture.pdf), section 1.3, printed pages 3–4, motivates the scale comparison and distinguishes weighted from unweighted expressions. Their exact finite arithmetic is [formalized separately](../../../Analysis/FiniteSums/weighted-degree-comparison.md).

Mochizuki's [official discussion sources](https://www.kurims.kyoto-u.ac.jp/~motizuki/IUTch-discussions-2018-03.html) remain independent claims. The May comments, printed page 5, C11 and C14, distinguish q and theta degree comparisons and describe average square scaling. Page 6, C17, denies that the criticized commuting loop is required and describes a common multiradial container. The August comments, printed pages 3–4, C10 and C14, describe one concrete pilot with indexed components and deny that all comparisons are scalar-linear. Accordingly a source interpretation must establish which model, invariant, region, and comparison operation actually apply; a scalar repair interface does not settle a nonlinear comparison claim.

The retrieved 2026 LANA report also retains an explicit integral-structure compatibility obligation. Its section 10.5 withholds a final verdict and states that the compatibility formula has not been proved in that report. These source assertions are neither accepted nor refuted by the abstract line theorems.

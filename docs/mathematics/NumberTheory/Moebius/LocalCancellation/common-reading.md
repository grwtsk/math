\]
The right side includes every closed touching atom. For any \(R_d\ge1\), the full strict omitted tail is paid by \(L_d/\sqrt{R_d}\), yielding
\[

\[
|C_U|\le\left[Z_2^{3/2}B_\mu(Y)+G\sqrt{K\frac YU(1+\log(U/Y))}\right]^2
+2Z_2^2\frac YU.
\]
Here \(G\) is the complete correction half-moment, \(\alpha(p)=p/(p+1-\sqrt p)\), \(\chi(p)=\alpha(p)^2-1\), \(\chi(p^k)=0\) for \(k>1\), and \(K=\sum_{r\ge1}\chi(r)/r\). The complete positive product supplies written \(K\le e^{14/3}\). The exact finite identity
\(\sum_{d\le D}\alpha_*(d)^2/d=\sum_{r\le D}\chi(r)H_{\lfloor D/r\rfloor}/r\)
retains squareful row indices and implies the harmonic bound \(K(1+\log D)\). This aggregate and its infinite factor replacement are written formalization ports. The form is indefinite; it is not a positive Gram norm.

# Decay of the unrenormalized safe-shift quartic coefficients

The exact monomial factorization does not by itself solve the positive-limit problem.

Pinned Mathlib proves absolute convergence of the von Mangoldt L-series throughout Re(s)>1. Hence every individual L-series term tends to zero. The canonical prime-power schedule tends to infinity, so the safe plus, safe minus, and center terms all tend to zero along that schedule.

For each fixed nontrivial zero, the axial and transverse normalizations divide only by fixed real scalars a^2 and a*t (with totalized zero-locus definitions). Their point coordinates therefore still tend to zero. The corresponding normalized three-point Gram volumes satisfy

A_n -> 0,
T_n -> 0.

Thus the unrenormalized coefficients cannot converge to a common positive L.

This is a substantive correction to the bridge architecture. The remaining target cannot be

A_n,T_n -> L>0

without another source-derived stage scale.

The next object must therefore be a positive renormalization r_n produced from the same arithmetic/test source, with

r_n A_n -> L_A > 0,
r_n T_n -> L_T > 0,

and ultimately L_A=L_T, or with the difference absorbed into a complete decaying residual.

The renormalization must not depend on the desired quartic target or assume RH. It should arise from the explicit safe-term asymptotics / test-function normalization.

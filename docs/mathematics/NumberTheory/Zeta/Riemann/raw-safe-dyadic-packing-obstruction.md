# Raw safe dyadic cumulative packing is circular at its lower-density frontier

The unconditional Chebyshev budget changes the status of the cumulative packing proposal.

For every actual nontrivial zero, the raw safe-half-plane dyadic occupied volumes satisfy

sum_j Q_j^D < infinity.

For an off-line zero, the quartic factor

c(a,t)=a^4+3a^2t^2

is strictly positive. Any nonnegative common lower weight lambda_j satisfying

lambda_j <= A_j^D,
lambda_j <= T_j^D

therefore satisfies

c(a,t) lambda_j <= Q_j^D.

Hence every finite partial sum of lambda_j is bounded above by the finite total occupied budget divided by c(a,t). Its partial sums cannot diverge.

This gives a precise pointwise no-go: for every supplied actual off-line zero, the requested divergent common lower-mass packet is impossible.

At the global proposition level the result is even sharper. The claim

for every actual nontrivial zero, if a is nonzero then such a divergent lower-mass packet exists

is equivalent to RH for this raw safe geometry. If an off-line zero exists, the requested packet is impossible. If RH holds, the off-line premise is vacuous.

Therefore this lower-density condition cannot be used as an independent supplier for RH.

This does not refute volume packing in general. It refutes the specific architecture in which unrenormalized safe Dirichlet blocks are fed directly into the final local three-window Gram and one then asks for divergent common normalized Gram mass.

A viable successor must alter the source before the final positive Gram while remaining noncircular. Candidate directions include:

- source-derived normalization of an entire dyadic block before point construction;
- a growing-cell direct-sum packing rather than one local three-window Gram;
- a frame or partition normalization whose total source budget and local mass are controlled by independent identities;
- the existing growing-rank W01 architecture, provided every map remains source-faithful.

Any successor must retain the exact prime, gamma, pole, trivial-zero, nontrivial-zero, endpoint, and tail lineage and must not normalize by the desired RH target or by the final Gram coefficient itself.

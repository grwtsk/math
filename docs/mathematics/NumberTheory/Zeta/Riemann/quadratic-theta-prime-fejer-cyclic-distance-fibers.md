# Cyclic Fejer distance fibers

The finite pairing problem has been reduced further.

For every residue r modulo 4X, the cyclic distance

d(r) = min(r.val, 4X-r.val)

satisfies

d(r) <= 2X.

If d(r)=d, then r is one of the two canonical residues

d  or  4X-d.

Therefore every distance fiber contains at most two residues.

This closes the multiplicity bookkeeping needed for the factor-four pairing.
The remaining finite work is to compare each nonnegative signed cyclic kernel
sample with the norm sample at its cyclic distance, and then regroup the finite
sum by distance.

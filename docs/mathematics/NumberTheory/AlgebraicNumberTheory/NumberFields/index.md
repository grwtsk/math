The study of finite field extensions of the rational numbers and their arithmetic structures, including rings of integers, embeddings, norms, ideals and units.

**Scope.** A number field has finite degree over Q and characteristic zero. It is not a finite field, nor an arbitrary infinite algebraic extension. A presentation by a polynomial requires an irreducibility condition to define a field quotient. An order and the full ring of integers need not coincide; integral bases and ideal-class information require their own justification.

# Complete density Möbius correction


Human mathematical direction, theorem selection and conceptual framing: grwtsk contributors. Formalization and verification assistance: OpenAI Codex. Local contributions are MIT; Mathlib retains upstream authorship and Apache-2.0 licensing.

For every natural integer define
\[
\rho(n)=\prod_{p\mid n}\frac p{p+1},\qquad
\nu(n)=\mu(n)\rho(n),\qquad \gamma=\nu*\zeta.
\]
Here \(\mu\) is Mathlib's actual integer Möbius function cast to the reals, \(*\) is Dirichlet convolution, and the arithmetic-function \(\zeta\) has value one on positive integers and zero at zero. This arithmetic function is distinct from the complex Riemann zeta function. The exact return is \(\mu*\gamma=\nu\). Both functions are multiplicative. For every prime \(p\) and every integer \(k\ge1\),
\[
\nu(p^k)=\begin{cases}-p/(p+1)&k=1,\\0&k>1,\end{cases}
\qquad \gamma(p^k)=1/(p+1).
\]
Every prime power, squareful integer and mixed-prime output is retained. The correction satisfies \(\gamma(0)=0\), \(\gamma(1)=1\), \(0\le\gamma(n)\le1\), and \(\gamma(n)>0\) for \(n>0\).

The complete positive reciprocal mass is
\[
\sum_{n\ge1}\frac{\gamma(n)}n
=\sum_{n\ge1}\frac1{n^2}=:Z_2.
\]
Its proof first compares every finite prefix with a complete inventory generated by finitely many primes. Both complete local factors equal \((1-p^{-2})^{-1}\). Summability follows before the global Euler-product identity is used. A finite prime inventory includes arbitrarily high powers of every retained prime; it is not a squarefree truncation.

The same order of proof establishes
\[
G:=\sum_{n\ge1}\frac{\gamma(n)}{\sqrt n}<\infty,
\qquad G\le\exp(4Z_{3/2}),\qquad
Z_{3/2}:=\sum_{n\ge1}n^{-3/2}.
\]
The complete positive-power local mass is
\(1/((p+1)(\sqrt p-1))\le4p^{-3/2}\).
This is a bound on the whole local series. A pointwise bound
\(\gamma(n)/\sqrt n\le4n^{-3/2}\) is false on sufficiently high prime powers.

For every real \(R\), the closed head \(n\le R\) and strict infinite tail \(R<n\) are independently summable and add to the complete reciprocal mass. For \(R\ge1\),
\[
\sum_{n>R}\frac{\gamma(n)}n\le\frac G{\sqrt R}
\le\frac{\exp(4Z_{3/2})}{\sqrt R}.
\]
A touching integer belongs to the closed head and contributes zero to the strict tail. The quantitative bound is not extended to \(R=0\). The native head is an indicator sum; its identification with an explicit finite real-floor prefix remains a separate consumer adapter.

For every natural row index \(d\), define the actual coprimality-masked source
\[
\nu_d(n)=\nu(n)\mathbf1_{\gcd(n,d)=1},\qquad \beta_d=\nu_d*\zeta.
\]
The exact signed return \(\mu*\beta_d=\nu_d\), both multiplicativities, the full divisor formula and every prime power are retained:
\[
\beta_d(p^k)=\begin{cases}1/(p+1)&\gcd(p,d)=1,\\1& p\mid d,\end{cases}
\quad k\ge1.
\]
The finite bounds \(0\le\beta_d(n)\le1\) and strict positivity for \(n>0\) hold even at \(d=0\). They do not alone give an infinite moment bound.

The next written source identification is \(\beta_d=\gamma*j_d\), where
\(j_d(p^k)=(p/(p+1))^k\) for \(p\mid d\), and zero otherwise at positive exponents. The excluded local convolution equals one by its complete geometric sum. For \(d\ge1\), the finite excluded-prime inventory gives
\(\beta_d(n)\le\gamma(n)\prod_{p\mid d}(p+1)\), and hence summability before Euler factor replacement. In writing the normalized correction \(h_d=\rho(d)\beta_d\) has complete moments
\[
\sum_{n\ge1}\frac{h_d(n)}n=Z_2,
\qquad \sum_{n\ge1}\frac{h_d(n)}{\sqrt n}
=G\prod_{p\mid d}\frac p{p+1-\sqrt p}=:L_d.
\]
The excluded moment identification, complete excluded tail and actual real-cutoff row adapter remain formalization obligations. At \(d=0\), \(\nu_0\) is the convolution unit, \(\beta_0=\zeta\), and the half-moment diverges. A prospective excluded half-moment theorem therefore requires \(d\ge1\).

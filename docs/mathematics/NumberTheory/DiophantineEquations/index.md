**Scope.** State the coefficient and solution domains, sign, primitivity and coprimality restrictions, and any height or finite cutoff. Polynomial and exponential equations require their actual hypotheses. Rational solutions must be identified explicitly. A finite count, an all-height estimate and an equivalence between conjectural formulations have different content.

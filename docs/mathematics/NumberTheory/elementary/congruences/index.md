The study of residue classes of integers and equations between them modulo a specified positive integer.

**Scope.** For m greater than zero, a is congruent to b modulo m exactly when m divides a-b. Congruence classes and their chosen representatives are distinct. Nonzero classes need not be invertible for composite moduli; cancellation requires the appropriate coprimality or unit hypothesis. A modular solution is not automatically an integer solution of the original equation.

The study of algorithms that decide whether a given integer at least two is prime, together with their correctness, complexity and error or certificate guarantees.

**Scope.** Primality decision is not the same output problem as finding a complete factorization. A probable-prime outcome, a proved prime and a separately checkable certificate must not be substituted for one another. Integers zero and one are not primes; interfaces accepting them need explicit handling. The cited AKS publication and its later errata are retained as separate historical records, not reproduced or audited implementations.

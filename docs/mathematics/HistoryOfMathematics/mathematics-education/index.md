Historical study of the aims, practices, texts, institutions and communities involved in teaching and learning mathematics.

**Scope.** Both mathematical instruction and historical change are essential to this scope. Teaching with episodes from mathematical history is a different activity: it may be studied here when its historical development is the question, but is not the whole subject. The native references `97-03` and `97A30` make this distinction explicit in the selected MSC pages; they are citation pointers, not imported records or accepted exact matches. The history parent treats teaching as a practice of mathematical transmission. The education parent treats historical development as an aspect of mathematical education. These two proposed specializations do not make all historical research educational research, nor all education historical research. Their adequacy and granularity require independent review.

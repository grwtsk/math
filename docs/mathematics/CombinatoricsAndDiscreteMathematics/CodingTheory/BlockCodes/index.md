The study of finite-length codes consisting of selected words in a fixed Cartesian power of a finite alphabet, together with their distance properties and encoding or decoding rules.

**Scope.** Fix block length n at least one and a finite alphabet A. A nonempty subset of A^n is the code; an encoder and a decoder are extra data. Hamming distance counts differing coordinates, not numeric differences between symbols. Minimum-distance claims require at least two codewords or an explicit singleton convention. List decoding, unique decoding and erasure recovery have different success criteria.

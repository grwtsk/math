The study of self-maps on ordered structures satisfying all three laws: `x <= c(x)`, order preservation, and `c(c(x)) = c(x)`, together with their fixed elements and structural relationships.

**Scope.** The order and carrier must be explicit. Finite directed reachability can define such an operation on seed sets ordered by inclusion even when the graph has cycles. Monotonicity alone is insufficient. Topological closure has additional conditions; this subject is not an operational invalidation policy.

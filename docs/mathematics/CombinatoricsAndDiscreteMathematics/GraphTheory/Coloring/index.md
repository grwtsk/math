The study of assignments of colors to graph constituents subject to specified compatibility constraints, and of the existence, optimization and counting of such assignments.

**Scope.** Vertex, edge, list and total coloring pose different constraints. Colors are labels, not physical pigments or presentation styles. A relation between edge coloring and vertex coloring of a constructed line graph requires that construction; it does not make the two input graphs identical. Variants need explicit qualifiers.

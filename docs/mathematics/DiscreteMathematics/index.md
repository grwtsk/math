The study of mathematical structures whose elements are treated as distinct units, with emphasis on finite and countable constructions and their relationships.

**Scope.** The term commonly organizes counting, graphs, relations, sequences and parts of logic and algorithms. It is not a synonym for combinatorics, all computer science or only finite mathematics. Its boundary is partly disciplinary convention; this draft does not force every related subject into a single exclusive parentage.

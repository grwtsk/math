The study of homology formed from simplicial chains and their boundary maps, with explicit choices of simplicial object, coefficients and augmentation convention.

**Scope.** For an abstract simplicial complex, simplices are finite vertex sets closed under taking nonempty subsets. This is distinct from a general delta-complex or simplicial set; comparison requires the appropriate construction. Infinite complexes are not excluded from the general subject, while the computational child below is explicitly finite. Over ordinary coefficients, homology in degree n is the kernel of the boundary map modulo the image in that degree.

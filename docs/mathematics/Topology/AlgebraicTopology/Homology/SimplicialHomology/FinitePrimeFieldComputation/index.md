The study of exact algorithms for ordinary simplicial homology of explicitly given finite abstract simplicial complexes with coefficients in a specified prime field F_p.

**Scope.** The integer p must be prime. A total vertex ordering fixes compatible simplex orientations; the input includes every required face. With boundary matrices D_n over F_p satisfying D_n D_(n+1) = 0, the dimension of unreduced H_n is the number of n-simplices minus rank(D_n) minus rank(D_(n+1)), with D_0 = 0. Composite-modulus arithmetic, integral torsion, infinite inputs and inference from sampled spaces require different contracts. Library prime-size limits and omitted top-dimensional output are implementation details, not restrictions of the mathematical definition.

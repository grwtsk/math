The classical Gaussian limit theory for normalized sums of independent identically distributed real random variables with a finite strictly positive variance.

**Scope.** Write S_n=X_1+...+X_n for n >= 1. For a common mean mu and variance sigma^2 in (0,infinity), the normalized sum (S_n-n mu)/(sigma sqrt(n)) converges in distribution to N(0,1). This record excludes zero or infinite variance and does not silently include triangular arrays or dependent inputs. The classical distributional conclusion does not supply a Berry-Esseen rate, convergence in total variation, almost-sure convergence of those normalized sums or a finite-sample normal law.

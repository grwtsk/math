The study of conditions under which normalized sums or averages concentrate around an expectation or another specified limiting value.

**Scope.** Weak laws concern convergence in probability; strong laws concern almost-sure convergence on a stated probability space. For the classical independent identically distributed real-valued case, finite absolute first moment suffices for the usual strong law. Broader dependence and nonidentical sampling require their own hypotheses. An almost-sure statement allows a null exceptional set and is not a statement about every possible sequence.

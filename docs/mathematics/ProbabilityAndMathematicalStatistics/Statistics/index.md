The discipline concerned with designing the collection of data, describing variation, and drawing and assessing conclusions from data in the presence of uncertainty.

**Scope.** This subject includes mathematical theory and empirical practice. It is not the same record kind as a statistic, a dataset, a software package or a numerical summary. Probability supplies mathematical models, but a relationship to probability does not make every statistical question a probability theorem. Data quality, measurement, sampling and interpretation remain part of the broader subject even when a formal model is used.

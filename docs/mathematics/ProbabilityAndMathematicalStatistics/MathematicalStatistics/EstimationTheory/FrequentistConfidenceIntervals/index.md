The study of interval-valued data-dependent procedures with stated repeated-sampling coverage properties for an unknown scalar statistical target.

**Scope.** Coverage is a property of the procedure under a model, not a posterior probability assigned to a fixed parameter after the interval is observed. Exact finite-sample coverage, conservative coverage and asymptotic coverage are different guarantees. Bayesian credible intervals, prediction intervals for future observations, and tolerance intervals have distinct targets or interpretations and are not aliases of this concept.

The study of procedures that use data to estimate unknown statistical targets, and of the error and uncertainty attached to those procedures.

**Scope.** Targets can be scalar parameters, vectors, functions or other features of a statistical model. The target, information available to a procedure, and criterion used to evaluate it must be specified. A numerical optimization problem is not automatically statistical estimation: it needs a statistical target and interpretation. Point estimation and interval estimation answer different questions.

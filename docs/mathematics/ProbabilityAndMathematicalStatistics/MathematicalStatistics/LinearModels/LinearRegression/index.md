The study of regression models whose specified mean is linear in the unknown coefficients, and of their identifiability, estimation and inferential properties.

**Scope.** Linearity concerns coefficients, not necessarily the observed covariates: prescribed polynomial or other basis functions can be columns of a design matrix. Rank-deficient designs, random designs, unequal variances and correlated errors need explicit treatment. Ordinary least squares, weighted fitting and regularized fitting are different procedures, not synonyms for the model family. Gaussian errors are an additional distributional assumption.

The study of real-valued indexed random families whose every finite linear combination has a Gaussian law, allowing degenerate Gaussian laws.

**Scope.** The index set is specified and need not be time. Consistent mean and positive-semidefinite covariance data determine finite-dimensional Gaussian laws, not an unstated sample-path regularity or choice of version. Gaussian one-time marginals are insufficient for joint Gaussianity. Stationarity, independence, the Markov property and continuity require separate conditions.

The study of real standard Brownian motion: a process B_t for t >= 0, starting at zero, with continuous paths almost surely and increments B_t-B_s that are independent over disjoint time intervals, Gaussian with mean zero and variance t-s for 0 <= s < t.

**Scope.** The normalization is E[B_s B_t]=min(s,t); drifted, rescaled, bridged and fractional variants are not silently included. The Markov parent uses the natural filtration. Matching finite-dimensional laws does not by itself select a continuous version or make two processes indistinguishable. Transition-law descriptions and physical diffusion models require separate assumptions.

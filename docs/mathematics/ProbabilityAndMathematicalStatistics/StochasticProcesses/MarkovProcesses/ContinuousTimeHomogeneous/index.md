The study of continuous-time stochastic processes whose conditional future law, at deterministic times, depends on the past through the present state, with transition kernels depending on elapsed time.

**Scope.** The local scope uses t >= 0, a specified standard Borel state space and the natural filtration, with a time-homogeneous family of transition kernels. Continuous time does not mean continuous paths. A deterministic-time Markov property does not automatically include a strong Markov property at stopping times. A transition semigroup does not, without additional analytic assumptions, supply a strongly continuous operator semigroup or a differential generator.

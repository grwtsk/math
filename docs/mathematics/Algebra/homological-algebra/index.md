The study of algebraic complexes, exactness, homology and cohomology, and the maps and constructions that relate them in suitable algebraic categories.

**Scope.** State the coefficient ring, ambient category, grading and differential direction. A chain differential squares to zero; in a module complex its homology is the kernel modulo the incoming image, regarded inside that kernel. General additive categories need not support every quotient used in an abelian category. An abstract complex need not be the cellular or singular chain complex of a specified space. Such an application requires its own geometric construction and comparison. Equal ranks do not identify source maps or homology objects. This subject is not a renaming of [topological homology](../../Topology/algebraic-topology/homology-theory/index.md).

The study of groups freely generated by specified sets, their words, homomorphisms, subgroups and quotient constructions, using the universal extension of a generator assignment into a group.

**Scope.** Generating sets need not be finite. A raw word, its free-group element, its image in a presented quotient and its abelianization are different data. Characters can forget commutator information. A quotient by additional relators need not be free; an algebraic presentation is not an identified fundamental group of a specified space.

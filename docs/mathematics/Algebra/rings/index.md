The study of associative rings with a multiplicative identity, their ideals, homomorphisms and structural properties, without assuming that multiplication is commutative.

**Scope.** This canonical record uses unital ring maps and permits the zero ring. Whether a source requires a nonzero identity or allows nonunital rings must be recorded, not normalized away. Left, right and two-sided ideals need not coincide. Commutative rings form a narrower subject; a field requires a nonzero commutative ring in which every nonzero element is invertible. Nonassociative algebras and rings without identity require explicitly qualified extensions.

The algebraic study of finite rectangular and square matrices over specified commutative fields, including rank, linear systems, determinants and canonical-form questions.

**Scope.** The dimensions and coefficient field are explicit; products are defined only for compatible sizes. Eigenvalues and similarity concern square matrices, while left-right equivalence allows separate row and column changes of basis. A change of basis for one endomorphism is not the same relation as arbitrary row and column operations. Matrices over rings, infinite matrices, numerical approximation and matrices equipped with special analytic structure need additional scope.

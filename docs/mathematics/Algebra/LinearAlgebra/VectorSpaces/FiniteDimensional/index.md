The theory of vector spaces with finite algebraic dimension over a specified commutative field and the linear maps between them.

**Scope.** The zero-dimensional space is included. Finiteness refers to dimension, not necessarily to the number of vectors. Matrix representation uses selected ordered bases; it does not give a preferred basis. Rank-nullity is a finite-dimensional statement here, and diagonalization or a spectral theorem requires further hypotheses on the operator, field and any inner product. Analytic completeness and floating-point conditioning are separate questions.

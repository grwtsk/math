The structural theory of unital modules over specified commutative rings with identity, including their submodules, quotients, tensor products and localization.

**Scope.** The coefficient ring need not be a field or an integral domain. Finite generation and finite presentation are distinguished from each other and from freeness. Rank over a domain, local rank and vector-space dimension have different premises. Tensor products over the ring do not automatically preserve injective maps; flatness is a separate hypothesis. The zero ring admits only the zero unital module.

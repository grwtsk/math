The study of unital left or right modules over specified associative unital rings, together with module homomorphisms, submodules, quotients and structural properties.

**Scope.** A coefficient ring and a side of its action are part of the data. A left module uses a map from the ring to endomorphisms of its additive group; a right action reverses the multiplication order in that description. Commutativity can identify left/right conventions but is not assumed here. Finite generation, finite presentation, freeness, projectivity and finite underlying cardinality are different conditions. The software meaning of module is excluded.

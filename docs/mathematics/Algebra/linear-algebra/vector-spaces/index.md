The structural study of vector spaces over specified commutative fields, emphasizing subspaces, spanning, linear independence, bases, quotients and changes of scalars.

**Scope.** A field and its scalar action are part of the data. Finite and infinite dimension are distinguished. No canonical basis, inner product or topology is supplied by the vector-space axioms. General basis-existence assertions in infinite dimension retain their foundational assumptions; this record makes no choice-free basis theorem. Modules over arbitrary rings and spaces over noncommutative division rings require their own conventions rather than being silently treated as this scope.

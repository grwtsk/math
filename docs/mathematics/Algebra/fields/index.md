The study of commutative fields and their extensions, including characteristic, algebraic and transcendental elements, splitting, separability and automorphisms.

**Scope.** A field has distinct zero and identity elements and every nonzero element has an inverse. Noncommutative division rings are not silently included under the local preferred label. An extension retains the embedding of the base field. Algebraic, finite-degree, separable, normal and Galois extensions impose distinct conditions.

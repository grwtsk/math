The theory of fields with finitely many elements, including their extensions, multiplicative structure, polynomial equations and automorphisms.

**Scope.** Every finite field has prime-power cardinality p to the r, with p prime and r positive, and fixed cardinality determines its isomorphism class, not a chosen concrete encoding. The notation for a field of size p to the r does not identify it with the residue ring of integers modulo p to the r when r exceeds one. Algorithmic representations, coding applications and cryptographic claims require separate records or evidence.

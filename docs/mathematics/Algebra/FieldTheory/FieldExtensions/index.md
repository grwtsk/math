The study of commutative fields together with specified embeddings of base fields, intermediate fields, generated subfields, composita and compatible field maps.

**Scope.** Keep the base embedding and common ambient field explicit. Finite-degree, algebraic, separable, normal and Galois extensions have distinct additional hypotheses; none is a default restriction. A finite list of intermediate fields need not have finite extension degree. Its compositum means the smallest intermediate field containing every listed field, not their union. Intersections, composita and comparison maps depend on the specified embeddings. Analytic and topological completions are separate constructions.

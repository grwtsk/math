The study of commutative rings with identity, their ideals, homomorphisms and modules, including localization and questions of finiteness, factorization and dimension.

**Scope.** The zero ring is permitted; integral domains and fields impose a nonzero condition and further restrictions. Noetherianity, reducedness, integral closure and unique factorization are different properties, not synonyms for commutativity. Algebraic geometry and algebraic number theory use this subject but are not thereby automatically asserted as its subtypes.

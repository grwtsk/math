The study of commutative unital rings and their modules after specified multiplicatively closed subsets are made invertible, together with the canonical maps and their universal properties.

**Scope.** Retain the original ring, multiplicative subset and scalar maps. The subset contains one and is closed under multiplication; excluding zero is an additional condition. Zero rings and zero localizations are permitted. A localization map need not be injective. Localization of modules is included with its scalar ring specified. Completion, arbitrary coefficient replacement and localization of categories require different scopes; a structure name alone supplies none of their universal properties.

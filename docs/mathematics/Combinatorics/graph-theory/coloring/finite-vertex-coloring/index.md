The study of maps from the vertices of a finite undirected simple graph to a color set such that adjacent vertices receive different colors.

**Scope.** Simple means no loops or multiple edges. Existence with a specified number of available colors, minimizing that number and counting assignments are separate questions within the scope. Edge coloring, prescribed-list coloring and infinite-cardinal coloring are not silently included in this qualified record. An unused color is allowed unless surjectivity is separately required.

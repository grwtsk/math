**Subject:** `urn:uuid:1f0d03cb-9777-4213-ac8c-b5d04a6d46ee` · editorial status: **proposed** · [definition, scope and sources](../../../../../../taxonomy/subjects/combinatorics/graph-theory/coloring/finite-vertex-coloring/chromatic-polynomials/index.md).

The study of the polynomial associated with a finite simple graph whose value at a nonnegative integer k counts its proper vertex colorings from a fixed palette of k labeled colors.

**Scope.** The graph is finite, undirected and simple. Colorings differing by a permutation of the palette are counted separately, as are distinct assignments related by graph automorphisms. The polynomial is not the chromatic number; equal chromatic polynomials do not themselves identify graphs. Generalized graph polynomials and list-color functions need their own definitions.

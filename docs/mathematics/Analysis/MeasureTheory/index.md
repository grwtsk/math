The study of measurable spaces, measures, measurable maps and the structures and convergence questions associated with assigning size to sets.

**Scope.** The baseline is a sigma-algebra with a nonnegative countably additive measure, possibly of infinite total mass. Probability measures add total mass one; signed measures, outer measures and capacities require distinct qualifications. An abstract measurable space need not be a subset of the real line.

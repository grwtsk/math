The study of real or complex inner-product spaces complete in the induced norm, with orthogonality, projections and continuous linear operators as central structures.

**Scope.** The inner product is positive definite; indefinite forms are outside this scope. In the complex case this catalogue uses linearity in the first argument and conjugate linearity in the second. Both separable and nonseparable spaces are included; a countable orthonormal basis is not assumed. Infinite-dimensional basis existence is not classified as a choice-free construction.

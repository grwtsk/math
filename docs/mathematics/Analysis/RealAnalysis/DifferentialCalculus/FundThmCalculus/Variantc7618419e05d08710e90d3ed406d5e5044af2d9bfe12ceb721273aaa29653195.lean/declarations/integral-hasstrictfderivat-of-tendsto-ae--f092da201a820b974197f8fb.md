If `f : ℝ → E` is integrable on `a..b` and `f` is continuous at `a` and `b`, then
`(u, v) ↦ ∫ x in u..v, f x` has derivative `(u, v) ↦ v • cb - u • ca` at `(a, b)` in the sense of

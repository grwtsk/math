# Autocorrelation derivatives and unsigned mass bounds

The genuine first and second kernel derivative identities retain every label
and translation. With `W(A)=sum(k=2..floor(exp(2A))) log(k)/sqrt(k)`, the checked
A>=1 absolute prices for K, Kprime and Kdoubleprime are respectively W(A) times
`162826/(7623*A^2)+(5/A)/2`, `132/A^3+(162826/(7623*A^2))/2`, and
`815/A^4+(132/A^3)/2`. The full unsigned W(A) remains; these analytic prices
supply no polynomial arithmetic cancellation budget.

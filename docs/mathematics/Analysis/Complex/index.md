The study of complex-variable functions, especially holomorphic functions, and the analytic, geometric and topological structures governing them.

**Scope.** The introductory scope includes differentiation on open complex domains, contour integration, analytic continuation and singularities. Several complex variables and complex manifolds belong to the wider discipline but are not populated here. Holomorphicity requires complex differentiability on a neighborhood, not a derivative at one isolated point. Using complex coefficients in unrelated algebra is not by itself complex analysis.

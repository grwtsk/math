The study of complete normed vector spaces over the real or complex numbers, their bounded linear maps and their normed and dual-space structure.

**Scope.** Completeness refers to the given norm, not merely to the existence of another topology. Finite-dimensional cases are included. A norm does not necessarily arise from an inner product; weak and norm convergence are different. Completeness of a space must not be inferred from completeness of its scalar field alone.

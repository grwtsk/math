The spectral study of finite, discrete-time transition operators reversible with respect to a strictly positive stationary probability distribution.

**Scope.** The operator is `Pf(x) = sum_y P(x,y)f(y)` on the weighted Hilbert space with inner product `sum_x pi(x) conjugate(f(x)) g(x)`. Its finite carrier, transition matrix and strictly positive distribution are part of the context. Nonreversible chains, unsupported zero-weight states and continuous-time generators require distinct treatment. No repository implementation has been exhaustively searched for this exact subject.

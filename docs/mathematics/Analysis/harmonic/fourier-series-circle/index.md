The study of integer-frequency expansions of periodic functions or distributions on the circle, including Fourier coefficients, summability and modes of convergence.

**Scope.** The baseline circle is R/(2*pi*Z) with normalized Lebesgue measure dx/(2*pi). For an integrable f, coefficient k is the integral of f(x) exp(-i*k*x) against that measure. Norm, almost-everywhere, pointwise and uniform convergence must be distinguished; an L2 expansion is not a pointwise identity for every representative. A finite discrete Fourier transform or numerical sample array is a different input problem.

The study of scalar measurable-function classes with finite p-norm, for 1 <= p < infinity, or finite essential-supremum norm for p=infinity, identifying functions that agree almost everywhere.

**Scope.** A nonnegative measure and real or complex scalar field are fixed. For finite p the norm is the p-th root of the integral of the p-th power of absolute value; for infinity it is the essential supremum. Values on null sets do not specify different vectors. Cases 0<p<1, variable exponents and vector-valued integration are excluded from this restricted record. Duality, separability and inclusions between exponents require their own measure-space assumptions.

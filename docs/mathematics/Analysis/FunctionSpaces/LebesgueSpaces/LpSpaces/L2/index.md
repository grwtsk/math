The study of square-integrable real or complex scalar-function classes on a specified measure space, with inner product obtained by integrating f times the conjugate of g.

**Scope.** The exponent is exactly two, the measure is nonnegative, and functions are identified almost everywhere. The inner product uses the first-linear convention. Counting measure gives sequence-space examples.

The study of fixed-length codes that are linear subspaces of a finite-dimensional coordinate space over a specified finite field, including their generators, parity constraints and decoding problems.

**Scope.** Fix a finite field F_q, n at least one and a k-dimensional subspace C of F_q^n. The zero subspace is allowed; any minimum-distance convention for that singleton must be declared. Generator and parity-check matrices describe a code but do not independently certify a decoder. Coordinate representations over a smaller field can change the metric and block parameters.

The study of linear block codes over finite fields that are invariant under cyclic permutation of their fixed-length coordinate positions.

**Scope.** For length n at least one, send (c_0,...,c_{n-1}) to (c_{n-1},c_0,...,c_{n-2}). This symmetry is stronger than linearity. With a fixed coefficient order, polynomial arithmetic is taken modulo x^n-1; ring multiplication is not ordinary unbounded polynomial multiplication. No coprimality of n and the field characteristic is assumed here, so repeated-root cases cannot be excluded silently.

The mathematical study of redundancy, code constructions, bounds and decoding rules intended to detect or recover from specified corruption of represented messages.

**Scope.** The corruption model may describe substitutions, erasures or other faults; the alphabet, metric and decoding criterion must be stated. The qualified label avoids confusing this subject with source compression, programming, or cryptographic secrecy. Error-detecting codes are included as a limiting recovery objective; the subject is not a claim that every studied code corrects a positive number of errors.

The theory of mechanical motion and equilibrium in the nonquantum, nonrelativistic regime, including particle, rigid-body and continuum formulations.

**Scope.** Newtonian, Lagrangian and Hamiltonian descriptions use different mathematical data; their equivalence requires assumptions rather than a shared name. Constraints, dissipation, external forcing and continuum limits must be stated. Not all classical systems are conservative or Hamiltonian. A discrete numerical integrator may approximate the dynamics without inheriting every invariant exactly.

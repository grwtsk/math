The mathematical study of motion, equilibrium, forces and mechanical constraints within explicitly stated modeling regimes for particles, bodies and continua.

**Scope.** The model specifies configuration, kinematics, forces or energy, constraints and constitutive assumptions. Classical, relativistic and quantum descriptions are not interchangeable, and finite-dimensional models do not encompass every continuum problem. This subject catalogue records mathematical questions about mechanics rather than validating an empirical model or asserting that all mechanics is one differential-equation subtype.

The classical theory of finite-dimensional mechanical systems formulated through a symplectic phase space and a Hamiltonian function generating their evolution.

**Scope.** In canonical coordinates the convention is q'=partial H/partial p and p'=-partial H/partial q. Regularity and domain assumptions are explicit. Time-independent H yields conservation along its classical trajectories; time-dependent H does not generally do so. Nondegenerate symplectic structure is not interchangeable with an arbitrary Poisson tensor, and damping is not silently absorbed into the conservative canonical equations.

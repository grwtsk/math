The study of the linear conjugate-gradient method for Ax=b with a real symmetric positive-definite matrix, equivalently its minimization of the associated strictly convex quadratic over successive affine Krylov spaces.

**Scope.** Here A is a fixed real n-by-n matrix with n positive, b and the initial iterate are real vectors, and the baseline recurrence uses exact arithmetic without preconditioning. For q(x)=x^T A x/2-b^T x, the unique minimizer solves Ax=b. An exact-arithmetic finite-termination statement does not promise the same behavior after rounding. Nonlinear conjugate gradients, indefinite systems, preconditioners and implementation error require additional hypotheses and are not silently included.

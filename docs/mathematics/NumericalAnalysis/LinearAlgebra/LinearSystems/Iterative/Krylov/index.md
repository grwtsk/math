The study of linear-system approximations built from subspaces generated by repeated action of a system operator on a starting residual, and of the associated projection and recurrence algorithms.

**Scope.** For a fixed square matrix A and residual r_0, the basic space is span(r_0,A r_0,...,A^(k-1)r_0). This page selects linear-system solvers, not every eigenvalue or matrix-function use of Krylov spaces. Preconditioned and restarted variants require their own operator and subspace specifications.

The mathematical study of numerical procedures for solving mathematical problems, including approximation, error, convergence and the cost of computation.

**Scope.** Continuous problems and finite numerical representations are central, but a numerical method may also have an exact-arithmetic formulation. The field is not synonymous with running software, symbolic manipulation, empirical data analysis or every discrete algorithm. Conditioning describes sensitivity of a problem to input changes; stability concerns the behavior of a chosen computational procedure. Neither property alone supplies the other.

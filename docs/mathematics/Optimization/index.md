The study of selecting feasible mathematical objects to minimize or maximize a specified objective, including existence, structure, characterization and computation of optima.

**Scope.** Continuous, discrete, constrained and unconstrained problems belong to the wider subject. Feasibility, attainment, uniqueness, local optimality and global optimality are different questions. The field is not limited to efficient algorithms or to convex problems, and an objective in a model is not an operational authorization or a guarantee that the model represents an application.

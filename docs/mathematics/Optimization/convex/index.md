**Scope.** The canonical direction here is minimization; a concave maximization can be reformulated by negating its objective. Convexity does not alone imply attainment, uniqueness, strong duality or numerical reliability. Nonconvex formulations and merely locally convex behavior must not be accepted under this subject without an explicit reformulation.

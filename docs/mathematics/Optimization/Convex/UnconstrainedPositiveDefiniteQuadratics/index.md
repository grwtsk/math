The study of minimizing real finite-dimensional quadratic functions with symmetric positive-definite quadratic part and no constraints on the variable.

**Scope.** The local convention is q(x)=x^T A x/2-b^T x+c on R^n, with n positive and A real symmetric positive definite. Its unique minimizer is A^{-1}b; the constant c does not change it. Positive-semidefinite or indefinite matrices, constraints, infinite-dimensional operators and stochastic objectives require different scopes. The underlying minimization problem is not the conjugate-gradient algorithm itself.

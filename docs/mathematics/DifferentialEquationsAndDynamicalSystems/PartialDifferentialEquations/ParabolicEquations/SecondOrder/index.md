The study of second-order partial differential equations of parabolic type, including diffusion-like evolution and its existence, regularity, comparison and long-time questions.

**Scope.** A representative uniformly parabolic scalar form is u_t-sum(a_ij u_xixj)=f with a symmetric spatial coefficient matrix satisfying lambda I <= a <= Lambda I for constants 0<lambda<=Lambda<infinity, under additional stated regularity assumptions. The broader subject also examines nonlinear and degenerate regimes; those are not assigned the same hypotheses or conclusions. Initial and boundary conditions and the solution notion remain explicit. A semigroup equation alone is not a demonstrated diffusion generator or a heat-flow theorem.

The theory of ordinary differential equations whose differential operator is linear in the unknown function and its derivatives, including homogeneous equations and specified inhomogeneous forcing.

**Scope.** A standard finite-system form is x'(t)=A(t)x(t)+f(t), on a stated interval with assumptions on A and f. Homogeneous solutions form a vector space, whereas solutions with a fixed nonzero forcing generally form an affine space. A fundamental matrix must be invertible on the interval concerned. The expression exp(tA) is specific to constant A; variable matrices cannot in general be handled by exponentiating their entrywise integral.

**Scope.** The dependent variable may be vector-valued. Order, coefficient regularity and domain are stated for each problem; rewriting a higher-order equation as a first-order system requires the appropriate solvability and regularity assumptions. Local existence is not global existence, and continuity of a vector field alone does not guarantee uniqueness. A numerical trajectory is not an exact solution without an error argument.

The study of state evolution under iterated maps, flows or semigroup actions, focusing on orbits, invariant sets, recurrence, stability and related long-time behavior.

**Scope.** Discrete and continuous time, reversible and noninvertible evolution, and measurable, topological or differentiable structures lead to different theories. A local flow has a restricted time domain; a global action requires the appropriate composition and existence properties. Not every dynamical system is presented by a differential equation. The subject label does not certify that a software process or informal recursion defines a mathematical system.

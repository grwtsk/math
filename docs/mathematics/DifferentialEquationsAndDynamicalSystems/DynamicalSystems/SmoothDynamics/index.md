The study of differentiable state evolution on smooth manifolds, including iterates of smooth maps and flows associated with smooth vector fields.

**Scope.** Finite-dimensional manifold structure, the degree of differentiability and local versus global existence are specified in each result. Smoothness of a vector field does not itself imply completeness. Stability, linearization, bifurcation and invariant-manifold questions have their own hypotheses. Discrete maps are not automatically time-one maps of flows, and a linear approximation is not an exact conjugacy without an applicable result.

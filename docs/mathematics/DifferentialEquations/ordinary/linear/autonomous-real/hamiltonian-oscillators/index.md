The study of finite-dimensional undamped linear mechanical oscillators M q''+Kq=0 with constant real symmetric positive-definite mass and stiffness matrices, in their equivalent first-order and canonical Hamiltonian formulations.

**Scope.** Fix n>=1, M and K real symmetric positive definite, q,p in R^n, p=M q', and H=(p^T M^(-1)p+q^T Kq)/2. Then q'=M^(-1)p and p'=-Kq form a homogeneous constant-coefficient system and a canonical Hamiltonian system. No damping, forcing, gyroscopic term, time variation or numerical discretization is included. Semidefinite stiffness permits zero modes and requires a different scope. Oscillatory bounded motion is not asymptotic decay to equilibrium.

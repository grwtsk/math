The study of message encoding and decoding over a specified noisy channel, with quantitative tradeoffs among rate, block length, error criterion and permitted resources.

**Scope.** One-shot, finite-blocklength, asymptotic, zero-error, feedback and multiuser formulations are distinct. A channel law and input constraints are part of any capacity statement. Existence of a sequence approaching an asymptotic limit does not supply an efficient decoder or a zero-error guarantee for a particular finite code.

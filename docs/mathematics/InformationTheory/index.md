The mathematical study of quantitative information measures and the limits and constructions of representation, communication and inference under explicit source and observation models.

**Scope.** Entropy, mutual information, compression and reliable communication are central questions. A probability model, logarithm base and admissible coding or decision rules must be specified for quantitative claims. Statistical uncertainty is not semantic truth or usefulness; quantum information and algorithmic information require their own settings rather than an implicit identification with classical Shannon theory.

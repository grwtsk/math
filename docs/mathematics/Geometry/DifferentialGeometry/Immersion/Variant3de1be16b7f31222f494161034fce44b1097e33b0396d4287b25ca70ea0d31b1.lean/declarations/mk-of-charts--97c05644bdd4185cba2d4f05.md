/-- `f : M → N` is a `C^n` immersion at `x` if there are charts `φ` and `ψ` of `M` and `N`
around `x` and `f x`, respectively such that in these charts, `f` looks like `u ↦ (u, 0)`.

but that `f` is continuous at `x`. -/

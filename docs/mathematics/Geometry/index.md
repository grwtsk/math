The study of spatial structures, configurations and transformations, including incidence, shape, size and curvature under explicitly chosen geometric assumptions.

**Scope.** Euclidean, differential, projective, discrete and other geometries impose different structures and equivalences. This pilot opens two branches rather than identifying all geometry with a drawing in the plane. The chosen subject divisions overlap; they are not claimed to partition geometry into disjoint classes.

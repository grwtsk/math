The study of smooth geometric structures using differentiation, including smooth manifolds, tangent constructions, connections and the geometry of maps and submanifolds.

**Scope.** Smooth structure is not specified by a topological space alone, and a smooth manifold need not come with a chosen Riemannian metric. Abstract intrinsic geometry and embedded geometry must be distinguished. A coordinate formula must include its chart and regularity assumptions.

# Documentation guide

Start with the [least-prime walkthrough](start/least-prime.md), then [verify its evidence](how-to/verify.md). For an existing application, browse the [selected result catalog](reference/results.md). For research context, use the [complete inventory of existing family pages](reference/library-pages.md).

## By mathematical subject

- **Number theory:** [least prime](mathematics/NumberTheory/Prime/least-prime.md); [RH criteria and their arithmetic premises](mathematics/NumberTheory/Zeta/riemann-hypothesis-criteria.md); zeta, Möbius and prime-filter research in the family inventory.
- **Combinatorics:** [coverage and discrete quotas](mathematics/Combinatorics/Sequences/coverage-and-quotas.md); graph-coloring sources and receipts remain available in the repository.
- **Analysis:** [polynomial error return](mathematics/Analysis/Asymptotics/polynomial-error.md); Fourier, integration and Laplace families in the inventory.
- **Linear algebra and dynamics:** ordered products, gauges and transfer matrices in the inventory.
- **Logic and order:** finite rule systems, locality and coverage in the inventory.

Source placement follows [the subject-layout specification](math-layout.json).
The [source-layout reference](reference/source-layout.md) separates mathematical
subject from source role, and the [consolidation report](reference/lean-consolidation-report.md)
records the original migration verification outcomes. The
[rigor and reconstruction report](reference/rigor-and-reconstruction-report.md)
separately records the current byte-reconstruction and formal-correctness
boundaries. These routes are a reader index, not a complete declaration catalog
or a proof graph.

Read [evidence roles](explanation/evidence.md) and [documentation maintenance](explanation/documentation.md) before interpreting a status label.

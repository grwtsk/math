Its source-formal integration profile, `SOURCE-FORMAL-CELL-ADMISSION/v1`, provides a bounded mechanical admission path. The completed representative fixture profile is an entry condition for rolling work; each actual source family still needs its own current evidence.

The transfer unit is a whole declaration family or dependency component under [the migration policy](https://github.com/grwtsk/math/issues/77). Freeze exact source bytes, immutable source identity, environment and rights before typing. Preserve command ownership, generated members, full elaborated contracts, separate type/body/proof dependencies, axiom observations and explicit partiality. Type-only inventory does not substitute for body preservation, proof dependencies or replay. An unsupported generated helper stays attached to its owner with its actual trust status.

Apply M0–M7 to each selected cell: freeze, type, plan, transform, verify, reuse, project and admit. Candidate compilation, private durable storage and final source-formal admission are separate observations. Verify original and candidate inputs, statement and definition-body preservation, generated-family correspondence, actual consumers and clean target reconstruction at the selected scope. Retain failed, conditional, historical and superseded observations. A resource stop is an unfinished check, not a mathematical refutation.

One inherited human direction may authorize the rolling campaign and its eligible Git operations within the [approval policy](https://github.com/grwtsk/math/issues/74). It cannot replace source rights, trust or proof evidence. Bounded execution leases and resource receipts remain current for each run. Preserve concurrent local work and resume an unfinished cell instead of creating a duplicate. Record exact blockers with their existing implementation owner and continue unrelated eligible cells.

Public projection includes only approved source, notices, attribution, dependencies, exact statements and appropriately scoped verification. Private origin paths, branches, worktrees, migration maps, custody records and operational logs remain outside the public tree. Keep upstream and local contributions distinct under [the documentation policy](https://github.com/grwtsk/math/issues/75); machine assistance does not establish authorship or originality.

The ongoing campaign reports coverage by immutable source versions, declaration families, commands, declarations and checked consumers, with exact exclusions and missing fields. Filesystem occurrences are not a denominator for local mathematical results. Retained versions are not independent results, and declaration counts are not proof counts. A complete typing record does not discharge an open application premise.

This handoff documents the rolling source-formal process. It does not certify every local source, admit an unrestricted bulk transfer, strengthen a theorem, resolve a research target, or authorize a paper or release. No mathematical source is changed by this document.

## Exact remaining manuscript comparisons

- **A**. Chart equality requires `Field K`, a CAT(0) metric space `X`, a given `SpectralPlacementPrices K X` satisfying synchronized geodesic convexity for placed binary diagonals, a finite-dimensional real inner-product space with its measurable and Borel structure, a convex set `C`, a map preserving distances on `C`, and a nonempty finite `S ⊆ C`. Compare the stronger theorem separately on every real Hilbert space. The manuscript's `hilbert.tex` uses high-dimensional frame concentration, compactness of pointwise bounded prices, finite-span width invariance, simultaneous compatibility under larger site sets and common scaling. Directly formalize these and the extension to arbitrary cardinal dimension. The Lean price convention for the zero tensor is real zero, whereas the paper uses logarithmic value minus infinity. Use an explicit extended-real adapter or compare on nonzero tensors. Rank and dimensions of coefficient tensors require coordinates and local-map equivalence, even where Mathlib tensor products already exist.
- **B**. The formal rectangle clamps all real weights and then sorts. Lean's total division makes the correction zero on coordinate planes, matching a rounded dimension of one. The paper's `intro.tex` rectangular corollary gives weaker bounds for the dual exponent; the exact dual and rectangular formula come from the source catalogue and Lean targets above. To recover the sharp finite bound with nonscalar gates, count a multiplication only when both operands are nonconstant rational functions and a division only when its divisor is nonconstant. Adapt the quotient jet induction in `obstruction.tex` and `QuotientCircuit.lean` to prove zero new mixed products for scalar gates. Then combine with the ordinary rank bound over the algebraic closure, cast the result and take the natural ceiling. For actual plans retain output transposition, coefficient-field specialization and a finite identity certificate. Exponent admissibility quantifies positive slack and a field-dependent constant. The integral formal model also gives the exact exponent over each nontrivial commutative ring, including ℤ when charging its arithmetic operations at unit cost. A bit or tape bound separately needs operand-width and arithmetic-machine suppliers.
- **C**. `intro.tex` specifies the state sum over edge assignments with one local table variable per vertex and the paper's exact border witnesses: path exponent `Ω(log log n)`, complete binary tree `Ω(sqrt(log log n))`, grid `Ω((log n)^(1/12))`, and determinant border formula consequence. Compare the exact path, tree and determinant constants and models before claiming those quantitative correspondences; the catalogue explicitly identifies the grid rate as missing. Preserve affine-linear ABP edges, polynomial degree and variable bounds, formal identities, and Boolean witnesses for VNP. Treat `cuts.tex`, `grid.tex`, `tree.tex` and `path.tex` as the quantitative proof locators.
- **D**. The exact circuit corollary does not prove the degree-component border statement in `intro.tex`. Recover a fixed positive absolute constant, characteristic-only threshold, and all sufficiently large lengths for the exponent `c(log N)^2/(log log N)^2`. Derive the degree-component projection and size transfers from the compression, curve and Cartier arguments in `compress.tex`, `curve.tex`, `cartier.tex` and `border.tex`. Account for the sparse-support corollary and distinguish the signed path bound. In characteristic two permanent equals determinant.
- **E**. Exact fields must split the group exponent and invert its order. Record the mixed batch's simultaneous global rank and support obligations before comparing to any error in a local algorithm. The upper polynomial multiplication circuit uses integer coefficients and hence field specialization; the formal batch convolution lower remains manuscript-only. Recover the reduction from formal polynomial products to the required Fourier lower-bound model, including field extension, simultaneous rank/support and gate-count transfers. On a nontrivial elementary abelian 2-group over characteristic two the ordinary sign characters all coincide, so their transform has rank one and usual inversion is unavailable.
- **F**. The machine has one head per tape, finite alphabet, fixed finitely many tapes, contiguous unsigned inputs with leading zeros, and contiguous output of width `2n`. Space counts distinct visited tape/address pairs. Lean's `WeakETH` negates existence of one fixed deterministic machine and `0 < η < 1` having bound `2^(C v^(1-η)) (1+v+L)^d` on each width-at-most-three input. A `2^O(v/sqrt(log v)) poly(L)` bound does not imply this power saving because `v^η/sqrt(log v) → ∞` for each fixed positive `η`. Division with remainder and floor square root remain manuscript-only. Their `uniformity.tex` corollaries need a uniform Newton schedule with linear space, exact rounding and verification, and reverse reductions that hit every sufficiently large width at linear cost. The time exponent of one half is an infimum under Weak ETH.
- **G**. Private dummies give the reduction from finite sets of size at most `k` to the existing uniform fixed-base theorem while retaining every pairwise intersection. The precise quantitative base still requires the manuscript estimates from `words.tex`, `filter.tex`, `design.tex`, `geometry.tex` and `recurrence.tex`. Prove the absolute constants for `H=log₂(2r)`, `L=log₂(2H)`, and `B_r=2^(C H^4 L^(5/2))` before transferring from word families to sets and padding. A sunflower by itself supplies a structural subfamily, with preservation of a proof-search or optimization objective requiring an additional theorem.

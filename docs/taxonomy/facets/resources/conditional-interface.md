# Conditional mathematical interface

[Resource kinds](index.md) · [Facets](../index.md)

## Definition

A formalization resource that specifies, exposes or composes mathematical
properties supplied as hypotheses, fields or external certificates, without
itself constructing all the named mathematical objects or discharging those inputs.

## Scope

This resource facet does not assert a subject, theorem dependency, proof defect,
or verification result. A useful conditional theorem can be fully valid. A
separately evidenced subject association may coexist when its actual definitions
and statement warrant one. Merely naming a field `indexFormulaCertified`, `BSD`,
`crystalline` or `criticalLineConclusion` does not warrant such an association.
Opaque propositions and toy predicates remain explicitly limited in source-bound
decisions. Record existence of a supplied property separately from constructing it.

The [proposal](../../proposals/new-concepts/semantic-review-resources.md) records
the local warrant and limitations. No semantic parent edge is asserted.

# Proposed bilinear and quadratic form subject

By grwtsk contributors.

[Proposals](../index.md) · [Linear algebra](../../subjects/Algebra/LinearAlgebra/index.md) · [Registry](../../registry.md)

## Problem and affected identities

The pinned Linear algebra branch has an empty form-theory navigation stub.
Fresh review of concrete binary forms, rational reflections and principal
restrictions needs a populated mathematical scope independent of folder names.
Add one persistent subject identity; preserve all existing identities.

## Evidence and inspection limits

[L66](../../standards/index.md) independently identifies standard form terminology
inside linear algebra. Only the official browser heading and local context were
inspected, with no complete classification import, reproduction or crosswalk.
The definition and explanatory prose are locally authored.

At the affected source pin, BinaryFormIdentities supplies an explicit real binary
quadratic and guarded algebraic inverse formulas; FiniteReflection supplies
rational four-coordinate dot/reflection identities; PrincipalRestrictions
supplies nonnegative rational coordinate restrictions and a negative global
fixture. These are source candidates, not compiled results or verified paper
transcriptions. Their exact editorial evidence and current invalidation live in
Noeaaeue; those records close no additional sources through this page.

## Recommendation and before/after relationships

Add one proposed subjectSpecialization under Linear algebra. Its existing field
convention and definition/scope remain unchanged; its browse list gains a link.
The new scope distinguishes characteristic, dimension, symmetry, positivity and
restricted versus whole-space statements. Neither a matrix representation nor
a directory implies theorem dependence, geometric equivalence or proof status.

## Alternatives and compatibility

Matrix theory supplies a coordinate perspective but does not identify an
intrinsic form with a chosen matrix. Broad Linear algebra is compatible but
less specific. Quadratic-form arithmetic, Hermitian/sesquilinear theory,
Euclidean geometry and general-ring forms need separately warranted scopes;
no additional semantic parent or physical/algorithmic conclusion is inferred.
A combined form subject preserves the characteristic-dependent distinction
rather than claiming bilinear and quadratic forms are interchangeable.

## Acceptance criteria and retained rationale

Independent mathematical and cataloging review must assess the field and
characteristic conventions, finite/infinite-dimensional boundary, Hermitian
exclusion, proposed parent and future division criteria. Check zero and singular
forms, degenerate dimensions, ordered-field positivity and the difference between
a coordinate restriction and all principal minors. Link/ID checks authenticate
structure only. Status remains proposed until separate editorial gates pass;
no new mathematics, independent review, external submission or release approval
is claimed.

## Problem and affected identities

The pinned Number theory branch explicitly leaves this standard subject
unpopulated. Source-specific review of positive primitive additive triples needs
to distinguish equation constraints, finite solution counts, and unproved
all-height bounds. A folder name or a conjecture title cannot supply a subject
identity. Add the new persistent ID above; preserve every existing ID and scope.

## Evidence and inspection limits

external mapping is imported. The canonical description is original local prose.

## Recommendation and before/after relationships

Add one canonical proposed subject under Number theory by a direct proposed
subjectSpecialization edge. Its scope includes specified arithmetic solution
domains and quantitative questions; it is not limited to one primitive-triple
representation. Number theory retains its definition and scope and gains a

## Alternatives and compatibility

Elementary number theory can host some basic divisibility arguments, but does
not name this whole equation/height/counting problem family. Analytic number
theory is a useful related perspective, while a finite cover need not establish
an analytic asymptotic estimate. Diophantine geometry and approximation require
their own scopes; no extra parent is inferred from a technique. New narrower
records need separate warrants rather than copying Lean paths.

## Acceptance criteria and retained rationale

Independent mathematical and cataloging review must assess the solution-domain
convention, overlap with geometry/approximation, proposed parentage and future
division criteria. Check ordinary versus primitive, ordered versus unordered,
positive versus signed, finite cutoffs versus uniform bounds, and conjectural
equivalence versus a proved estimate. Identity/registry/link checks authenticate
structure only. Retain proposed status until the separate editorial gates pass;
no new mathematics, external standards submission or release approval is claimed.

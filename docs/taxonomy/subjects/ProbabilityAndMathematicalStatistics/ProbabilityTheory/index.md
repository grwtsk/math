# Probability theory

[Subjects](../../index.md) · [Taxonomy](../../../index.md)

## Definition

The mathematical study of probabilistic models, random quantities and their distributions and dependence.

## Scope

Stochastic processes are included. Statistical inference is not simply a synonym for this subject, even when a bibliographic range or source directory combines them. The limit and process branches below remain a pilot, not complete probability coverage.

Browse by mathematical task: [probability limit theory](../ClassicalProbability/LimitTheorems/index.md). Browse by class of probabilistic model: [stochastic process theory](../StochasticProcesses/index.md) and the retained [discrete-time Markov chains](../StochasticProcesses/MarkovChains/index.md). These are separate browsing groups, not a disjoint partition or an inference that limit theorems are model classes. Broader parentage above this navigation home remains unassessed. The [LCC outline-range candidate](../../../crosswalks/lcc/index.md) illustrates a broader bibliographic category without merging probability and statistics.

# Partial differential equations

[Differential equations](../DifferentialEquations/index.md) · [Subjects](../../index.md) · [Taxonomy](../../../index.md)

## Definition

The study of differential relations for unknown functions of several independent variables, with theories of local and global solutions, regularity, boundary data and evolution.

## Scope

Classical, weak, distributional and viscosity solutions are distinct notions whose compatibility requires hypotheses. Equation order and principal part help organize subclasses but do not supply existence or uniqueness by themselves. Domains, initial or boundary conditions, coefficients and function spaces are part of each mathematical problem. The selected parabolic branch is not exhaustive: elliptic, hyperbolic, mixed-type and other equations remain within the intended coverage.

## Example and boundary

The relation u_xx+u_yy=0 has many solutions until a domain and suitable auxiliary conditions are specified. An equation type is not a unique solved boundary-value problem.

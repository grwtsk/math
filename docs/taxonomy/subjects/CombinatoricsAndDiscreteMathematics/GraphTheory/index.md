# Graph theory

[Combinatorics](../Combinatorics/index.md) · [Subjects](../../index.md) · [Taxonomy](../../../index.md)

## Definition

The study of graphs through their vertices, edges, incidence and adjacency, including structural, extremal and algorithmic questions.

## Scope

Finite and infinite graphs occur in the field. Directedness, loops, multiple edges and additional labels or weights must be specified where relevant. A graph used to plot a function, or a software dependency graph, is not automatically a graph-theoretic theorem. A drawing and an abstract graph are different mathematical data.

## Browse

Selected division criterion: Graph-theoretic problem family. Browse [Graph coloring theory](Coloring/index.md).

## Example and boundary

Changing the drawing of an abstract graph does not necessarily change its adjacency. Properties of a particular embedding need a separate embedding specification.

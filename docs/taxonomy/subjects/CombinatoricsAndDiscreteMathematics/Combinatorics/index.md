# Combinatorics

[Subjects](../../index.md) · [Taxonomy](../../../index.md)

## Definition

The study of arrangements and discrete configurations, including their existence, enumeration, structure and extremal properties.

## Scope

Counting is important but does not exhaust the subject. Graphs, designs, ordered structures and infinite combinatorial questions require distinct treatments. Relations to probability, algebra and topology do not make every application a subtype of the method it uses. This local field is not a declaration that two repository namespaces are equivalent.

## Browse

Selected division criterion: Established combinatorial research subject; selected children may overlap. Browse [Enumerative combinatorics](../EnumerativeCombinatorics/index.md), [Graph theory](../GraphTheory/index.md). ## Example and boundary

Asking whether a configuration exists differs from counting configurations up to a specified equivalence. An enumeration must state that equivalence.

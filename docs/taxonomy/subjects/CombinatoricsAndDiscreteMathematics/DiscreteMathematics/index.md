# Discrete mathematics

[Subjects](../../index.md) · [Taxonomy](../../../index.md)

## Definition

The study of mathematical structures whose elements are treated as distinct units, with emphasis on finite and countable constructions and their relationships.

## Scope

The term commonly organizes counting, graphs, relations, sequences and parts of logic and algorithms. It is not a synonym for combinatorics, all computer science or only finite mathematics. Its boundary is partly disciplinary convention; this draft does not force every related subject into a single exclusive parentage.

## Example and boundary

Counting arrangements and investigating adjacency structures are distinct discrete questions. A numerical approximation of a continuous system needs an explicit discretization relation rather than a renamed subject.

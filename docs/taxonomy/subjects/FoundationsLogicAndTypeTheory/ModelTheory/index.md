# Model theory

[Mathematical logic](../MathematicalLogic/index.md) · [Subjects](../../index.md) · [Taxonomy](../../../index.md)

## Definition

The study of structures as interpretations of formal languages and of the connections between their properties, definability and the sentences they satisfy.

## Scope

A language, structure and satisfaction relation form part of the mathematical context. First-order model theory is central but does not exhaust model-theoretic inquiry. A statistical model, a simulation and a machine-learning model are not automatically instances of this subject merely because they use the word model.

## Browse

Selected division criterion: Model-theoretic setting; narrower records remain unpopulated. No narrower canonical pages are authored here yet; that is not a claim that no narrower subjects exist.

## Example and boundary

One may ask whether two structures satisfy the same first-order sentences in a specified language without asserting that the structures are isomorphic.

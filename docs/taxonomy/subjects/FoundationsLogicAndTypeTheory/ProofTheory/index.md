# Proof theory

[Mathematical logic](../MathematicalLogic/index.md) · [Subjects](../../index.md) · [Taxonomy](../../../index.md)

## Definition

The mathematical study of formal deductions, proof systems and their transformations, strengths and limitations.

## Scope

Derivability, normalization and comparison of formal systems are representative questions. A proof-theoretic result has a specified calculus and hypotheses; it is distinct from a successful parser, a proof-file count, or an operational permission. Semantic methods may be used without making proof theory synonymous with model theory.

## Browse

Selected division criterion: Proof-system or proof-theoretic question; narrower records remain unpopulated. No narrower canonical pages are authored here yet; that is not a claim that no narrower subjects exist.

## Example and boundary

Comparing two deduction systems requires a translation and a statement of what it preserves. The presence of both systems in a catalogue supplies neither.

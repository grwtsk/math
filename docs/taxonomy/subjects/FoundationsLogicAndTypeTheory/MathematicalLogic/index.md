# Mathematical logic

[Subjects](../../index.md) · [Taxonomy](../../../index.md)

## Definition

The mathematical study of formal languages, consequence, theories and their interpretations, including the capabilities and limitations of formal reasoning.

## Scope

The field includes syntactic, semantic, set-theoretic and effective approaches. It is not exhausted by one classical calculus, a particular proof assistant, or philosophical argumentation in general. Foundations of mathematics has broader connections than any single logical formalism; this page does not rename that whole field.

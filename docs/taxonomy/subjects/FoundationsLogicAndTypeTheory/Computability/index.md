# Computability theory

[Mathematical logic](../MathematicalLogic/index.md) · [Subjects](../../index.md) · [Taxonomy](../../../index.md)

## Definition

The study of effective computation, computable functions and sets, and the limits and relative strength of algorithmic procedures.

## Scope

The model of computation, representations of inputs and outputs, and any oracle or resource assumptions must remain explicit. Decidability is not the same as recognition of positive cases, and computability is not an efficiency guarantee. Complexity theory and practical software engineering have related but distinct questions.

## Browse

Selected division criterion: Computability notion or degree structure; narrower records remain unpopulated. No narrower canonical pages are authored here yet; that is not a claim that no narrower subjects exist.

## Example and boundary

An algorithm that halts on positive instances need not decide negative instances. This distinction cannot be removed by classifying both under computation.

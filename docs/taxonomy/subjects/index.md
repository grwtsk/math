# Subject browsing

[Taxonomy](../index.md) · [Alphabetical registry](../registry.md) · [Scope](../scope.md)

This is navigation collection `subjects`, not a concept purporting to be the unique superclass of every mathematical subject. The full MSC-based scope remains open; the following are pilot branches, not a complete top-level classification.

The [source-signal draft](../proposals/new-concepts/source-signal-subjects.md)
adds distinct proposed records for [category theory](CategoriesAndHomologicalAlgebra/CategoryTheory/index.md),
[order theory](CombinatoricsAndDiscreteMathematics/OrderAndLatticeTheory/OrderTheory/index.md), [type theory](FoundationsLogicAndTypeTheory/TypeTheory/index.md),
[theory of data structures](FoundationsLogicAndTypeTheory/DataStructureTheory/index.md), and
[proof-assistant metaprogramming](FoundationsLogicAndTypeTheory/FormalLibraryEngineering/ProofAssistantMetaprogramming/index.md).
Candidate queue routing is not editorial acceptance of a source association.

The [source-reviewed standard addition](../proposals/new-concepts/source-reviewed-standard-subjects.md)
adds [closure operators](CombinatoricsAndDiscreteMathematics/OrderAndLatticeTheory/OrderTheory/ClosureOperators/index.md),
[order embeddings](CombinatoricsAndDiscreteMathematics/OrderAndLatticeTheory/OrderTheory/OrderEmbeddings/index.md), and
[free groups](Algebra/GroupTheory/CombinatorialGroupTheory/FreeGroups/index.md) under existing order/group
subjects. Their identities are independent of source moves; their edges and
source associations remain proposed.

The [homological-algebra addition](../proposals/new-concepts/homological-algebra.md)
separately indexes [abstract algebraic complexes and homology](CategoriesAndHomologicalAlgebra/HomologicalAlgebra/index.md).
It does not identify an algebraic model with a specified space's topological homology.

[Analysis](Analysis/index.md) provides six nested specialization levels. [Probability theory](ProbabilityAndMathematicalStatistics/ProbabilityTheory/index.md) supplies a second route to the same terminal concept. Every edge is proposed pending independent review; directory descent is not an exclusive semantic-parent claim.

[History of mathematics](HistoryOfMathematics/index.md) and [mathematics education](MathematicsEducation/index.md) are additional subject entry points, not resource formats. Both reach the same [history of mathematics education](HistoryOfMathematics/MathematicsEducation/index.md) record. All new parentage remains proposed.

Candidate 0.3 adds [Mathematical logic](FoundationsLogicAndTypeTheory/MathematicalLogic/index.md), [Algebra](Algebra/index.md), [Combinatorics](CombinatoricsAndDiscreteMathematics/Combinatorics/index.md), [Discrete mathematics](CombinatoricsAndDiscreteMathematics/DiscreteMathematics/index.md). These are selected field entries, not a complete disciplinary partition. The new [chromatic-polynomial subject](CombinatoricsAndDiscreteMathematics/GraphTheory/Coloring/FiniteSimpleVertexColoring/ChromaticPolynomials/index.md) has coloring and enumerative parents.

Candidate 0.4 opens [geometry](Geometry/index.md) and [topology](Topology/index.md). The topology route reaches a finite prime-field computational-homology subject from both its simplicial-homology and computational-topology parents. This is not complete coverage of either field.

Candidate 0.5 opens [statistics](ProbabilityAndMathematicalStatistics/Statistics/index.md). Its inferential and regression routes share one restricted least-squares subject; probability, data summaries, decision authority and statistical hypotheses retain separate meanings. These are selected branches, not complete statistical coverage.

Candidate 0.6 opens [numerical analysis](NumericalAnalysis/index.md) and [mathematical optimization](Optimization/index.md). Krylov-solver and strictly convex quadratic routes share the restricted conjugate-gradient subject. Conditioning, stability, exact arithmetic and software qualification remain distinct; neither field is complete.

Candidate 0.7 opens [number theory](NumberTheory/index.md). Its elementary, analytic, algebraic and computational branches retain different questions and overlapping scope. The algebraic and computational routes share one computational-algebraic-number-theory page.

Candidate 0.8 opens [differential equations](DifferentialEquationsAndDynamicalSystems/DifferentialEquations/index.md), [dynamical systems](DifferentialEquationsAndDynamicalSystems/DynamicalSystems/index.md) and [mechanics](Mechanics/index.md). Two shared model-class records connect the equation, smooth-evolution and Hamiltonian routes without identifying the broader disciplines.

Candidate 0.9 opens [information theory](InformationTheory/index.md) and [error-correcting coding theory](CombinatoricsAndDiscreteMathematics/CodingTheory/index.md). Compression and channel tasks remain distinct; the block and algebraic routes share one finite-field linear-code page.

Candidate 0.10 expands [algebra](Algebra/index.md) with [linear algebra](Algebra/LinearAlgebra/index.md), [rings](Algebra/RingTheory/index.md), [modules](Algebra/ModuleTheory/index.md) and [fields](Algebra/FieldTheory/index.md). Two shared structural subjects have canonical homes rather than duplicated definitions.

Candidate 0.11 extends [Analysis](Analysis/index.md) with real/complex, measure/integration, Banach/Hilbert and harmonic branches. [The proposal](../proposals/new-concepts/analysis-measure-fourier.md) retains quotient and convergence distinctions.

Candidate 0.12 expands [probability](ProbabilityAndMathematicalStatistics/ProbabilityTheory/index.md) with separate [limit tasks](ProbabilityAndMathematicalStatistics/ClassicalProbability/LimitTheorems/index.md) and [stochastic-process families](ProbabilityAndMathematicalStatistics/StochasticProcesses/index.md). The earlier Markov branch remains at its original home; no reparenting is implied by a browsing link.

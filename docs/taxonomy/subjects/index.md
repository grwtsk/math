# Subject browsing

[Taxonomy](../index.md) · [Alphabetical registry](../registry.md) · [Scope](../scope.md)

This is navigation collection `subjects`, not a concept purporting to be the unique superclass of every mathematical subject. The full MSC-based scope remains open; the following are pilot branches, not a complete top-level classification.

The [source-signal draft](../proposals/new-concepts/source-signal-subjects.md)
adds distinct proposed records for [category theory](category-theory/index.md),
[order theory](order-theory/index.md), [type theory](type-theory/index.md),
[theory of data structures](data-structure-theory/index.md), and
[proof-assistant metaprogramming](proof-assistant-metaprogramming/index.md).
Candidate queue routing is not editorial acceptance of a source association.

The [source-reviewed standard addition](../proposals/new-concepts/source-reviewed-standard-subjects.md)
adds [closure operators](order-theory/closure-operators/index.md),
[order embeddings](order-theory/order-embeddings/index.md), and
[free groups](algebra/group-theory/free-groups/index.md) under existing order/group
subjects. Their identities are independent of source moves; their edges and
source associations remain proposed.

[Analysis](analysis/index.md) provides six nested specialization levels. [Probability theory](probability/index.md) supplies a second route to the same terminal concept. Every edge is proposed pending independent review; directory descent is not an exclusive semantic-parent claim.

[History of mathematics](history-of-mathematics/index.md) and [mathematics education](mathematics-education/index.md) are additional subject entry points, not resource formats. Both reach the same [history of mathematics education](history-of-mathematics/mathematics-education/index.md) record. All new parentage remains proposed.

Candidate 0.3 adds [Mathematical logic](mathematical-logic/index.md), [Algebra](algebra/index.md), [Combinatorics](combinatorics/index.md), [Discrete mathematics](discrete-mathematics/index.md). These are selected field entries, not a complete disciplinary partition. The new [chromatic-polynomial subject](combinatorics/graph-theory/coloring/finite-vertex-coloring/chromatic-polynomials/index.md) has coloring and enumerative parents.

Candidate 0.4 opens [geometry](geometry/index.md) and [topology](topology/index.md). The topology route reaches a finite prime-field computational-homology subject from both its simplicial-homology and computational-topology parents. This is not complete coverage of either field.

Candidate 0.5 opens [statistics](statistics/index.md). Its inferential and regression routes share one restricted least-squares subject; probability, data summaries, decision authority and statistical hypotheses retain separate meanings. These are selected branches, not complete statistical coverage.

Candidate 0.6 opens [numerical analysis](numerical-analysis/index.md) and [mathematical optimization](optimization/index.md). Krylov-solver and strictly convex quadratic routes share the restricted conjugate-gradient subject. Conditioning, stability, exact arithmetic and software qualification remain distinct; neither field is complete.

Candidate 0.7 opens [number theory](number-theory/index.md). Its elementary, analytic, algebraic and computational branches retain different questions and overlapping scope. The algebraic and computational routes share one computational-algebraic-number-theory page.

Candidate 0.8 opens [differential equations](differential-equations/index.md), [dynamical systems](dynamical-systems/index.md) and [mechanics](mechanics/index.md). Two shared model-class records connect the equation, smooth-evolution and Hamiltonian routes without identifying the broader disciplines.

Candidate 0.9 opens [information theory](information-theory/index.md) and [error-correcting coding theory](error-correcting-codes/index.md). Compression and channel tasks remain distinct; the block and algebraic routes share one finite-field linear-code page.

Candidate 0.10 expands [algebra](algebra/index.md) with [linear algebra](algebra/linear-algebra/index.md), [rings](algebra/rings/index.md), [modules](algebra/modules/index.md) and [fields](algebra/fields/index.md). Two shared structural subjects have canonical homes rather than duplicated definitions.

Candidate 0.11 extends [Analysis](analysis/index.md) with real/complex, measure/integration, Banach/Hilbert and harmonic branches. [The proposal](../proposals/new-concepts/analysis-measure-fourier.md) retains quotient and convergence distinctions.

Candidate 0.12 expands [probability](probability/index.md) with separate [limit tasks](probability/limit-theory/index.md) and [stochastic-process families](probability/stochastic-processes/index.md). The earlier Markov branch remains at its original home; no reparenting is implied by a browsing link.

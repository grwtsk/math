# Riemannian geometry

[Differential geometry](../index.md) · [Subjects](../../../index.md) · [Taxonomy](../../../../index.md)

## Definition

The study of smooth real manifolds equipped with a smoothly varying positive-definite inner product on each tangent space, and the associated distance, geodesic and curvature structures.

## Scope

Positive definiteness distinguishes this scope from indefinite pseudo-Riemannian geometry. Completeness, connectedness, boundary conventions and smoothness must be stated where a result needs them; none is silently assumed for every manifold in the subject. Locally defined coordinates do not make every metric globally Euclidean.

## Browse

Selected division criterion: Metric or curvature condition. No narrower canonical pages are authored here yet; this does not assert that no narrower subjects exist.

## Example and boundary

The usual round metric and a rescaled round metric on the same sphere have the same underlying topology but different length scales.

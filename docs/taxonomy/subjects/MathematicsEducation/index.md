# Mathematics education

[Subjects](../index.md) · [Taxonomy](../../index.md)

## Definition

Study and development of the teaching and learning of mathematics, including the aims, practices, resources and institutions through which mathematical learning is organized.

## Scope

The subject includes formal and informal educational settings and is not restricted to one age group or national curriculum. Its questions can concern learners, teachers, curriculum, assessment and the contexts of instruction. [L5](../../standards/index.md) supplies the representative international scope; the local wording is not copied from a classification schedule.

A mathematical exposition is not automatically research in mathematics education. A proof that a calculation is valid does not establish a teaching method's effectiveness; educational evidence and its context must be assessed separately. An introductory textbook about an unrelated mathematical subject is not an education subdiscipline merely because it is introductory. Source publication format, mathematical subject and pedagogical method remain distinct facets.

## Browse and examples

The selected child division criterion is the aspect of mathematics education investigated. An illustrative research question is how learners interpret equivalent mathematical representations in a specified setting. No empirical finding or recommended intervention is claimed here. The [MSC reference profile](../../standards/msc2020/index.md) preserves the distinction between the history of education and using mathematical history in teaching. Neither wording supplies an exact crosswalk. Repository implementation is unassessed.

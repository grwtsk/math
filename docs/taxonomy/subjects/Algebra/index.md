# Algebra

[Subjects](../index.md) · [Taxonomy](../../index.md)

## Definition

The study of operations, equations and algebraic structures, together with maps that preserve the relevant operations and relations.

## Scope

Groups, rings, fields and modules are representative settings, not an exhaustive partition. Elementary equation-solving and abstract structural questions have different levels of generality. The discipline algebra is not identical to an individual algebra over a field, nor to symbolic manipulation as a software operation.

## Browse

Selected division criterion: Principal algebraic structure. Browse [Group theory](GroupTheory/index.md), [Linear algebra](LinearAlgebra/index.md), [Ring theory](RingTheory/index.md), [Module theory](ModuleTheory/index.md) and [Field theory](FieldTheory/index.md). These subjects overlap; this is not a disjoint partition. The [algebra-structures proposal](../../proposals/new-concepts/algebra-structures.md) records the new proposed placements.

## Example and boundary

A homomorphism preserves specified operations; a shared data format or similar notation does not by itself supply such a map.

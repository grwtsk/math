# Module theory (unital modules)

[Algebra](../index.md) · [Subjects](../../index.md) · [Taxonomy](../../../index.md)

## Definition

The study of unital left or right modules over specified associative unital rings, together with module homomorphisms, submodules, quotients and structural properties.

## Scope

A coefficient ring and a side of its action are part of the data. A left module uses a map from the ring to endomorphisms of its additive group; a right action reverses the multiplication order in that description. Commutativity can identify left/right conventions but is not assumed here. Finite generation, finite presentation, freeness, projectivity and finite underlying cardinality are different conditions. The software meaning of module is excluded.

## Browse

Selected division criterion: Coefficient-ring restrictions. Browse [Module theory over commutative rings](ModulesOverCommutativeRings/index.md), [Vector space theory (over fields)](../LinearAlgebra/VectorSpaces/index.md).

## Example and boundary

The abelian group of integers modulo two is a unital module over the integers. It is generated by one element but is not a free integer module: its nonzero element is annihilated by multiplication by two.

## Placement and conventions

Vector-space theory and modules over commutative rings are overlapping coefficient-restriction routes, not disjoint partitions. The former has one canonical page under linear algebra. A module does not acquire a basis merely because its coefficient ring has an identity.

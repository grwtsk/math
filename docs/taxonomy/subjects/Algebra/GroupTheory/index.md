# Group theory

[Algebra](../index.md) · [Subjects](../../index.md) · [Taxonomy](../../../index.md)

## Definition

The study of groups, their subgroups, actions, homomorphisms and structural relationships.

## Scope

An abstract group has an associative binary operation, an identity and inverses. Finite and infinite groups belong here. Topological, Lie and algebraic groups carry additional structure that requires its own qualifications. The field is not restricted to permutation notation, finite classifications or a repository directory called GroupTheory.

## Browse

Selected division criterion: Restriction on the class of groups. Browse [Finite group theory](FiniteGroups/index.md).

## Example and boundary

Integers under addition form a group. Addition on the nonnegative integers lacks an inverse for every element, so that monoid is not a group.

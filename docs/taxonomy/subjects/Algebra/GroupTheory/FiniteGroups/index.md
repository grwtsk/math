# Finite group theory

[Group theory](../index.md) · [Subjects](../../../index.md) · [Taxonomy](../../../../index.md)

## Definition

The study of groups with finitely many elements, including their subgroup structure, actions and classification problems.

## Scope

Finite order refers to the whole group, not merely to a chosen generator or to finite generation. A finitely generated group may still be infinite. This record includes more than finite simple groups and does not assert a local implementation, replay or verification of any classification theorem.

## Browse

Selected division criterion: Finite-group family or structural question; narrower records remain unpopulated. No narrower canonical pages are authored here yet; that is not a claim that no narrower subjects exist.

## Example and boundary

The integers modulo a positive integer under addition are finite groups; the infinite cyclic group is generated by one element but is not finite.

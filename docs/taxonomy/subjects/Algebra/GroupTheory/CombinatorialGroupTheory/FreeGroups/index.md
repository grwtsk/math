# Free group theory

[Group theory](../../index.md) · [Registry](../../../../../registry.md)

## Definition

The study of groups freely generated by specified sets, their words,
homomorphisms, subgroups and quotient constructions, using the universal
extension of a generator assignment into a group.

## Scope

Generating sets need not be finite. A raw word, its free-group element, its
image in a presented quotient and its abelianization are different data.
Characters can forget commutator information. A quotient by additional relators
need not be free; an algebraic presentation is not an identified fundamental
group of a specified space.

## Example and boundary

For distinct free generators, `a*b*a^-1*b^-1` has zero exponent sums but is not
the identity. Its abelianized image is not a cellular/singular homology comparison.

# Geometry

[Subjects](../index.md) · [Taxonomy](../../index.md)

## Definition

The study of spatial structures, configurations and transformations, including incidence, shape, size and curvature under explicitly chosen geometric assumptions.

## Scope

Euclidean, differential, projective, discrete and other geometries impose different structures and equivalences. This pilot opens two branches rather than identifying all geometry with a drawing in the plane. The chosen subject divisions overlap; they are not claimed to partition geometry into disjoint classes.

## Browse

Selected division criterion: Geometric structure and associated questions. Browse [Euclidean geometry](ClassicalGeometry/EuclideanGeometry/index.md); [Differential geometry](DifferentialGeometry/index.md).

## Example and boundary

A triangle-congruence question fixes a metric and an allowed transformation class; a visually similar diagram alone does not establish congruence.

# Euclidean geometry

[Geometry](../../index.md) · [Subjects](../../../index.md) · [Taxonomy](../../../../index.md)

## Definition

The study of configurations in real Euclidean spaces, with their incidence, distance, angle and congruence structure, including axiomatic treatments of the Euclidean plane.

## Scope

The Euclidean parallel condition is not a theorem of neutral plane geometry without an additional assumption. Affine and projective equivalence need not preserve Euclidean lengths or angles. The cited axiomatic source primarily warrants the plane branch; solid and higher-dimensional coverage remains incomplete.

## Browse

Selected division criterion: Dimension or class of Euclidean configuration. No narrower canonical pages are authored here yet; this does not assert that no narrower subjects exist.

## Example and boundary

In the Cartesian plane, translating both endpoints of a segment by the same vector preserves their difference vector and hence its Euclidean length.

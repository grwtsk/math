# Prime distribution in arithmetic progressions

[Distribution of prime numbers](../index.md) · [Subjects](../../../../../index.md) · [Taxonomy](../../../../../../index.md)

## Definition

The study of rational primes in specified integer residue classes and the counting or asymptotic behavior of those primes as a size parameter varies.

## Scope

The modulus q is positive and the residue class is a modulo q. Coprime and noncoprime classes must be distinguished: a common divisor can prevent an unbounded prime family. Existence, asymptotic density for fixed q and estimates uniform over varying q are separate statements. References to classical results are not local proof certificates or assumed generalized Riemann hypotheses.

## Example and boundary

Every integer congruent to 2 modulo 4 is even, so only 2 itself can be prime in that class. This explains why coprimality restrictions cannot be discarded.

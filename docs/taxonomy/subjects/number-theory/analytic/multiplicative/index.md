# Multiplicative number theory

[Analytic number theory](../index.md) · [Subjects](../../../index.md) · [Taxonomy](../../../../index.md)

## Definition

The study of arithmetic functions, divisibility, factorization and prime-distribution questions associated with the multiplicative structure of the integers.

## Scope

The chosen home under analytic number theory reflects the classical analytic literature, not a claim that every proof in the field uses complex analysis. A multiplicative function satisfies its product law on coprime arguments; complete multiplicativity is a stronger property. Additive problems, group multiplication in general and numerical multiplication are not interchangeable with this scope.

## Browse

Selected division criterion: Arithmetic object or distribution problem; the prime-distribution branch is populated here.

Selected narrower subjects: [Distribution of prime numbers](prime-distribution/index.md).

## Example and boundary

The positive-divisor count has d(2)=2 and d(4)=3, so its multiplicativity must not be read as d(2 times 2)=d(2)d(2).

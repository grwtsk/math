# Smooth dynamical systems

[Dynamical systems](../index.md) · [Subjects](../../index.md) · [Taxonomy](../../../index.md)

## Definition

The study of differentiable state evolution on smooth manifolds, including iterates of smooth maps and flows associated with smooth vector fields.

## Scope

Finite-dimensional manifold structure, the degree of differentiability and local versus global existence are specified in each result. Smoothness of a vector field does not itself imply completeness. Stability, linearization, bifurcation and invariant-manifold questions have their own hypotheses. Discrete maps are not automatically time-one maps of flows, and a linear approximation is not an exact conjugacy without an applicable result.

## Example and boundary

The smooth vector field x'=x^2 on the real line has solutions with finite-time blowup. Smooth coefficients alone do not give a global real-time action.

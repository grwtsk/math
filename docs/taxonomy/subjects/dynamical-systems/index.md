# Dynamical systems

[Subjects](../index.md) · [Taxonomy](../../index.md)

## Definition

The study of state evolution under iterated maps, flows or semigroup actions, focusing on orbits, invariant sets, recurrence, stability and related long-time behavior.

## Scope

Discrete and continuous time, reversible and noninvertible evolution, and measurable, topological or differentiable structures lead to different theories. A local flow has a restricted time domain; a global action requires the appropriate composition and existence properties. Not every dynamical system is presented by a differential equation. The subject label does not certify that a software process or informal recursion defines a mathematical system.

Selected division criterion: Regularity and structural setting of the evolution.

## Example and boundary

Iterating a map f gives the discrete evolution f^n. Inverting time requires an invertible map; forward iteration alone does not provide a group action.

# History of mathematics

[Subjects](../index.md) · [Taxonomy](../../index.md)

## Definition

Historical investigation of mathematical ideas, practices, texts and communities, including their development and transmission across periods and cultural settings.

## Scope

The subject concerns evidence about mathematics in its historical contexts, not only a present-day reconstruction of old arguments. Historical claims and present-day mathematical validity are different questions. Translation, interpretation and the dating or attribution of sources can remain contested; a formal proof does not settle them.

A dated repository copy or Git history is not by itself a contribution to this subject. Historical sources may support research, but their custody and authorship require their own evidence. A bibliography or textbook is a resource kind, not an interchangeable historical subdiscipline. The literary warrant is [L4](../../standards/index.md), supplemented by the selected native MSC references [S1](../../standards/msc2020/index.md); neither is an endorsement of this local definition.

## Browse and examples

The selected child division criterion is the mathematical practice whose history is studied. [History of mathematics education](MathematicsEducation/index.md) concerns mathematical instruction and transmission; it has an additional education parent. This one child is not an exhaustive partition of history.

Example research question: how a mathematical procedure was represented and transmitted in particular texts and teaching settings. This is an illustrative question, not a claimed historical finding. International terminology and distinctions among historiography, biography and textual scholarship need further review. Repository associations remain unassessed.

# Homotopy theory (topological spaces)

[Algebraic topology](../index.md) · [Subjects](../../../index.md) · [Taxonomy](../../../../index.md)

## Definition

The study of spaces and continuous maps up to homotopy, including homotopy equivalences, homotopy groups and constructions that preserve or compare homotopy types.

## Scope

Homotopy of maps fixes a domain and codomain; relative or pointed homotopies require their extra constraints. Homotopy equivalence is weaker than homeomorphism. This page selects the topological-space setting, not every model-categorical or type-theoretic use of the word homotopy.

## Browse

Selected division criterion: Homotopy invariant or construction. No narrower canonical pages are authored here yet; this does not assert that no narrower subjects exist.

## Example and boundary

The interval contracts to a point, but it is not homeomorphic to a point. This separates homotopy type from topological isomorphism.

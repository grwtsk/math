# Differential equations

[Subjects](../index.md) · [Taxonomy](../../index.md)

## Definition

The study of equations constraining unknown functions through their derivatives, together with the solution notions, auxiliary data and qualitative properties appropriate to those equations.

## Scope

Ordinary and partial differential equations distinguish the independent-variable setting. Existence, uniqueness, regularity and stability are different questions, and each depends on domains, coefficients, data and the chosen solution space. A formal differential expression alone does not specify a well-posed initial or boundary problem. Differential-algebraic, stochastic, delay and fractional equations need further explicit branches; they are not silently covered by a deterministic finite-dimensional example.

## Example and boundary

An equation y' = y and an initial value y(0)=c have different roles: the equation selects a family, while the additional datum selects a member under the stated classical setting. This elementary example does not validate arbitrary differential models.

# Mechanics (mathematical sciences)

[Subjects](../index.md) · [Taxonomy](../../index.md)

## Definition

The mathematical study of motion, equilibrium, forces and mechanical constraints within explicitly stated modeling regimes for particles, bodies and continua.

## Scope

The model specifies configuration, kinematics, forces or energy, constraints and constitutive assumptions. Classical, relativistic and quantum descriptions are not interchangeable, and finite-dimensional models do not encompass every continuum problem. This subject catalogue records mathematical questions about mechanics rather than validating an empirical model or asserting that all mechanics is one differential-equation subtype.

## Browse

Selected division criterion: Physical modeling regime.

[Classical mechanics](Classical/index.md)

## Example and boundary

A force-balance equation for an equilibrium and an equation of motion ask different questions even when they concern the same physical body. A fitted trajectory does not by itself establish a constitutive law.

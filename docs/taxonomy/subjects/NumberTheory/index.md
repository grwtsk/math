# Number theory

[Subjects](../index.md) · [Taxonomy](../../index.md)

## Definition

The study of integers, rational numbers and related arithmetic structures, including divisibility, arithmetic equations, primes and arithmetic in field extensions.

## Scope

The subject includes structural, analytic and algorithmic questions. Arithmetic here is not merely elementary calculation, and number theory is not identified with a single conjecture, implementation or notation. Its elementary, analytic, algebraic and computational branches overlap; they are disciplinary perspectives, not a disjoint partition or an exhaustive set.

## Browse

Selected division criterion: Established disciplinary perspectives; additive, Diophantine and other branches remain unpopulated.

Selected narrower subjects: [Elementary number theory](ElementaryNumberTheory/index.md), [Analytic number theory](AnalyticNumberTheory/index.md), [Algebraic number theory](AlgebraicNumberTheory/index.md), [Computational number theory](ComputationalNumberTheory/index.md).

## Example and boundary

For integers a and b, asking whether a divides b differs from asking how quickly such a question can be decided for a specified encoding. Both may belong to number theory, but their claims have different evidence requirements.

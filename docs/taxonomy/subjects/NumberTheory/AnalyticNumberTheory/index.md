# Analytic number theory

[Number theory](../index.md) · [Subjects](../../index.md) · [Taxonomy](../../../index.md)

## Definition

The study of arithmetic questions using analytic estimates, limiting processes and real or complex functions associated with arithmetic data.

## Scope

The field includes averages, prime distribution and related arithmetic asymptotics, not only complex zeta functions. An analytic continuation, an identity in its domain of convergence and a statement about zeros are different claims. No Riemann hypothesis, uniform error estimate or bridge from a finite computation is assumed by this subject definition.

## Browse

Selected division criterion: Arithmetic problem family; multiplicative questions are the selected populated branch.

Selected narrower subjects: [Multiplicative number theory](MultiplicativeNumberTheory/index.md).

## Example and boundary

Counting primes up to x and bounding the discrepancy from a proposed approximation are different tasks; a finite count alone cannot establish an asymptotic estimate.

# Integer congruence theory

[Elementary number theory](../index.md) · [Subjects](../../../index.md) · [Taxonomy](../../../../index.md)

## Definition

The study of residue classes of integers and equations between them modulo a specified positive integer.

## Scope

For m greater than zero, a is congruent to b modulo m exactly when m divides a-b. Congruence classes and their chosen representatives are distinct. Nonzero classes need not be invertible for composite moduli; cancellation requires the appropriate coprimality or unit hypothesis. A modular solution is not automatically an integer solution of the original equation.

## Example and boundary

Modulo 6, 2 times 1 and 2 times 4 agree, but 1 and 4 do not. Thus cancellation of a nonzero factor is invalid without additional conditions.

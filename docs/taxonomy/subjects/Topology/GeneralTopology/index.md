# General topology

[Topology](../index.md) · [Subjects](../../index.md) · [Taxonomy](../../../index.md)

## Definition

The study of topological spaces and continuous maps through basic constructions and properties such as convergence, separation, compactness, connectedness, products and quotients.

## Scope

Point-set topology is a supported alternative label. Assumptions such as Hausdorffness or countability are hypotheses, not built into every topological space. Sequence convergence alone need not characterize the topology; nonmanifold and nonmetrizable spaces remain in scope.

## Browse

Selected division criterion: Topological property or construction. No narrower canonical pages are authored here yet; this does not assert that no narrower subjects exist.

## Example and boundary

A product topology and a quotient topology solve different continuity problems; they are not interchangeable because both construct a new space.

# Algebraic topology

[Topology](../index.md) · [Subjects](../../index.md) · [Taxonomy](../../../index.md)

## Definition

The study of spaces and maps by algebraic invariants and constructions, including fundamental and higher homotopy groups, homology, cohomology and their functorial relationships.

## Scope

The subject is not a synonym for all topology or all algebra. An invariant generally forgets information: agreement of one invariant is not by itself a classification theorem. Cohomology and generalized theories remain in the intended scope although their own pages are not populated in this batch.

## Browse

Selected division criterion: Family of invariant or construction. Browse [Homotopy theory (topological spaces)](Homotopy/index.md); [Homology theory (topology)](Homology/index.md).

## Example and boundary

A continuous map can induce a homomorphism on a specified homology group; the existence of that homomorphism does not make the original map a homeomorphism.

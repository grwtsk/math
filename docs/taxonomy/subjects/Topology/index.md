# Topology

[Subjects](../index.md) · [Taxonomy](../../index.md)

## Definition

The study of topological spaces, continuous maps and properties expressed through continuity rather than a chosen distance or coordinate system.

## Scope

General, algebraic, geometric and computational topology are related but distinct organizing subjects. Not every topological space is a manifold, metrizable or Hausdorff. The sources here support representative branches, not an exhaustive definition of all current topological research.

## Browse

Selected division criterion: Established topological subdiscipline. Browse [General topology](GeneralTopology/index.md); [Algebraic topology](AlgebraicTopology/index.md); [Computational topology](ComputationalTopology/index.md).

## Example and boundary

A homeomorphism is a continuous bijection with continuous inverse; a continuous bijection without the inverse condition does not satisfy that definition.

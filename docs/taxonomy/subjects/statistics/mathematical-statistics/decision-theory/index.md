# Statistical decision theory

[Mathematical statistics](../index.md) · [Subjects](../../../index.md) · [Taxonomy](../../../../index.md)

## Definition

The mathematical study of statistical procedures as rules choosing actions from data, evaluated using a specified model and consequences represented by a loss function.

## Scope

Estimation and testing can be formulated as decision problems, but this does not identify their canonical subjects or force one inferential philosophy. Expected loss, minimax criteria, Bayes risk and admissibility require their own definitions and assumptions. A decision-theoretic optimum is not ethical approval, repository permission or authority to execute an action.

## Example and boundary

With squared-error loss, a rule is judged by expected squared error under the selected law. Changing the loss can change which rule is preferred even though the data and parameter space stay fixed.

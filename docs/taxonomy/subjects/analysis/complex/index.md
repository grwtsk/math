# Complex analysis

[Analysis](../index.md) · [Subjects](../../index.md) · [Taxonomy](../../../index.md)

## Definition

The study of complex-variable functions, especially holomorphic functions, and the analytic, geometric and topological structures governing them.

## Scope

The introductory scope includes differentiation on open complex domains, contour integration, analytic continuation and singularities. Several complex variables and complex manifolds belong to the wider discipline but are not populated here. Holomorphicity requires complex differentiability on a neighborhood, not a derivative at one isolated point. Using complex coefficients in unrelated algebra is not by itself complex analysis.

## Browse

Selected division criterion: Number of complex variables or geometric setting. No narrower canonical records are authored yet; the field is not thereby exhausted.

## Example and boundary

For complex conjugation, the difference quotient along real increments equals one and along purely imaginary increments equals minus one. The incompatible limits exclude a complex derivative despite real differentiability of the map of two real coordinates.

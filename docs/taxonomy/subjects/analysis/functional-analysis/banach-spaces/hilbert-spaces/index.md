# Hilbert space theory (real or complex)

[Banach space theory (real or complex)](../index.md) · [Subjects](../../../../index.md) · [Taxonomy](../../../../../index.md)

## Definition

The study of real or complex inner-product spaces complete in the induced norm, with orthogonality, projections and continuous linear operators as central structures.

## Scope

The inner product is positive definite; indefinite forms are outside this scope. In the complex case this catalogue uses linearity in the first argument and conjugate linearity in the second. Both separable and nonseparable spaces are included; a countable orthonormal basis is not assumed. Infinite-dimensional basis existence is not classified as a choice-free construction.

## Browse

Selected division criterion: Specified realization of the inner-product space. Browse [L2 space theory (scalar measure spaces)](../../../real/measure/lebesgue-integration/lp-spaces/l2/index.md).

## Example and boundary

For e1 and e2 in R^2 with the l1 norm, the squared norms of e1+e2 and e1-e2 sum to eight, whereas twice the sum of their squared individual norms is four. This norm fails the parallelogram law and is not induced by an inner product.

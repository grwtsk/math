# Harmonic analysis

[Analysis](../index.md) · [Subjects](../../index.md) · [Taxonomy](../../../index.md)

## Definition

The study of frequency decompositions, oscillation and averaging of functions and distributions, including Fourier methods and associated analytic operators.

## Scope

Fourier series and transforms are central but do not exhaust the field: singular integrals, maximal operators and analysis on groups are also part of its established scope. The ambient space, measure, function class and normalization matter. This subject is not synonymous with the study of functions satisfying Laplace's equation, nor with every application that uses a transform.

## Browse

Selected division criterion: Ambient space for the frequency expansion. Browse [Fourier series theory on the circle](fourier-series-circle/index.md).

## Example and boundary

For a trigonometric polynomial on the circle, translating its argument multiplies each frequency coefficient by the corresponding phase factor. That finite calculation illustrates the interaction between translation and frequency; it does not justify an infinite interchange or a transform on an arbitrary space.

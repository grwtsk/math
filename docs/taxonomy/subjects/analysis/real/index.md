# Real analysis

[Analysis](../index.md) · [Subjects](../../index.md) · [Taxonomy](../../../index.md)

## Definition

The study of limits, continuity, differentiation and integration for real-variable functions, together with the analytic structures needed to formulate these operations.

## Scope

Real-variable domains may support real- or complex-valued functions. The subject is broader than symbolic calculus and does not require every function to be differentiable. Several-variable and abstract measure settings need their own hypotheses. A selected measure branch is not a complete account of real analysis.

## Browse

Selected division criterion: Analytic subdiscipline. Browse [Measure theory](measure/index.md).

## Example and boundary

On [0,1], the functions x^n converge pointwise to a function that is zero below 1 and one at 1. Each approximant is continuous, but the limit is not; the convergence is not uniform. This separates two convergence questions without asserting a general exchange-of-limits rule.

# Scope and coverage contract

[Taxonomy](index.md) · [Measured status](coverage/index.md)

## Declared scope

The eventual baseline is every entry and native relationship at every published MSC2020 level, not merely subjects present in GRWTSK. Foundations, pure mathematics, applied mathematics, probability, statistics, numerical and symbolic computation, optimization, physics and mechanics, interdisciplinary applications, history and mathematics education remain in scope even without local source. The pilot does not reduce that scope.

MSC is retained as an external classification, not silently converted into the local subject ontology. An external code can designate a subject or an indexing provision. Resource-kind, computational, historical, modeling and data provisions require explicit dispositions; suffix patterns alone are not concept definitions. Do not synthesize absent combinations of a subject prefix and suffix.

## Five independent measures

Every measure must publish its input revision, exact finite universe or a declared UNAVAILABLE denominator, completed subset, unresolved identifiers and exclusion rationale. An unknown denominator cannot be reported as zero or converted into a percentage.

1. External registration: for each native level L, let E_L be the exact identifier set reconciled against legally acquired, hash-pinned official inputs. R_L contains records with correct identifier, edition, native description/relationship provenance and rights disposition. Report R_L/E_L and E_L minus R_L. A reference-only pointer is not a fully registered external entry.
2. Substantive coverage: obligations are all baseline subject and indexing entries, with explicit record-kind dispositions. Count an obligation only when reviewed local concept records or an appropriate indexing/facet treatment explain its scope. Count draft prose, link-only records, stubs and reviewed records separately. Multiple codes covered by one concept and one code covered by several concepts require explicit coverage records, not duplicate counts.
3. Crosswalk review: the denominator is the finite set of requested source/target edition pairs and mapping obligations fixed in the release plan. Report reviewed accepted correspondences, reviewed no-suitable-match decisions, and unassessed/disputed/inaccessible/license-blocked cases separately. Do not count all conceivable scheme pairs or silently shrink the requested set.
4. Repository classification: maintain separate universes for maintained source occurrences, historical occurrences, guides and proposal records. Each occurrence is pinned by repository, commit, path, mode and blob; history may require a distinct capture/context identity even when bytes match. Count reviewed classification associations, not just unique filenames or declarations.
5. Mathematical evidence: use the finite set of exact statement/application obligations selected for verification, not the subject catalogue. Record accepted evidence, conditional results, open premises, NOT_RUN and UNAVAILABLE at their actual source and trust scope. A subject does not require a local proof in order to exist.

## Reconciliation and release

Reconcile official PDF and an authorized structured source where available; resolve duplicate, missing, retired, malformed and differently rendered identifiers explicitly. Report source-specific counts and the exact set differences. Publisher headline totals are comparison expectations only. A parser that yields the expected number but the wrong identifiers fails.

Bounded releases identify their admitted subsets while retaining all outstanding baseline obligations. Retired external codes remain resolvable within their edition. Splits and merges preserve predecessor records and require replacement decisions rather than identity reuse.

Repository absence may be stated only against an exhaustively inspected, named occurrence universe. Before that, use `unassessed`. Distinguish no local content, not classified, not formally verified, unknown mathematical status and genuinely nonexistent definitions. No naming or file-count heuristic establishes mathematical novelty.

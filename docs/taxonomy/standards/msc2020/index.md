## Source identity and edition

Publisher: Mathematical Reviews / American Mathematical Society and zbMATH, jointly. Scheme: MSC; edition: 2020. The [official publication](https://msc2020.org/) and [AMS search guidance](https://mathscinet.ams.org/mathscinet/info/docs/search-extras/search-msc) identify MSC2020 as the current published classification at the 2026-10-19 inspection. This local reference profile is not an external scheme revision or endorsement.

The official publication links a [PDF](https://msc2020.org/MSC_2020.pdf), [TeX source](https://msc2020.org/MSC_2020.tex) and [CSV](https://msc2020.org/MSC_2020.csv). Native code spellings and distinctions belong to that source; do not synthesize absent suffix combinations or replace its relationships with local subject parentage. The PDF presents 224 pages. Selected pages support citation-level inspection, not a complete parsed baseline.

## Reference access is not an imported inventory

The browser exposes PDF text and selected rendered pages. The TeX and CSV responses are not readable through that browser interface because of their application/octet-stream content type; original-byte retrieval to the working environment was unavailable in this delivery. Original source checksums and cross-format byte-bound identifier reconciliation remain **UNAVAILABLE / NOT_RUN**. A hash of retyped text would identify the transcription, not the publisher's original file.

The reported totals 63 / 529 / 6,022 are publisher comparison expectations for native levels, not the cardinalities of reconciled local identifier sets. No complete code list, descriptor hierarchy or scheme entry is imported. No external target is accepted solely because its code appears in a cited excerpt. Full registration still requires exact-set reconciliation, duplicate/missing-code treatment, native relationship preservation and source rights disposition.

## Selected boundary references

The history/education subject pilot cites `01-XX` and `01Axx` on PDF page 5, and `97-XX`, `97-03` and `97A30` on pages 219-220 (one-based). The last pair distinguishes the history of mathematics education from the use of history in mathematics education. These are source pointers, not new production crosswalks. The absence of `01-03` is an explicit omission discussed by the publisher, not permission to fabricate it.

## Rights and distribution arrangement

The official publication links [CC BY-NC-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/). The [legal code](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/legalcode.en), sections 2-4, limits the licensed reproduction/adaptation grant to noncommercial uses and imposes attribution and applicable share-alike/database conditions. The actual intended reuse, rights basis and downstream distribution must be assessed; neither a public URL nor an MIT repository license answers that question.

Selected arrangement for this draft: original local prose, bibliographic metadata and selective reference links only. No full MSC data, descriptions, converted hierarchy or derivative code catalogue is included in either repository or the conversation distribution. Moving such data into a private cache or Noe directory would not itself establish permission. Local original prose retains its local license; cited material retains its own terms.

Bulk source acquisition for reuse, imported scheme registration and derivative distribution remain blocked pending a source-specific disposition. Possible paths are a qualifying license-compliant use with retained notices and distribution controls, or separately granted permission; this document selects neither on the user's behalf. It grants no operational authority and is not a legal determination that every possible reference use is exempt.

## Required evidence before the gate can change

Retain the exact original source bytes, format, locator, retrieval time, checksum and license evidence through the proper Noe owner; document the intended use and its rights basis. Reconcile every identifier at every native level between the selected published formats, preserving indexing provisions and exact differences. Then bind external registration, local subject coverage and mapping reviews separately. Until those facts exist, missing source bytes are not an empty successful dataset and aggregate counts cannot discharge the gate.

## Mathematical comparison and open barriers

The paper's harmonic route controls mass by a bulk integral of `|Hess u|²/|grad u| + R_g |grad u|`, with linear-growth harmonic `u`, exterior Neumann data, and rigorous handling of critical points and boundary terms. The uploaded source instead builds an elaborate local-coordinate, minimal-surface and cylindrical-boundary argument. It must be checked that this route actually recovers the full paper inequalities, their domains, normalizations and rigidity; the named `stern_harmonic_cylinder_bound` is **not** already the exact paper Theorem 1.2. Schoen–Yau minimal hypersurface and Witten spinor/Lichnerowicz proofs are genuine related methods, not interchangeable evidence or independent verification of this source.

**First hostile statement check:** the submitted `AsymptoticallyFlat` structure stores `numEnds : ℕ` without a field `0 < numEnds`; the final theorem quantifies `∀ k : Fin hAF.numEnds`, which is vacuous for zero ends. Determine whether the chosen hypotheses exclude this case and prove nonvacuous paper correspondence where needed. Also verify that endwise scalar-curvature integrability and the custom Riemannian distance express the paper's `R_g ∈ L¹(M)` and metric completeness; verify chart independence and the exact `1/(16π)` sphere measure normalization.

No theorem in these sources relates ADM mass to the **pole-inclusive original arithmetic Weil form** for zeta and primitive Dirichlet characters. For any future RH/GRH use, a new exact source-return theorem would have to preserve all prime powers, character phases, conductor, gamma/parity, poles, multiplicities, test space and quantifiers. Geometric positivity does not supply such a theorem.

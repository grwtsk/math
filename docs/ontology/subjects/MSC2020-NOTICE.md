# MSC2020 classification data

The native codes, two-digit titles, and hierarchy in the `msc2020-*.yaml` subject records are adapted from the Mathematics Subject Classification 2020 by Mathematical Reviews and zbMATH. Copyright 2020 Mathematical Reviews and zbMATH. The classification data and its projection use [CC BY-NC-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/). Source descriptions and scope notes can be retrieved from the official publication. [MSC2020 publication](https://msc2020.org/)

The adaptation groups the original identifiers into YAML files by top-level class and retains native code spellings. The CSV and TeX identifiers were reconciled by exact set comparison. Their observed counts, source digests, and the differing landing-page counts are recorded separately. [Source record](../sources/msc2020.yaml) · [Coverage record](../corpora/msc2020.yaml)

The frontier plans and supplemental learning subject are original material by grwtsk contributors. Future acquisition from the sources named in these plans depends on the rights, permitted uses, and access conditions of each source unit.

# Bibliographic qualifications

The verified fields are under each source’s `bibliography` object in `catalog.json` (schema version 2). Every evidence record binds the local asset SHA-256 and identifies a PDF page, primary arXiv/Crossref record, or owner report. `verified-in-paper` means the local paper displays that bibliographic claim; it does not authenticate a preprint’s assertions, prove a theorem, or establish legal authorship. Original extracted metadata and identifiers remain verbatim under the legacy `bibliographic_claims` and `identifiers` arrays; those raw candidates are not the verified bibliography.

The owner identifies anonymous/software-user metadata labels with grwtsk contributors and directs that name for the project research drafts. These are owner-reported statements. Printed named scholarly bylines are retained separately; no other named paper is reassigned to the owner. The exact username alias scope and individual contribution allocation remain open under issue #254. “Most is unpublished research” is not applied as a blanket publication-status override.

## Date meanings and versions

A missing publication date stays unresolved. ArXiv first posting and queried-version update dates, draft dates, ebook release dates, original book dates, and journal online/print dates are separate events. No filesystem, PDF-creation or copyright date is silently promoted to publication. The publication-date field identifies its selected event in its notes. Dates attached to a DOI publication can concern a later published version; this does not replace the local preprint’s chronology.

All 516 byte identities and library paths remain separate. A matching arXiv title can establish a work family without authenticating the exact local version. Such version numbers are explicitly labeled filename candidates. A shared DOI or title never merges assets.
